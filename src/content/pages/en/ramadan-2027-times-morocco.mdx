Pick your city: the table gives **imsak** (start of the fast, set at Fajr time) and **iftar** (breaking the fast, Maghrib time) for each of the 30 days. The times are **calculated in your browser** with the Moroccan Ministry of Habous method: nothing is sent or tracked.

> **Provisional dates.** Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin on **Monday 8 February 2027** by astronomical calculation, but it depends on the crescent sighting and on the **official announcement of the Ministry of Habous and Islamic Affairs**, the evening before. A one-day shift is possible: use the first-day selector.

**Convention.** Imsak = Fajr (19° angle), with no safety margin; some calendars publish imsak about ten minutes earlier. **Legal time.** Morocco has been on GMT+0 all year since 20 September 2026: no return to GMT during Ramadan, so the times above are in the country's standard time.
