## What we collect

- **Audience statistics** with Umami, a privacy-friendly tool that does not use cookies and does not store personal data.
- **No account, no form:** we don't ask for your name or email.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where you consent. Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent message managed by Google (certified CMP) and can change their choice at any time. Learn more: [How Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [Ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the partner (e.g. Booking.com, Airalo) may set a cookie to attribute your booking to us. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

## Your rights

You can ask us any question about your data at the address above. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
