**Contact:** contact@sahlamaroc.com

This policy relies on Law No. 09-08 (dahir 1-09-15 of 18 February 2009) on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, published on [cndp.ma](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf) (French), and on the CNDP guidelines for websites. Data controller: the site publisher, identified in the [legal notice](/en/legal-notice).

## What we collect, why and on what basis

- **Contact form**: name, email address, subject, message and, if you enter it, the address of the page concerned. Purpose: to reply to you. Basis: your request and your consent when you send it. Required fields are marked; without them we cannot reply. Depending on configuration, the message is sent to a form service or prepared in your email app, and nothing is sent until you confirm.
- **Newsletter**: your email address, only if you subscribe (double opt-in confirmation). Purpose: to send the newsletter and the promised guide. Basis: your consent. It is neither sold nor handed over; every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **Audience statistics**: if Umami is enabled, it counts page views and some events (search, calculator, affiliate link click) without cookies or personal data as configured. If it is not enabled, nothing is measured.
- **Saved on your device**: your light/dark theme and checklist progress are kept in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. Erase them with your browser settings or the "Reset" button.
- **Calculators**: the values you enter stay in your browser and are not sent to us.
- No user accounts. Fonts are hosted on the site itself.

## Recipients

- **Brevo**: sending the newsletter (email address).
- **Google (AdSense)**: serving and measuring ads, when enabled.
- **Umami**: audience statistics, when enabled.
- **Vercel**: site hosting (technical connection data such as the IP address, processed to serve pages).
- The contact form service, if any, to deliver your message.

These providers may be established, or host data, outside Morocco. Law 09-08 (arts. 43 and 44) governs transfers abroad; according to the CNDP guidelines, a transfer request is filed together with the declaration or authorisation of the processing.

## Retention

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for (art. 3 of Law 09-08): the contact message while your request is handled, the newsletter address until you unsubscribe. No fixed period is announced here until the publisher has set one.

## Advertising (Google AdSense), cookies and consent

The CNDP guidelines call for consent before cookies that rely on personal data are set, and for stating their purpose and how to refuse them. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads if you consent. Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent message managed by Google and can change their choice at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Learn more: [How Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [Ads settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the partner (e.g. Booking.com, Airalo) may set a cookie to credit us for your booking. Its own privacy policy applies on its site.

## Your rights (Law 09-08)

With proof of identity, you can write to the address above to:

- **access** your data: confirmation of processing, purposes, categories, recipients, copy of the data (art. 7, free of charge);
- **correct, update, erase or block** non-compliant data; the controller must make the corrections free of charge within ten clear days (art. 8);
- **object**, on legitimate grounds, to processing, and free of charge to any marketing use (art. 9).

If refused, or with no reply within ten days, you may refer the matter to the CNDP (art. 8): [cndp.ma](https://www.cndp.ma).
