## What we collect

- **Contact form**: name, email address, subject, message and, if you enter it, the address of the page concerned. This is used only to reply to you. Depending on how the site is configured, the message is sent to a form service or prepared in your email app, and nothing is sent until you confirm.
- **Newsletter**: your email address, only if you subscribe. It is never shared or sold. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- **Audience statistics**: if a privacy-friendly analytics tool (Umami) is enabled, it counts page views and some events (search, calculator, affiliate link click) without cookies or personal data. If it is not enabled, nothing is measured.
- **Saved on your device**: your light/dark theme choice and your checklist progress are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and you can erase them with your browser settings or the “Reset” button.
- **Calculators**: the values you enter stay in your browser and are not sent to us.
- No user accounts. Fonts are hosted on the site itself.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

When enabled, Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads if you consent. Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent message managed by Google and can change their choice at any time. Learn more: [How Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [Ads settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the partner (e.g. Booking.com, Airalo) may set a cookie to credit us for your booking. Its own privacy policy applies on its site.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of the data you sent us by writing to the address above, and block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
