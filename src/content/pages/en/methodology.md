## Our sources

1. **Official bodies first**: customs, tax authority, land registry, ministries, foreign-exchange office, Bank Al-Maghrib, public transport operators. Each guide lists the bodies to check a rule with.
2. **Operators and providers** (ferry companies, trains, airports) for timetables and fares, shown as indicative.
3. **Recognised press** for current figures, named in the text when used.
4. **First-hand experience** of the author, who lives in Morocco. The “Checked on the ground” badge appears only when the check was really done.

We do not publish a figure whose source we do not know. When data cannot be found or sources disagree, we say so.

## Dates

Every guide shows its **publication date** and **update date**. Figures that change (exchange rates, annual statistics) are dated in the text.

## Calculators

- Each calculator shows its **method**, **assumptions**, the **check date** of its default values and its **sources**.
- Results are **estimates**: they replace neither a quote nor the advice of a notary, tax adviser or bank.
- Calculations run in your browser: the values you enter are sent to no one.
- The EUR/MAD rate in the calculators is an indicative rate, updated regularly; it is not a live exchange rate.

## Sensitive topics

For regulatory, tax or financial topics, each guide reminds you that rules can change and points to the competent authority. Our content is informational and is not legal, tax or financial advice.

## Translations

French and English are written and maintained by the author. The Spanish, German and Arabic versions are machine translations awaiting human review.
