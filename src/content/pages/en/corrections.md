## Report a mistake

Something outdated, inaccurate or unclear? Tell us: [report a mistake](/en/contact?type=correction). Please give the page concerned and, if you can, the source that contradicts our text.

Every guide also has a **“Report a mistake”** link that pre-fills the form with the page address.

## What happens next

1. We check with the official body or source concerned.
2. If the error is confirmed, we fix the guide and update its date.
3. Significant corrections (a figure, a rule, a procedure) are recorded below.
