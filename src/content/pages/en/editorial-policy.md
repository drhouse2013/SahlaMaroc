## Principles

- **Useful first.** A guide must let you act: prices, steps, deadlines, documents. No filler.
- **Accurate and honest.** We do not invent figures, rules, sources or customer reviews. If we do not know, we say so.
- **Dated.** Every guide shows its update date; figures that change are dated.
- **Careful with sensitive topics.** Tax, money, property and paperwork: we always point to the competent authority. Our content is not legal, tax or financial advice.

## Independence

- Affiliate links and advertising fund the site, but **no partner reviews or approves our content**, and we do not accept money in exchange for a favourable opinion.
- We only recommend services we would use ourselves.
- Paid links are flagged to search engines (`rel="sponsored"`) and explained in the [affiliate disclosure](/en/affiliate-disclosure).

## Updates

We revisit guides when we learn that a rule, price or timetable has changed, or when a reader tells us. The update date changes only when the content has really been reviewed.

## Corrections

Errors are fixed as soon as they are confirmed, and significant changes are listed on the [Corrections](/en/corrections) page.

## Translations and tools

The Spanish, German and Arabic versions are machine translations awaiting human review. Assistive tools may be used for drafting or translation; editorial responsibility remains with the author.
