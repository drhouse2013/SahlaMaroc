## How we calculate

Figures come from our own spending notes from living in Morocco (Casablanca area) and are adjusted by city. Housing for a monthly stay assumes a furnished studio or one-bedroom outside the tourist medinas; short trips use per-night prices from hostels to 4-star riads. Prices are reviewed every quarter.
