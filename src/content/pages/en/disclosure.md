Sahla Morocco is free to read. To pay for research and hosting, we earn money in two ways:

1. **Affiliate links.** When you book or buy through some links (for example Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Viator, Airalo, Wise, SafetyWing or DiscoverCars), we may receive a commission. **The price you pay is the same.** These links are marked as sponsored for search engines.
2. **Advertising** served by Google AdSense, clearly labelled "Advertisement".

Partners have no say in what we write, and we don't accept payment for positive reviews.
