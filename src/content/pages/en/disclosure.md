Sahla Morocco is free. To fund the research and the hosting, we earn money in two ways:

1. **Affiliate links.** When you book or buy through certain links (for example Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Viator, Airalo, Wise, SafetyWing or DiscoverCars), we may earn a commission. **The price for you is the same.** These links are flagged as sponsored to search engines (`rel="sponsored"`) and open in a new tab.
2. **Advertising** served by Google AdSense, clearly labelled “Advertisement”.

## Why we recommend a service

We only mention services we would use ourselves, in the context of a guide where they meet a specific need (staying, getting connected, sending money, insuring a trip). Partners have no say over our content and we do not accept money in exchange for a favourable opinion.

## Where do these links appear?

A short notice appears at the top of guides that contain affiliate links. A guide without the notice contains no paid link.
