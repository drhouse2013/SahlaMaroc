**Sahla Morocco — the practical guide to Morocco for Moroccans abroad and travellers.** *Sahla* means “easy” in Darija: our mission is to make Morocco simple to understand and to get around.

## Who writes it?

Sahla Morocco is written by **Yassine**, who lives near Casablanca. I speak Darija, French and English. I write the guides I wish I could have handed to friends arriving from abroad on day one.

## Who is it for?

- **Moroccans living abroad (MRE)**: the summer return, your car and the 180-day rule, sending money, property, paperwork.
- **Travellers**: arrival, transport, where to stay, day trips, budget.

## How we work

- **Sourced and dated.** Every guide shows its update date. Sensitive rules and prices point to the competent authority. Read our [methodology](/en/methodology) and [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy).
- **Estimates are labelled.** Calculators show their assumptions, sources and check date, and their results are always presented as estimates.
- **Corrections welcome.** Something outdated or wrong? See [corrections](/en/corrections) or [report it here](/en/contact?type=correction).

## How we make money

The site is free. Some links are affiliate links and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; ads may be displayed. Partners do not review our content. Details in the [affiliate disclosure](/en/affiliate-disclosure).

## Languages

French and English are the reference versions. The Spanish, German and Arabic versions are machine translations awaiting human review; they are flagged as non-indexable to search engines until they have been reviewed.
