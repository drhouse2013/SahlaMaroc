It is too early to lock in accommodation for the 2030 World Cup: FIFA has not published the match schedule, so nobody knows which city will host which game. This guide, **valid as of 9 October 2026**, helps you choose types of areas and lodging, anticipate demand and avoid pitfalls. It gives **no prices**, because no match-period rates exist today.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
Do not book for a specific match yet (schedule unpublished). Pick a base city well connected by train, favour free-cancellation offers and check that any short-term rental is legally authorised. Stadiums can be outside city centres, notably in Benslimane.
</Callout>

## Legend

**(confirmed)**: established fact. **(announced)**: stated by an authority, not finalised. **(provisional)**: press figure to be reconfirmed. **(hypothesis)**: not announced, our own reasoning.

## What is known about supply and demand

- Nearly 20 million tourists visited Morocco in 2025, per figures reported by the press in July 2026 (confirmed by those sources).
- Tourism Minister Fatim-Zahra Ammor told Reuters on 20 July 2026 that Morocco planned to add **60,000 hotel beds** by 2030, from a capacity of a little over 300,000 beds today (announced). Where the new beds will be is not specified. Rabat is described as likely to play a bigger role for events.
- The match allocation is undecided, so we **cannot** know which cities will be most in demand on a given date (hypothesis: Casablanca and its region for a possible final, but this is not official). See [the stadium status guide](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-stadiums-status).

## Types of accommodation

- **Hotels**: simplest for ID checks, cancellation and service. They will fill first near railway stations.
- **Riads and guesthouses** (Marrakech, Fes, Rabat, Tangier): charming, but limited capacity and sometimes awkward access with luggage in the medina.
- **Furnished apartments**: useful for groups, but subject to strict rules (see below).
- **Resorts and beach hotels** (Agadir, Tangier, Casablanca's Ain Diab): comfortable, but far from stadiums.

### Special case: the Benslimane stadium

The Grand Stade Hassan II is outside Casablanca (38 to 60 km depending on the source). If matches are played there, the logical option would be to sleep in Casablanca or a city linked by road or the announced RER, but **no official access plan is published**. See the [transport guide](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-transport).

### Staying in a nearby city linked by train

The idea of "sleep in Rabat and go to Casablanca" relies on the Al Boraq line and existing trains (see the [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq)). It is a reasonable way to avoid price spikes, to be validated once fixtures are known (hypothesis).

## Precautions

1. **Free cancellation**: favour it until the schedule is published.
2. **Short-term rentals**: in Morocco, renting to tourists is regulated (law 80-14, operating authorisation, guest declaration). Check the listing is authorised and read [our short-term rental rules](/en/short-term-rental-morocco-rules).
3. **Payments**: pay through the booking platform; be wary of requests for bank transfers or off-platform messaging, common around big events.
4. **Rates**: check at booking; they can move sharply.
5. **Owners abroad (MRE)**: if you plan to rent out your property, assess the legal and tax obligations first.

## Common mistakes

- Booking before knowing the match city.
- Paying a deposit outside the platform.
- Choosing lodging far from any station.
- Forgetting luggage in a medina with narrow lanes.

## Checklist

1. Base city chosen.
2. Free cancellation.
3. Authorised listing verified.
4. Payment through the platform.
5. Access from station or stadium checked.
