Nador is the main city of Morocco's north-east Rif and is closely tied to the diaspora: many families spend the summer here, and the port at Beni Enzar is a gateway into Morocco from Spain and France. The right place to stay depends less on the city than on how you arrive and for how long.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Practical stay (shops, offices, taxis):** the **centre of Nador**.
- **Arriving or leaving by ferry:** consider **Beni Enzar**, where the port is, about 13 km from Nador city centre according to [Baleària](https://www.balearia.com/fr/routes-horaires/bateau-nador-almeria). A stopover night there avoids a rush.
- **Waterside setting:** the **Marchica lagoon**, an area still being developed: check what is actually open before you book.
- **Summer (July-August):** book early; this is peak season for Moroccans living abroad.
</Callout>

## The areas to know

### Nador centre

The simplest choice for a few nights or for administrative errands: hotels, cafés, shops and services are close together, and short trips are easy by taxi. The Nador Ville railway station is also here. The centre is livelier and noisier in summer.

### Beni Enzar and the port

Beni Enzar is the municipality of the port and the border with Melilla. According to [Marsa Maroc](https://www.marsamaroc.co.ma/en/ports-and-terminals/nador-port), which runs the passenger terminal, the port of Nador has routes to Almería and Sète; Ferryhopper also mentions Motril and Barcelona, to be confirmed with the operators. Baleària advertises 4 departures a week to Almería. Staying nearby helps if your boat leaves early or arrives late, or if you would rather not drive tired after the crossing. For schedules, operators and durations, which change by season, see our [ferry to Morocco guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide).

### Marchica lagoon

The Nador lagoon (Marchica) is the large body of water beside the city. A development programme, **Marchica Med**, aims, according to [its official website](https://marchicamed.ma/en/about-us-en/), to make the lagoon a reference for eco-tourism and housing through public and private investment projects (hotels, marina, golf, housing programmes). The site gives no precise timeline, so the real progress of the projects needs confirming locally. Do not book on project visuals alone; check the exact location and access.

## Which type of accommodation?

| Situation | Best option |
|---|---|
| One or two nights around the ferry | Hotel in Beni Enzar or the centre |
| Family of four or more, 1 to 3 weeks | Furnished apartment (kitchen, several bedrooms) |
| Short visit without a car | Hotel in the centre |
| Waterside stay | Place near the lagoon or coast, after checking |

**Guesthouses** and **campsites** exist in the region, but the offer is uneven: compare recent reviews and photos before booking. For the rules on furnished rentals, read our [short-term rental guide](/en/short-term-rental-morocco-rules).

## Getting there

- **By air:** Nador-Al Aroui airport (NDR), run by ONDA, is listed in the [ONDA airport network](https://www.onda.ma/Je-suis-Passager/Guide-du-voyageur/R%C3%A9seau-a%C3%A9roportuaire); it is about 24 km south of the city according to [Wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aéroport_de_Nador-Al_Aroui) (ONDA does not state the distance). It is listed with seasonal routes to several European cities; confirm with the airlines. A taxi is the most direct way into town. See also [Morocco's airports and getting to the city centre](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre).
- **By ferry:** port of Nador (Beni Enzar), see above. Baleària advertises 4 departures a week to Almería (a crossing of 5 to 7 hours depending on the company's pages), but rotations change with the season: confirm with the operator.
- **By train:** an ONCF line links Nador to Taourirt, where it connects to the Oujda-Fes-Rabat-Casablanca line. Timetables change: check [oncf.ma](https://www.oncf.ma) before planning.
- **By car:** if you land at Tanger Med, read the [Tanger Med motorway route](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route) and plan a long leg.
- **Nador West Med:** [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/07/21/avant-louverture-fin-2026-nador-west-med-acheve-ses-derniers-preparatifs-1727043/) (21 July 2026) describes this new port as a port, industrial and logistics hub due to open in the fourth quarter of 2026. It is not presented as a passenger port, so do not plan your trip around it.

## Summer return: precautions

- **Book early** for July-August; supply fills fast when many families come home. See [Operation Marhaba](/en/operation-marhaba-guide) for the general calendar.
- **Seasonal prices:** rates vary strongly between summer and the rest of the year and must be checked at booking. Do not pay cash in advance without a receipt.
- **Check the listing:** exact address, recent photos, equipment (air conditioning, hot water), house rules.
- **Deposit and cancellation:** ask in writing for the deposit amount, refund conditions and cancellation policy.
- **General checklist:** [summer return checklist](/en/summer-return-checklist-mre).
