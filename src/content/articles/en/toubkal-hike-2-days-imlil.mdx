**Toubkal (4,167 m)** is North Africa's highest peak, about **1 h 30 from Marrakech**. The standard ascent takes **two days** from the village of **Imlil**, with a night in a mountain hut. It is accessible to fit walkers, but altitude and weather are not negotiable.

<Callout type="tip" title="The essentials">
- **Duration:** 2 days (night at the hut at 3,207 m).
- **Day 1:** Imlil → Toubkal refuge, about **4 to 5 h** of walking.
- **Day 2:** summit ascent (about **3 to 4 h**) then descent to Imlil.
- **Best season:** **April to October**, with September–October a good trade-off between weather and crowds.
- **Guided budget:** around **1,500 to 2,500 MAD** per person for 2 days (indicative).
- **Level:** moderate to demanding, with roughly 2,000 m of cumulative climb.
</Callout>

## Day-by-day route

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 1" title="Imlil → Toubkal refuge (3,207 m)">

Start from Imlil (around 1,740 m). The path follows the valley and climbs steadily to the **Toubkal refuge** (known as the Neltner refuge), run by the **French Alpine Club**, with dormitories. Big bags are often carried by **mule** to the refuge; beyond, you carry your day pack.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 2" title="Summit then back to Imlil">

Start early, often before dawn, to enjoy the coolness and the view. The climb takes about 3 to 4 h, on a stony path then scree. From the summit the view covers the whole High Atlas. The descent takes you back to the refuge then Imlil the same day.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## When to go

- **April to October:** normal walking conditions. **September–October** give a good balance of weather and crowds.
- **Winter:** snow and ice are common. The climb becomes a mountaineering outing requiring **winter gear** (crampons, ice axe) and real experience, with a competent guide.

## Guide, budget and gear

- **A local guide**: since 2019, the ascent is done with a local guide, with checkpoints on the trail (notably at Aroumd), according to several sources; confirm the rule locally or with your operator. A guide also knows conditions, weather and trails (essential in winter).
- **Guided 2-day packages** usually include the guide, the hut night and meals; check what's included (transport from Marrakech, mule, meals).
- **Bring:** good hiking boots, warm layers (cold at altitude even in summer), waterproof jacket, gloves, hat, headlamp, water, sunscreen and snacks.

## Altitude and safety

- Climb **at your own pace** and drink regularly.
- **Altitude sickness** is possible around 3,000–4,000 m: headache, nausea, unusual fatigue. Descending generally improves symptoms.
- **Weather:** check forecasts and turn back if conditions worsen.
- **Insurance** covering high-altitude hiking: check before you go.
