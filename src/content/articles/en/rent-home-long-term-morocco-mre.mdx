Renting before buying is a prudent choice on return: you test the city and neighbourhood without committing. This guide covers **long-term rental** of a home, not tourist rentals (see [short-term rentals](/en/short-term-rental-morocco-rules)). It flags what still needs confirming.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- Residential leases fall under **law 67-12** (19 November 2013, per secondary sources); rentals under 30 days do not.
- Insist on a **written contract**, a detailed **inventory of condition** and a **receipt** for each payment.
- The **security deposit** must be returned at departure, less sums owed; its amount is **not set by the text** we found (the practice cited is two months' rent).
- The rules for **lease registration** (deadline, duties) could not be confirmed: ask the tax authority or a notary.
</Callout>

## 1. Before you search

- Set your **budget** with the [cost of living](/en/cost-of-living-morocco).
- Prepare papers: CNIE or passport, proof of income and, if needed, a guarantor. Real requirements vary by landlord.
- If you are not there yet, avoid paying any advance without a **viewing**, yours or by a trusted person.

## 2. The lease

- Check the **landlord's identity** and title to the property.
- Have the following spelled out: duration, rent, charges, payment date and method, notice, renewal terms.
- A **guarantee deed** is separate from the lease; do not sign a guarantee without understanding its scope.

## 3. Deposit and inventory

The deposit covers the tenant's breaches, not normal wear. Photograph the home on entry and exit, sign a joint inventory of condition and keep a copy. One source reports that the written lease and inventory were planned from the bill stage: check the version in force.

## 4. Taxes on the landlord's side

Rents are property income declared by the landlord. If you own a property you rent out yourself, see [taxes on sale and rental](/en/property-tax-selling-renting-morocco-mre). A tenant normally does not declare them.

## 5. Housing and your return plan

- [Permanent return](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide): arrange housing before arriving with your family.
- [Children and school](/en/return-morocco-children-school-equivalence-cnss): the neighbourhood may depend on the school.
- [Daam Sakane](/en/daam-sakane-housing-aid-mre) concerns purchase, not rental.

## What was not confirmed

- The deadline and duties for registering a residential lease.
- Any legal cap on the deposit.
- The rules on rent revision.
