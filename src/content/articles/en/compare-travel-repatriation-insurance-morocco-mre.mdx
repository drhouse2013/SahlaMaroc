No prices are given here: rates vary with age, length and policy, and we hold no official source for them. This guide gives the **criteria** so you can compare two or three policies yourself.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- Check what you already have first: **premium bank card**, **health cover**, vehicle breakdown assistance. Cover sometimes overlaps, but activation conditions differ.
- The **European Health Insurance Card** covers the European Union: it does not apply in Morocco.
- Above all compare the **medical expenses cap**, **medical repatriation** cover and **exclusions**.
- Call the **assistance line before any major expense**: many policies require prior approval.
</Callout>

## Criteria to compare

| Criterion | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Area covered | Is Morocco expressly included? Some policies limit cover to a list of countries. |
| Length of stay | Maximum duration per trip, and whether a long stay (whole summer, retirement) is covered. |
| Medical costs | Cap, deductible, reimbursement or direct payment to the hospital, private clinics accepted. |
| Medical repatriation | Medical transfer to your country of residence, decided by the assistance provider's doctor, any cap. |
| Pre-existing conditions | Often excluded or limited: read the clause, not just the benefits table. |
| Age | Age limit at purchase and surcharges or exclusions after a certain age. |
| Activities | Sports, high-altitude hiking, quad biking: often excluded or optional. |
| Assistance | Line reachable 24/7, language, number from Morocco, response times. |
| Luggage, cancellation | Separate covers: useful depending on the trip, rarely health-related. |
| Family | Children covered, eligibility conditions, cap per person or per policy. |

## Medical repatriation versus repatriation of remains

These are **two separate covers**. Medical repatriation concerns a sick or injured person. Repatriation of remains concerns a death and has its own guide: [repatriation insurance for MRE](/en/body-repatriation-insurance-morocco-mre). Check both, especially if your family wants burial in Morocco.

## Pitfalls to avoid

- A high advertised cap but a **deductible** or exclusion list that empties the cover.
- Cover that requires you to **advance costs** with no advance limit.
- A policy bought **after** the health problem began.
- Not keeping invoices, medical reports and payment receipts.

## Before you leave

1. List your existing cover (card, health plan, car assistance).
2. Ask for each policy's **terms and conditions** and read the exclusions.
3. Save the assistance number in your phone and on paper.
4. For health on site, see our guide to [health and emergencies in Morocco](/en/health-morocco-travellers-insurance-emergencies).
