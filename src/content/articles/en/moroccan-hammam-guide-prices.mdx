The **hammam** is a pillar of Moroccan life: a place for hygiene, relaxation and meeting people. Whether you visit a neighbourhood public bath or a riad spa, here is the ritual, indicative prices and good manners.

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- **Public hammam:** entry about **10 MAD**; with a scrub by a *tayeb(a)*: **60 to 100 MAD**.
- **Hammam + scrub (combo):** about **150 MAD**, **200 MAD** with henna.
- **Spa / riad hammam:** **100 to 500 MAD** (up to about 800 MAD with full treatments).
- **Bring** (public hammam): a towel, sandals, black soap, *kessa* glove.
</Callout>

## The ritual step by step

1. **Hot room:** you settle in so the steam opens the pores.
2. **Rinse** with hot water.
3. **Black soap** applied all over the body, left on for about ten minutes.
4. **Scrub with the kessa glove,** which removes dead skin.
5. **Full rinse.**
6. **Ghassoul (rhassoul) mask,** then **argan oil** to moisturise (depending on the package).

Prices vary by city, venue and season.

## Public or spa?

- **Public hammam:** authentic, inexpensive, used by locals; hours are separate (women / men) or there are separate rooms. Bring your own kit.
- **Riad or hotel spa:** more comfortable, towels and products provided, often with massage; ideal for a first time.

## Good manners

- **Modesty:** keep underwear or a swimsuit on, especially at first.
- **Quiet and calm:** speak softly, share water with respect.
- **Tip** the person who scrubs and massages: see [tipping](/en/tipping-in-morocco), around 20–50 MAD for a scrub.
- **Hygiene:** shower before entering a spa, hydrate afterwards.
- **Health:** avoid the hammam if you have a fever, high blood pressure or after a big meal.

## Where to try

The hammam is everywhere: [Marrakech](/en/where-to-stay-in-marrakech), [Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes), [Essaouira](/en/where-to-stay-in-essaouira). Many riads offer their own spa: see [riad or hotel](/en/riad-or-hotel-morocco-choose).
