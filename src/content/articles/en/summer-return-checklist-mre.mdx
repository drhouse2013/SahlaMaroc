Departure stress almost always has one cause: starting too late. This countdown tells you **what to do and when**, from three months before you leave to departure day. Tick items as you go: your progress is saved on your device.

<Callout type="tip" title="The 5 rules that avoid 90 % of problems">
- **Book the ferry in April–May** for July–August travel.
- **Green card** showing **MA** not crossed out, otherwise border insurance.
- **Valid passports** for everyone, children included.
- **Reasonable gifts**: no batches of identical new appliances.
- **Time margin**: arrive at the port 3 hours early in peak season.
</Callout>

## The countdown

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="3 months out" title="Book and prepare the car">

- **Book the ferry**: the best fares go in April–May. Compare options in the [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide) (fixed-date tickets in August).
- **Get the car serviced**: tyres (spare included), brakes, fluids, air conditioning, battery. A garage check costs less than a breakdown on a Spanish motorway.
- **Check your dates**: temporary admission of your car is limited to 180 days per year. See the [180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule).
- **Note the expiry dates** of every passport and ID card in the family.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="1 month out" title="Insurance, budget, paperwork">

- **Car insurance**: ask your insurer for a green card valid in Morocco (**MA** not crossed out) or plan on **border insurance**. Everything is explained in [Car insurance for Morocco](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border).
- **Budget**: estimate ferry, fuel, tolls, stopovers and spending with the [summer budget calculator](/en/summer-in-morocco-budget).
- **Money**: plan your transfers and compare their real cost with the [transfer calculator](/en/money-transfer-cost-morocco).
- **Children and pets**: start early, some steps take weeks. See [Travelling to Morocco with children and pets](/en/travel-morocco-children-pets).
- **Route**: plan your way from the port with the [Tanger Med motorway and tolls guide](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="1 week out" title="Documents, cash, stopovers">

- **Print or save offline**: ferry tickets, insurance certificate, hotel bookings for stopovers, and national transport tickets (CTM bus, ONCF train) if you continue without the car. Keep digital **and** paper copies.
- **Withdraw a little cash** for the road and tolls; dirhams are obtained in Morocco. See [cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
- **Book your stopovers** if the drive is long (a night in Spain, a night in Tangier).
- **Download** offline maps and sort out an eSIM or SIM for arrival: [SIM and eSIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).
- **Stock up on medicines** with prescriptions: [customs rules](/en/morocco-customs-what-to-bring).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Day before" title="Loading and last check">

- Load the car **sensibly**: wedge luggage, keep the rear shelf clear.
- Keep within reach: passports, registration, driving licence, insurance, tickets, water, snacks.
- Check cold tyre pressure and fill up.
- Tell relatives in Morocco your arrival time **in Moroccan time**: Morocco is on GMT all year, 1 hour behind France or Spain in winter and 2 hours behind in summer ([time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt)).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Departure day" title="Road, port, arrival">

- Take a break every 2 hours, especially at night in Spain.
- **Arrive at the port 3 hours early** in peak season.
- On arrival, declare the car under temporary admission and keep the document until it leaves Morocco.
- Follow the advice of [Operation Marhaba](/en/operation-marhaba-guide) if you need help.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Your tick-box checklist

<Checklist id="summer-return" lang="en" title="Summer return: my departure">

- Ferry booked (tickets saved offline)
- Car serviced, spare wheel checked
- Green card with **MA** not crossed out, or border insurance planned
- Valid passports for everyone (children included)
- Registration certificate, driving licence, insurance certificate
- Family record book and permissions for minors if needed
- Prescriptions and a reasonable supply of medicines
- Cash for the road, bank card tested abroad
- Stopovers booked if the drive is long
- SIM or eSIM and offline maps
- Gift budget respected, receipts kept
- Family told your arrival time (Moroccan time)

</Checklist>

## Paperwork: what must be valid

- **Passport**: valid for the whole stay for every family member, children included. Moroccan nationals can also show a valid **CNIE**, but carry a passport in case of checks or flight changes.
- **Car**: original registration certificate (or the owner's authorisation), valid driving licence, insurance valid in Morocco.
- **Tickets**: ferry tickets and, if relevant, CTM bus or train tickets from the [ONCF app](https://oncf-voyages.ma/) for the rest of the journey. Keep them digitally **and** on paper.

## Holiday gifts: stay within customs rules

Gifts for relatives are the main cause of unpleasant surprises at customs. The principle:

- **Personal effects and non-commercial gifts** enter duty-free up to limits set by the administration (**around 25,000 MAD per year** for MREs with an activity abroad, according to the circulars reported for Marhaba 2026), **without concentrating the value on a single type of item**.
- Anything that looks like **trade** is taxed: several TVs or flat screens, new appliances in numbers, many identical phones.
- When in doubt, **declare**: it is always cheaper than a seizure or a fine.

Limits change, so check them on the Moroccan customs (ADII) website before you leave. Details in our guide [Morocco customs: what you can bring](/en/morocco-customs-what-to-bring).
