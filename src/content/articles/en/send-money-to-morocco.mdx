Moroccans abroad sent **89.2 billion dirhams** home in the first eight months of 2026 alone (+9 % year on year, Office des Changes), after more than 122 billion in the whole of 2025. On amounts like that, a few percent in fees means a lot of money for families. Here's how to pay less.

<Callout type="tip" title="The golden rule">
Always compare **the final amount received in dirhams**, not the displayed fee. The biggest cost is often hidden in the **exchange rate**.
</Callout>

## Your options

- **Your European bank:** convenient, but international transfers are often expensive (fixed fee + poor rate).
- **Moroccan banks with branches in Europe:** dedicated MRE offers, sometimes good value for regular transfers.
- **Cash transfer agencies:** fast and available all over Morocco, but check the rate applied.
- **Online apps:** often the cheapest thanks to a rate close to the real one. <AffiliateLink provider="wise">Wise</AffiliateLink>, for example, shows its fee and rate before you send.

## 5 habits

1. Note **today's rate** (Google, XE) before comparing.
2. Look at the **amount received** by your family, fees included.
3. Group transfers: one monthly transfer costs less than four small ones.
4. Avoid converting at your bank **and** on arrival: convert once.
5. For a property purchase, keep the transfer receipts: you'll need them if you ever repatriate the funds. See [buying property from abroad](/en/buying-property-morocco-from-abroad).
