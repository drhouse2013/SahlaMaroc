If your nationality isn't visa-exempt, in some cases you can apply for an **e-Visa** online instead of going through a consulate. Beware: this is also a topic where **intermediary sites** charge a lot for a procedure you can do directly. Here's how to do it properly.

<Callout type="warning" title="One official address only">
The e-Visa application is made **exclusively** on the kingdom's official portal: **acces-maroc.ma**. Many "assistance" sites rank at the top of searches and add **large service fees** for the same procedure. Always check the address before you pay.
</Callout>

## Who is concerned?

Citizens of the **EU, UK, US, Canada, Australia** and many other countries can enter **visa-free for 90 days**: they don't need an e-Visa. See the [visa and 90-day guide](/en/morocco-visa-90-day-rule).

The e-Visa is aimed at specific profiles, which the portal groups roughly into three cases:

1. **Certain nationalities** allowed to apply for the e-Visa with just a valid ordinary passport.
2. **Foreign residents** of certain countries (EU, US, Canada, UK, Australia, Japan, Switzerland, etc.) with a residence permit valid for a minimum period.
3. **Holders of a multiple-entry visa** from certain countries (Schengen, US, Canada, UK…), with minimum remaining validity.

The exact list of countries and conditions is published on the portal and **changes**: check it on the day you apply.

## Length and validity

- An e-Visa generally allows a **maximum stay of 30 days**, with a **validity of 180 days** from issue, for a **single entry**.
- If you plan to stay longer you'll need another permit; an e-Visa **cannot be converted** into a residence permit. See [residence permit in Morocco](/en/morocco-residence-permit-guide).

## Step-by-step

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 1" title="Check your eligibility">

On the official portal, check your nationality, residence status and the conditions of any visa you hold.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 2" title="Prepare documents">

A copy of your **passport** (sufficient validity), a **passport photo**, and any documents required in your case (residence permit, visa).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 3" title="Fill in and pay online">

Complete the form, upload documents and pay the **official fee** by bank card. Amounts are shown on the portal: check them before confirming.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 4" title="Receive the decision">

The answer arrives **by email**, generally within a few working days (an expedited option exists). Print the visa and keep it with your passport.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Before you travel

- Check **passport validity**, return ticket and accommodation. See [what to know about customs](/en/morocco-customs-what-to-bring).
- Keep a **digital copy** of the visa and confirmation.
- If refused, ask the portal or consulate for details before reapplying.

<Callout type="info" title="Fees and timing: always check">
Amounts and timing differ between sources (official fees, expedited options, intermediary services) and can change. The only reliable reference is the official portal at the time of your application.
</Callout>
