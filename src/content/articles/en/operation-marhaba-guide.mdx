Every summer, millions of Moroccans living abroad travel home. In 2026, Operation Marhaba welcomed **more than 4.1 million MREs** over the summer, 1.8 % more than in 2025 (Mohammed V Foundation for Solidarity). Here's how to prepare a stress-free return.

<Callout type="tip" title="The essentials">
- Marhaba 2026 ran **from 5 June to 15 September**; it usually spans early June to mid-September, peaking in late July and early August.
- **Clocks:** Morocco is on **GMT** all year since 20 September 2026 (no more Ramadan switch). Spain and France are 1 h ahead in winter and 2 h ahead in summer: remember this when planning arrival times and port check-in.
- Book the ferry **in April–May** and avoid peak weekends.
- Check your documents (ID or passport, car registration, insurance valid in Morocco) **before** you leave.
</Callout>

## What is Operation Marhaba?

Run by the **Mohammed V Foundation for Solidarity** with Moroccan authorities and transit countries, Marhaba sets up **reception areas** on the road and in ports in Europe and Morocco: social and medical assistance, information, rest areas and help for travellers in difficulty.

## On the road

- Take a break every 2 hours, especially when driving through Spain at night.
- Keep water, snacks and documents within reach for checks.
- At the port, arrive **at least 2–3 hours** before departure in high season.

## Driving back to Europe

Returns peak at the end of August. If you can, shift your departure by a few days to avoid queues at Tanger Med.
