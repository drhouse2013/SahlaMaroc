Every summer, millions of Moroccans living abroad travel home. For the 2026 edition, the official portal [maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/marhaba-2026-plus-de-41-millions-de-mre-accueillis) reports **more than 4.1 million MRE welcomed** (4,137,594 according to the Mohammed V Foundation for Solidarity, about 1.8% more than in 2025), with peaks of around 80,000 arrivals a day. Here is how to prepare yours without stress.

<Callout type="tip" title="The essentials">
- Marhaba 2026 ran **from 10 June to 15 September**; it generally covers early June to mid-September, peaking in late July and early August.
- **Time:** Morocco has been on **GMT all year** since 20 September 2026 (decree 2.26.530, Official Bulletin no. 7521). Spain and France are 1 hour ahead in winter and 2 hours in summer.
- Our advice: book the ferry **as soon as sales open** and avoid peak weekends.
- Check your papers (ID card or passport, registration certificate, insurance valid in Morocco) **before** leaving.
</Callout>

## What is Operation Marhaba?

Run by the **Mohammed V Foundation for Solidarity** with the Moroccan authorities and transit countries, Marhaba sets up **reception areas** in ports and along the routes, in Europe and Morocco (26 in 2026): social and medical assistance, information, guidance and help for travellers in difficulty. In 2026 more than 66,000 people received direct support from the Foundation, nearly 14,000 of them for administrative, customs or legal matters. Keep that in mind if you hit a paperwork problem at the border.

## At customs: what to know

The ADII (customs) publishes a circular dedicated to Marhaba each year. For 2026, several media report easing measures, including a duty-free cap raised to MAD 40,000 for non-commercial items on a permanent return; we have not read the circular itself. If in doubt, consult the [customs portal](https://www.douane.gov.ma) or the [ADII MRE guide](https://www.casainvest.ma/sites/default/files/2025-07/GUIDE_MRE_DOUANE_fran%C3%A7ais.pdf) (French). If you are coming home for good, read the [permanent return guide](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide) first.

## On the road and at the port

- Take a break every 2 hours, especially at night.
- Keep water, snacks and papers within reach for checks.
- At ports, arrive **at least 2 to 3 hours** before departure in peak season (a prudent rule: check the check-in deadline on your ticket).
- Tell family your real arrival time: Moroccan time differs from European time.

## Common mistakes

1. Booking at the last minute for late August, when returns peak.
2. Discovering at the port that "MA" is crossed out on the green card.
3. Forgetting the exit authorisation or papers for a child travelling without both parents.
4. Exceeding the car's 180-day limit within the calendar year.
5. Leaving without cash or a payment method for tolls and fuel on arrival.

## Frequently asked questions

**When does Marhaba 2027 start?** Dates are not published. See [Marhaba 2027](/en/marhaba-2027-dates-ports-booking).

**Do I need to register for Marhaba?** No: it is a reception and assistance scheme, not a registration.

**What if I have a problem at the port?** Go to the Mohammed V Foundation reception areas in the ports.

## Going back to Europe

Returns peak in late August. If you can, shift your departure by a few days to avoid queues at Tanger Med.

## Your timeline, step by step

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="3 months before" title="Fix your dates and book the crossing">

Pick dates that avoid the late-July and late-August peaks, then book the ferry as soon as sales open ([ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide)). Book the **return** at the same time: it is the tightest moment of the season.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="2 months before" title="Check everyone's documents">

Check the validity of each passenger's passport or CNIE, the children's papers and, if needed, exit authorisations. See [travelling with children and pets](/en/travel-morocco-children-pets).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="1 month before" title="Sort out the car and insurance">

Service the vehicle, bring the original registration document, licence and a **green card with MA not crossed out** (otherwise border insurance, see [car insurance](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border)). Re-read the [180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="1 week before" title="Prepare the budget and the route">

Estimate the total (crossing, fuel, tolls, stopovers) with the [summer budget calculator](/en/summer-in-morocco-budget), prepare dirhams in small notes and plan the route from the port ([Tanger Med motorway](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route)).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Travel day" title="Port, border, first stop">

Arrive early, keep documents within reach, go through police then customs. On arrival, do not drive tired: make a short first leg or sleep nearby.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Travelling with children or older relatives

Bring water, snacks, usual medicines (with prescription) and a plan B for delays: waiting at the port can exceed several hours on peak dates. Marhaba reception areas offer social and medical assistance; for health on the ground, read [health and emergencies](/en/health-morocco-travellers-insurance-emergencies) and the [travel insurance comparison](/en/compare-travel-repatriation-insurance-morocco-mre). The exact number and location of reception areas change each year: confirm them on the Foundation's website.

## What this operation does not do

Marhaba is neither a discount nor a priority lane: it does not buy you a ferry place and does not replace your documents. Never pay an "intermediary" who promises to get you through faster: only the carriers' official counters and State services are authorised. For any doubt about a customs circular, the source is ADII.

- Route from the port and rest stops planned
- Family told the arrival time (Morocco time: GMT)
