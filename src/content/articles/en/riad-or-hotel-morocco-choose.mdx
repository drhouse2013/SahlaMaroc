In Morocco, accommodation is part of the trip. A **riad** (a traditional house around a courtyard) doesn't offer the same experience as a **hotel** or a **resort**. Here's how to choose, and the questions to ask before booking.

<Callout type="tip" title="The right choice at a glance">
- **Discovering a medina (Marrakech, Fes, Essaouira, Chefchaouen):** a **riad**.
- **Family with children, pool, simplicity:** a **hotel or resort**.
- **More than 4 nights, remote work, a kitchen:** an **apartment**.
- **Small budget:** a **guest house** or **hostel**.
</Callout>

## The options compared

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Riad / guest house** | Charm, rooftop breakfast, personal welcome, quiet inside | Access by lanes (no car), no lift, variable Wi-Fi |
| **Hotel** | Services, 24 h reception, parking, pool | Less authentic, sometimes far from sights |
| **Resort / all-inclusive** | Everything on site, ideal with kids | Tourist bubble, little immersion |
| **Furnished apartment** | More space, kitchen, long-stay prices | No services, rules to check |
| **Hostel** | Low price, meeting people | Dorms, limited privacy |

## The 6 questions to ask before booking

1. **How do I get there from the station, car park or taxi?** In medinas, cars stop at a gate: ask to be met.
2. **Is the Wi-Fi reliable for video calls?** Ask for a speed test.
3. **Are breakfast and air conditioning / heating included?** In winter, some riads are cold.
4. **What is the cancellation policy?**
5. **What is the tourist tax?** It is sometimes paid on site.
6. **Noise:** courtyard or street-facing room? Near a mosque or café?
