An expired passport or CNIE can ruin a holiday departure. For MREs, procedures go through the **consulate** you belong to, with an appointment system that fills up fast. Here's how not to be caught out.

<Callout type="tip" title="The 4 reflexes">
- **Plan 3 to 4 months ahead** of any trip: delays stretch sharply in summer.
- **Book an appointment** on the dedicated consular portal (rdv.consulat.ma).
- **Compliant photos** in biometric format, otherwise the file is rejected.
- **Check consular registration:** it must be up to date before applying.
</Callout>

## The biometric passport

### Appointment

Booking goes through the **consular portal** (rdv.consulat.ma): create an account, choose your consulate and the "Passport" procedure. Slots are released in waves (often Monday mornings) and go very fast. Plan to log in right at opening.

### Documents to provide (typical list)

- **Two recent passport photos** (under 6 months), **35 × 45 mm**, plain light background, no smile or accessories.
- **Biometric passport application form**, completed, dated and signed.
- **Up-to-date consular registration.**
- **Tax stamp** (the amount, around 500 MAD according to sources, is paid according to the consulate's procedure).
- Old passport, birth certificate and ID documents depending on your case.

On the day, the consulate **checks the file**, takes your **digital fingerprints** and **photo**: you must attend in person.

### Timing and validity

- **3 to 4 weeks** in normal periods after a complete file; **6 to 10 weeks or more** from June to August.
- Passports are now **biometric** (electronic chip) and valid for **5 years**, for adults and minors alike.

## The CNIE (electronic national ID card)

- It is **mandatory from age 16** and valid for **10 years** for adults.
- At the consulate: appointment on the same portal, a birth certificate extract **under 6 months old**, the old card (for renewal), **two compliant photos** and proof of address. Allow **four to eight weeks**.
- The national police's digital services (e-police, cnie.ma) are expanding: check what can be done remotely in your situation.

## For children

Each child needs their **own passport**. For travel with only one parent, see [travelling with children and pets](/en/travel-morocco-children-pets).

## Checklist

<Checklist id="passport-cnie" lang="en" title="Passport and CNIE: my checklist">

- Expiry dates of everyone's passport and CNIE noted
- Consular registration up to date
- Account created on the consulate's appointment portal
- Appointment booked (at least 3 to 4 months before travel)
- Compliant photos (35 × 45 mm, light background, under 6 months)
- Form completed, dated and signed
- Tax stamp / payment planned according to the consulate
- Recent birth certificate (CNIE) and old document (renewal)

</Checklist>

<Callout type="info" title="Confirm with your consulate">
Documents, fees and procedures vary between consulates and change over time. Always check your consulate's website before travelling.
</Callout>
