An expired passport or CNIE can ruin a holiday departure. For MRE, procedures are done at the **consulate** you depend on, with an appointment system that fills quickly. This article gathers what consular pages and practical guides say, flags where sources diverge (stamps, number of photos, age of documents) and proposes a realistic timeline so you are not caught out.

<Callout type="tip" title="The 4 reflexes">
- **Plan 3 to 4 months ahead** of any trip: slots and times tighten sharply in summer.
- **Book an appointment** on the dedicated consular portal (rdv.consulat.ma).
- **Compliant photos** in biometric format, or the file is rejected.
- **Check your consular registration**: it must be up to date before applying.
</Callout>

## The biometric passport

### Appointment

Booking goes through the **consular portal** (rdv.consulat.ma): create an account, choose your consulate and the "Passport" procedure. Slots are released in waves and go very quickly. Log in as soon as they open and do not leave your file to the last moment: a slot missed in June can mean waiting until September.

### Documents to provide (typical list)

Consular pages for an adult abroad cite:

- a **valid Moroccan CNIE** (original and double-sided copy), or a copy of the CNIE application **receipt**;
- an up-to-date **consular registration**;
- **two ID photos**, recent, **35 x 45 mm**, plain light background, face uncovered, no smile or accessory; vending-booth photos are often refused according to practical guides;
- the **application form**, filled in and signed;
- the **old passport** if renewing (it is cancelled and returned to you perforated);
- the **tax stamp**: about 500 MAD for an adult according to third-party guides, with a reduced rate for minors; the exact amount and payment method are to be confirmed with the consulate.

On the day of the appointment, the consulate **checks the file** and takes your **fingerprints** and **photo**: you must attend in person. For a child, a parent must be present; proof of parentage and parental authority depends on the situation, so ask in advance.

### Times and validity

- Allow **3 to 4 weeks** in normal periods for a complete file, **6 to 10 weeks or more** from June to August (estimate from user feedback, not official).
- Consular pages give a passport valid for **5 years** for an adult, not extendable, and **3 years** for a child under 3. The July 2026 decree on the new passport generation (published in the Bulletin officiel of 16 July) does not change the validity according to the press: confirm with your consulate.
- **Collection:** an uncollected passport is eventually cancelled. The retention time differs by source (2 months according to the passeport.ma FAQ, 6 months according to some guides): collect it as soon as you are notified.

## The CNIE (electronic national ID card)

- It is **compulsory from age 16** under law 04-20 as described by the press, and valid **10 years** for an adult according to consular pages.
- At the consulate: appointment on the same portal, a **recent** birth certificate extract or full copy (under 6 months for some consulates, under 3 months for others: the gap is flagged by guides), old card to return, compliant photos and consular registration certificate. The form is pre-filled on the CNIE portal. Stamps cited range from 75 to 105 MAD depending on the source: confirm them.
- A receipt is given at filing and is needed to collect the card.
- DGSN digital services are expanding: find out what can be done remotely in your situation.

## MRE-specific cases

- **Loss or theft abroad:** report it first to local authorities, then contact the consulate: renewal follows the procedure of your place of residence. Keep digital copies of your documents.
- **Child born abroad:** their civil status must be declared and transcribed. See [Moroccan birth certificate](/en/moroccan-birth-certificate-full-copy-extract-watiqa-mre).
- **Apostilled or translated documents:** if the consulate asks for a foreign document, see [apostille and legalisation](/en/apostille-legalisation-documents-morocco).
- **Imminent departure without a valid passport:** the airline may refuse boarding; check the validity required before booking. See [cheap flights Europe-Morocco](/en/cheap-flights-europe-to-morocco-guide).
- **Military service:** Moroccans abroad who wish to register voluntarily for the census must give their consular registration number (Decree 2-19-46, art. 12). See [military service and MRE](/en/moroccan-military-service-mre-census-exemption).

## For children

Every child must have their **own passport**. For travel with a single parent, see [travelling with children and pets](/en/travel-morocco-children-pets). Before summer, the schedule in the [summer return checklist](/en/summer-return-checklist-mre) puts the passport first.

## Checklist

<Checklist id="passport-cnie" lang="en" title="Passport and CNIE: my checklist">

- Expiry dates of the passport and CNIE of the whole family noted
- Consular registration up to date
- Account created on the consulate appointment portal
- Appointment booked (at least 3 to 4 months before departure)
- Compliant photos (35 x 45 mm, light background, recent)
- Form filled in, dated and signed
- Tax stamp / payment planned according to the consulate
- Recent birth certificate (CNIE) and old document (renewal)
- Receipt kept until collection

</Checklist>
