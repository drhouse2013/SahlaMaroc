Buying in Morocco while living in Europe is common, but it is also where the biggest losses occur: a poorly checked property, a badly drafted promise, a deposit paid to an intermediary, a rejected power of attorney. Here is the method, **in order**, with the official figures we could read.

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- Prefer a **titled** property (land title), have a **notary** check the file and demand a recent **ownership certificate**.
- Official costs: **4%** registration (housing), **1.5%** land registry, notary fees: budget **6 to 8%** on top of the price. [Calculate them here](/en/morocco-property-buying-costs-calculator).
- **Pay by traceable means**: a cash payment can cost 2% extra duty.
- New home and first purchase: the [Daam Sakane](/en/daam-sakane-housing-aid-mre) aid (70,000 to 100,000 MAD) may apply.
</Callout>

## The 7-step journey

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 1" title="Set the overall budget">

Price + acquisition costs + works and furniture + exchange margin. If you borrow, simulate with the [mortgage calculator](/en/morocco-mortgage-calculator) and read the [MRE mortgage guide](/en/mortgage-morocco-mre-guide): banks may finance up to 80% of the price, per the Office des changes.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 2" title="Check the property's legal status">

- **Titled property**: ask for a recent **ownership certificate**. ANCFCC offers this online, with an authenticity check of issued documents and land-publicity consultation. It shows the owner, mortgages, encumbrances and reservations.
- **Melkia (unregistered)**: ownership rests on adoulary deeds. Registration duty on a title-establishing deed is 3% per the tax guide. Risk is higher: get a professional to help.
- **Joint ownership or several heirs**: require the presence or written agreement of all.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 3" title="Check the seller or developer">

Verify the seller's identity (national ID), that they are the title holder, and that no mortgage or seizure encumbers the property. For a **developer**: legal existence, delivered projects, the land title, the **building permit**, the actual progress of works, and the off-plan sale protections (law 44-00). Visit, or have a trusted person visit with dated photos and video.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 4" title="Sign the promise of sale at the notary">

The promise sets the price, conditions precedent (obtaining the loan, clearing mortgages) and the deposit. **Never hand over cash**, or pay an intermediary without a receipt. Since law 41.25, published per the press in the Official Bulletin of 16 July 2026, promises of sale and powers of attorney tied to a transfer of real property rights require an **authentic deed**, on pain of nullity: do not rely on a simple private paper.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 5" title="Finance and transfer the funds">

Use a traceable banking channel and keep the receipts: they matter for a later resale and repatriation (see [tax on selling and renting](/en/property-tax-selling-renting-morocco-mre)). State the payment method in the deed.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 6" title="Sign the deed: in person or by proxy">

If you cannot attend, give a **special power of attorney**, precise and time-limited. The required form has changed several times: read the [power of attorney guide](/en/power-of-attorney-morocco-property-mre) and ask your notary what he accepts.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 7" title="Registration and land-registry entry">

The deed must be **registered within 30 days** of being drawn up. The notary pays the duties and files the registration request at the land registry, which issues a new ownership certificate in your name. Ask for it and keep it.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Official costs, line by line

| Item | Rate or amount | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Registration duty, housing | 4% of the price | 2026 MRE tax guide |
| Registration duty, bare land | 5% of the price | 2026 MRE tax guide |
| Title-establishing deed (melkia) | 3% | 2026 MRE tax guide |
| Land registry | 1.5% + fixed fee 100 MAD, minimum charge 500 MAD | ANCFCC tariff (decree 2.16.375) |
| Notary fees | Variable, plus 20% VAT; exact scale not confirmed here | Ask for a written quote |
| Surcharge for non-traceable payment (> 300,000 MAD) | 2% | 2026 MRE tax guide |

Notary fees are not detailed here: a draft scale was reported in the press, but publication of the final text was not confirmed. Ask for a written quote.

## Documents to prepare

- Passport or national ID, and proof of residence.
- For the seller: land title or ownership certificate, national ID, and the local-tax proofs the notary will ask for.
- The promise of sale, the reservation contract (new build) and the plans.
- Proof of origin and transfer of funds.

## Classic pitfalls

- **Buying off-plan without checking the developer**, the permit and the guarantees.
- **Under-declaring the price** "to pay less in fees": illegal, and penalising on resale (your capital gain, computed on too low a purchase price, rises).
- **Paying a cash deposit** to a broker or "friend" with no receipt or written agreement.
- **Buying without a visit**: photos and video calls do not replace a real visit.
- **Too broad a power of attorney**, or one accepted in the wrong form.
- **Forgetting running costs**: housing tax and municipal services tax (75% reduction of the rental value for an MRE's main home, per the tax guide) and building service charges.

<Checklist id="acheter-bien-maroc" lang="en" title="Property purchase: my checklist">

- Total budget (price, 6 to 8% costs, works, exchange) calculated
- Recent ownership certificate obtained and read
- Seller or developer verified (title, permit, guarantees)
- Promise of sale drafted at the notary with conditions precedent
- Traceable price payment method stated in the deed
- Power of attorney form validated by the notary
- Deed registered within 30 days, new ownership certificate received

</Checklist>

## After the purchase

Think about future tax: the capital gain on resale is taxed at 20% of the net profit with a minimum of 3% of the price, with a possible exemption for a main home occupied for at least 5 years (a home kept by an MRE or occupied free of charge by close family counts, as long as it is not rented). Simulate with the [capital gains calculator](/en/property-capital-gains-tax-morocco-calculator). To rent out, see [tax on selling and renting](/en/property-tax-selling-renting-morocco-mre).
