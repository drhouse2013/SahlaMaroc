Morocco has the **first high-speed line in Africa**. Along with comfortable intercity trains run by the national operator **ONCF**, it makes the train the best way to travel between big cities.

<Callout type="tip" title="Typical journey times">
- **Tangier → Rabat:** about 1 h 20 (Al Boraq)
- **Tangier → Casablanca:** about 2 h 10 (Al Boraq)
- **Casablanca → Marrakech:** about 2 h 40
- **Casablanca → Fes:** about 3 h 50
- **Marrakech → Fes:** about 6 h 30
</Callout>

## Al Boraq, the high-speed train

Opened in November 2018, **Al Boraq** reaches **320 km/h** between Tangier and Kenitra, then continues on upgraded tracks to Rabat and Casablanca. Trains are modern, punctual and air-conditioned, with first- and second-class cars. An extension towards Marrakech is under construction.

## Regular intercity trains

Air-conditioned intercity trains link Casablanca, Rabat, Fes, Meknes, Marrakech, Oujda and other cities. **First class** gives you an assigned seat, which is worth it on busy weekends and around holidays.

## Casablanca airport train

A shuttle train links **Mohammed V airport** to Casa-Voyageurs and Casa-Port stations in about 45 minutes, with departures roughly every hour during the day. It's the cheapest way into the city.

## Booking tips

- Buy on the **ONCF website or app** to get an e-ticket with a QR code.
- Book early: fares are lower in advance, and Al Boraq trains sell out at weekends.
- Arrive 15 minutes early: there's a security check before the platforms.
- Bring snacks and water for long journeys.

## Where trains don't go

For Essaouira, Chefchaouen, Agadir or the desert, take **Supratours** (ONCF's partner bus line) or **CTM** buses, or a tour. See our [Essaouira guide](/en/essaouira-day-trip-from-marrakech) and [Sahara tour guide](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour).
