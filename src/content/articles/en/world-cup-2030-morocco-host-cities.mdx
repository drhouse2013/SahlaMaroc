Morocco will co-host the **2030 FIFA World Cup** with Spain and Portugal. Six Moroccan cities are planned to host matches. Here is what is known, what is still moving, and how to prepare.

<Callout type="warning" title="Status as of 9 October 2026">
These six cities are the ones Morocco proposed in its bid. FIFA has not yet published the final list of stadiums, the match allocation or the venue of the final: see our guide to [stadiums and official status](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-stadiums-status) for what is confirmed and what is only announced.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="The six proposed host cities">
**Casablanca · Rabat · Marrakech · Tangier · Fes · Agadir**
</Callout>

## Stadiums and capacities

Capacities are approximate and can change as renovation works progress.

| City | Stadium | Approx. capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Casablanca (Benslimane) | Grand Stade Hassan II (new build) | 115,000 seats (announced) |
| Rabat | Prince Moulay Abdellah Complex | 68,700 seats (confirmed, opened 4 September 2025) |
| Tangier | Tangier Grand Stadium | about 75,600 (announced) |
| Agadir | Adrar Stadium | about 46,000 targeted (provisional) |
| Marrakech | Grand Stade de Marrakech | 45,860 targeted by 2028 (provisional) |
| Fes | Fes Stadium | 35,600 today, 55,800 targeted (provisional) |

The new Casablanca stadium is designed to be one of the largest football stadiums in the world. It is located outside the city, so plan your transport to the matches.

## Getting around

- **Trains:** the **Al Boraq** high-speed line links Tangier, Rabat and Casablanca. An extension towards Marrakech is under construction. See our [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
- **Taxis and apps:** Uber is back in Casablanca, Marrakech, Rabat, Agadir and Tangier. See [taxis and ride apps](/en/taxi-apps-morocco).
- **Airports:** Casablanca (CMN) and Marrakech (RAK) are the busiest. Read about [Casablanca airport to the city](/en/casablanca-airport-to-city-centre) and the [Marrakech airport guide](/en/marrakech-airport-to-medina).

## Where to stay

Match days will push prices up. Book early and consider a nearby city linked by train. Our guides: [where to stay in Marrakech](/en/where-to-stay-in-marrakech) and [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes).

## What to do now

1. Follow the official FIFA schedule for match dates and ticketing. Beware of unofficial resellers.
2. Keep your passport valid beyond your stay. See the [visa guide](/en/morocco-visa-90-day-rule).
3. Remember Morocco is on **GMT all year**, so kick-off times in Morocco differ from Spain and Portugal. See [Morocco's time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt).

## The six cities at a glance

Each city has its own page on the site: [Casablanca](/en/cities/casablanca), [Rabat](/en/cities/rabat), [Marrakech](/en/cities/marrakech), [Tangier](/en/cities/tangier), [Fes](/en/cities/fes) and [Agadir](/en/cities/agadir). A few pointers to pick your base without waiting for the final list:

- **Casablanca**: the country's main air and rail hub. The announced stadium is at Benslimane, outside the city, so plan to sleep in Casablanca and check the access plan later. See [where to stay in Casablanca](/en/where-to-stay-in-casablanca).
- **Rabat**: the capital, with a stadium already opened. Linked to Casablanca and Tangier by Al Boraq, it is a logical base to see several cities. See [where to stay in Rabat](/en/where-to-stay-in-rabat).
- **Tangier**: the gateway from Europe (ferry, high-speed train). See [where to stay in Tangier](/en/where-to-stay-in-tangier).
- **Marrakech**: the classic tourist city, but its high-speed link is only announced for late 2029. See [where to stay in Marrakech](/en/where-to-stay-in-marrakech).
- **Fes**: the smallest of the six stadiums for now, with a listed medina. See [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes).
- **Agadir**: a southern seaside resort, away from the high-speed network. See [where to stay in Agadir](/en/where-to-stay-in-agadir).

## Official status: "proposed" until FIFA decides

As of 9 October 2026, FIFA has confirmed that Morocco, Spain and Portugal host the 2030 edition (decision of 11 December 2024, [FIFA article](https://inside.fifa.com/news/world-cup-2030-morocco-spain-portugal-argentina-paraguay-uruguay-reactions)), but that article names no Moroccan city. The list of six cities comes from the Moroccan bid as relayed by the press. We therefore call them "proposed" and will update this status as soon as FIFA publishes an official list. No match date, ticket price or room rate is given here: none exists yet.

## Common mistakes

- Treating the six-city list as a final FIFA decision.
- Booking a flight or hotel for a "match in Casablanca": the stadium is at Benslimane and no match has been assigned.
- Buying a ticket or "package" outside FIFA's official channels.
- Trusting a stadium capacity with no date or source: figures vary between articles.

## Checklist

1. Official FIFA city list checked.
2. Base city chosen with the train in mind.
3. Stadium status verified.
4. Bookings cancellable.
5. Tickets only through FIFA.
