A missed check at the border can ruin a whole holiday. With children or a pet, **paperwork needs weeks of lead time**. Here's what to gather, in order.

<Callout type="warning" title="Do these first">
- **Pet:** the **rabies antibody titre test** required to return to the European Union needs a blood sample and a waiting period of **several weeks**. Start **at least 4 months** before your return date.
- **Child travelling without one parent:** the authorisation must be ready before you leave.
</Callout>

## Travelling with children

### Basic documents

- **Individual valid passport** for every child, whatever their age. Check the expiry date.
- **Family record book** (or birth certificate extract): useful to prove the link with the parents.
- For children with Moroccan nationality, also check their Moroccan documents.

### When an authorisation is needed

- **A minor living in France and travelling without one of their parents** needs an **exit authorisation (AST)**. The form (Cerfa no. 15646) can be downloaded from service-public.fr; it must be **signed by a parent with parental authority** and accompanied by a **copy of their ID**.
- A child travelling **with their father or mother** does not need an AST, but keep the family record book.
- **On the Moroccan side**, officers may ask for a **parental authorisation**: better to carry one **signed, and ideally legalised** (town hall or notary) if the child is not with both parents, especially after a separation or when travelling with a grandparent.

### For the road and the ferry

- Plan suitable car seats or boosters, regular breaks and activities.
- Keep the children's documents in the **same waterproof wallet** as yours.
- For stops and budget, use the [summer return checklist](/en/summer-return-checklist-mre).

## Travelling with a dog or cat

Rules depend on the country you leave from, the species and the animal's age. Contact **your vet** (ideally an approved one) as soon as possible. The big picture:

### Entering Morocco

- **Permanent identification:** a readable microchip (or older tattoo).
- **Valid rabies vaccination** recorded in the animal's **European pet passport**.
- **Health certificate in the ONSSA model**, endorsed by an **official vet of the country of origin within the 24 hours before loading or departure**. A certificate dated several days earlier is rejected at the border. Always download the **current model from the ONSSA website**.
- Animals of certain **breeds or categories** may face restrictions: check before booking.

### Returning to the European Union

Morocco is classified as a **third country with a rabies risk**. To bring your pet back into the EU you generally need:

1. a **rabies antibody titre test**, sampled **at least 30 days after the vaccination**;
2. then a **3-month waiting period** after the sample before entering the EU (confirm with your vet);
3. a **health certificate** for EU entry, issued less than 10 days before;
4. entry through an approved travellers' entry point for animals.

### Leaving Morocco

To leave Morocco with your pet you need an **export health certificate (ONSSA model)** signed by an official vet. The window is a little wider, often **24 to 48 hours before boarding** (up to 3 days according to some sources): confirm it with the vet.

<Callout type="tip" title="High-value tip">
Book the vet appointment for **the exact day before departure**, and plan around **weekends and public holidays**: official veterinary services (for example the DDPP in France) are closed and a missed certificate can cancel the trip.
</Callout>

That's why you **don't decide at the last minute** to take the dog on holiday to Morocco.

### Ferry and accommodation

Each ferry company sets its own rules (pet-friendly cabins, kennels, muzzles). Book **early**: the number of authorised cabins is limited. Also check that your accommodation in Morocco accepts pets. See the [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide).

## Family checklist

<Checklist id="kids-pets" lang="en" title="Family and pets: my paperwork">

- Valid passport for each child
- Family record book
- AST (Cerfa 15646) signed + copy of the parent's ID, if the child travels without one parent
- Legalised parental authorisation if needed on the Moroccan side
- Pet: microchip read and noted in the passport
- Pet: valid rabies vaccine
- Pet: rabies titre sampled (30 days after vaccine) and 3-month wait respected
- Pet: health certificate for each trip
- Ferry cabin that accepts pets booked
- Contact details of a vet on site

</Checklist>

<Callout type="info" title="Up-to-date sources">
For the EU: your national agriculture ministry and local veterinary services. For Morocco: ONSSA (the national food safety office). Rules are updated regularly: confirm with them before you travel.
</Callout>
