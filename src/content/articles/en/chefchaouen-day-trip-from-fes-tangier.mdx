Chefchaouen, the blue town of the Rif mountains, is one of Morocco's most photographed places. It's easy to reach from **Tangier** but a long day from **Fes**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **From Tangier:** about **2 h 15 to 2 h 30** by bus, around **60–85 MAD** one way. A day trip works.
- **From Fes:** about **4 to 4 h 30** by bus, around **75–140 MAD** one way. Better to stay **one night**.
- **Buses:** CTM and Supratours both serve the route. Book ahead on weekends and holidays.
</Callout>

Prices and schedules change, so check them on the operator's website or app.

## From Tangier

Several buses run each day. If you're arriving by sea or flight, it's a natural first stop. See the [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide) for the crossing. A day trip gives you time for a walk through the medina and lunch, but you'll be back late.

## From Fes

CTM buses leave in the morning, and the last return bus from Chefchaouen leaves early evening. That makes a day trip very tight: about eight hours on the road for a few hours in town. Staying one night is far more rewarding. Compare areas in our [Fes guide](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes).

## What to see

- **The blue medina:** wander without a plan. Early morning and evening are quietest.
- **Plaza Uta el-Hammam** and the kasbah, for a coffee break.
- **Ras el-Maa:** the spring and waterfall at the edge of town, where locals wash and relax.
- **A short hike** to the Spanish Mosque viewpoint for sunset.

## Tips

1. Wear comfortable shoes. The town is hilly.
2. Ask before photographing people.
3. Bring cash. Cards are rare in small shops.
4. Weather turns cooler in the mountains: bring a layer in winter.
