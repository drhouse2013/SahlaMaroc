Chefchaouen, the blue city of the Rif mountains, is one of Morocco's most photographed places. The Moroccan National Tourist Office describes it as a town built on a mountainside, with a medina of narrow lanes, a kasbah, waterfalls nearby and hiking trails. It is easy to reach from **Tangier** or **Tetouan**, much less so from **Fes**. This guide helps you choose between a day and a night.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **From Tetouan:** about 65 km by road (about 1 h 26) per the tourist office.
- **From Tangier:** about 112 km (about 2 h 11).
- **From Fes:** about 194 km (about 3 h 24) by car; a same-day return is very tight. Better to stay **one night**.
- **Bus:** timetables and fares change; confirm on the carrier's website.
</Callout>

## Choosing by starting point

| From | Day trip possible? | Advice |
|---|---|---|
| **Tetouan** | Yes | The closest, ideal for half a day or a day |
| **Tangier** | Yes, with a late return | Good rhythm if you arrive by port or airport |
| **Fes** | Very tight | A night on site is more rewarding |

For buses, see [CTM and Supratours](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco) and confirm timetables with the carriers. For the crossing from Spain, see the [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide). To rent a car, see [car rental](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide).

## A typical two-day stay

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 1" title="Arrival, medina and sunset">

Arrive and drop your bags. Get lost in the **medina** without a map: early morning and evening are the calmest. Take a coffee break at Outa el-Hammam square, by the kasbah. Late in the day, climb to a viewpoint for sunset.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 2" title="Ras el-Maa or a hike, then return">

Go down to **Ras el-Maa**, the spring and waterfall at the entrance to town, where locals gather. If you walk, head for the **Akchour waterfalls** or a hike in Talassemtane National Park, both cited by the tourist office. Check access, difficulty and season before setting off.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## From Tangier

Several services run daily; confirm times and frequency. If you arrive by sea or air, it is a natural first stop. A day leaves time for a walk and lunch, but the return is late. See the [Tangier city page](/en/cities/tangier).

## From Fes

A same-day round trip means several hours of road each way. A night is far more pleasant. To prepare your departure, see [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes) and the [Fes city page](/en/cities/fes). The [Chefchaouen city page](/en/cities/chefchaouen) and [where to stay in Chefchaouen](/en/where-to-stay-in-chefchaouen) list places and accommodation.

## What to see

- **The old medina:** narrow lanes and blue facades.
- **The kasbah and its gardens**, in the centre of the medina, and the **Grand Mosque**.
- **The ethnographic museum:** old weapons, photos and textiles.
- **Ras el-Maa:** the spring and waterfall, at the town entrance.
- **Akchour and Talassemtane:** waterfalls and nature, farther out.

## Best time and level

Spring and autumn are generally the most pleasant in a mountain town; summer draws more people, winter can be cool and damp. The town is **hilly**, with many steps: bring good shoes. Hikes nearby (Akchour, Talassemtane) demand more fitness.

## What to pack

- Comfortable, non-slip shoes, the town is on a slope
- A warm layer in the evening and in winter
- **Cash**: cards are rare in small shops
- Water, hat and sunscreen for hikes
- A power bank for photos

## Common mistakes

1. **Trying Fes - Chefchaouen in a day** and spending most of it on the road.
2. **Photographing residents without asking.**
3. **Arriving without cash.**
4. **Underestimating the climb** of the lanes with a suitcase.
5. **Setting off on a hike without checking** the weather and access.
6. **Buying under pressure**: take time to compare.

## How to choose a tour operator

We recommend no names. Ask: departure and return time, real time on site, what is included, cancellation, driver or guide licence. For hiking, ask for a qualified local guide.
