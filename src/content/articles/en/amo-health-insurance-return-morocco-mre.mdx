After years of cover abroad, returning to Morocco quickly raises the health question: who covers you, from what date, with which papers? Basic compulsory health insurance (**AMO**) is run by **CNSS** for private-sector employees and non-salaried workers, under the regulation of **ANAM**. The main trap for an MRE is the **gap in cover** between the end of foreign entitlements and the effective opening of Moroccan rights. This guide complements the [permanent return guide](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide) and the [health and insurance page](/en/health-morocco-travellers-insurance-emergencies).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Employee in Morocco**: affiliation goes through your employer; it is not optional.
- **Self-employed or auto-entrepreneur**: you must register yourself (the "Travailleur non salarié" profile on cnss.ma); contribution amounts published on third-party sites **differ**, confirm them with CNSS.
- **No activity**: AMO Tadamon for needy people (RSU), voluntary AMO Achamil for those who can contribute.
- **Retiree**: a pension of at least 500 MAD a month according to CLEISS; see [retiring in Morocco](/en/retire-in-morocco-pension-tax-guide).
- **In every case**: plan interim cover, because rights open after a qualifying period.
</Callout>

## 1. The main situations

| Your situation | Likely cover | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| You are hired | AMO through the employer (CNSS) | Give your papers to the employer; 54 working days of contributions |
| You work for yourself | AMO "non-salaried worker" (TNS) | Register with CNSS; 3-month qualifying period; see [starting a business](/en/start-business-auto-entrepreneur-morocco-mre) |
| You receive a pension | Depends on the fund and agreement; a pension of at least 500 MAD a month opens AMO rights (CLEISS) | Ask your fund for the right form |
| You have no income | AMO Tadamon (needy) or AMO Achamil (voluntary) | Register in the RSU, then apply |

## 2. What CNSS and CLEISS let us confirm

The cnss.ma site presents sections by profile (employee, domestic worker, non-salaried worker, AMO Tadamon, AMO Achamil, pensioner), tutorials to activate a MaCNSS account and a customer relations centre on **3939**. The figures come from the CLEISS sheet on the Moroccan scheme (2024) and should be confirmed at source:

- **Qualifying period**: 54 working days of contributions in the 6 months before care for an employee or voluntary insured; 3 months from registration for a non-salaried worker.
- **Children**: dependants up to 21, 26 for higher education, no limit for disability.
- **Spouse**: a dependant only if they have no other scheme.
- **Continued rights**: up to 6 months after activity ends.
- **Needy people**: moved from RAMED to AMO Tadamon on 1 December 2022.

## 3. Unified Social Registry and AMO Tadamon

The press reports that in March 2024 the Health Minister announced about 7.7 million effective beneficiaries of the support scheme after integrating 10.4 million former RAMED beneficiaries. These figures date from 2024. For a returning MRE, RSU registration assumes a household living in Morocco; the eligibility score is calculated by the administration, with no promised outcome.

## 4. Recommended order of steps

1. **Before leaving**: ask your foreign insurer how long it maintains rights after departure, and cancel nothing before you have written confirmation of your Moroccan rights.
2. **Gather** national ID, legalised civil-status records ([apostille](/en/apostille-legalisation-documents-morocco)), proof of status (employment contract, self-employed registration, pension notice).
3. **Register**: through your employer if employed, through CNSS (branch, MaCNSS portal or 3939) if self-employed.
4. **Declare your dependants** (spouse, children): see [children and CNSS](/en/return-morocco-children-school-equivalence-cnss). Law 55.19 led CNSS to accept a sworn declaration (inactivity, dependency, non-marriage) in place of certain certificates, according to May 2021 press; confirm at a branch.
5. **Keep interim insurance** until your first card and first reimbursement.

## 5. Common mistakes

- **Cancelling too early** the insurance of the country you left: a claim during the qualifying period is not covered.
- **Thinking a national ID is enough** to open rights: you need affiliation and contributions.
- **Paying on the strength of a third-party site**: some sites say 144 MAD a month, others 224 to 1,200 MAD; the gap shows that no figure is reliable without CNSS.
- **Forgetting dependants**: children and spouse must be declared.
- **Confusing AMO with top-up insurance**: AMO is a basic basket with a patient share.

## 6. What we could not confirm

- The exact **contribution amounts** for TNS and Achamil in 2026 and the access conditions for a returning MRE to AMO Achamil.
- The **list of documents** by status.
- Coverage of **care received before rights open**.
- The **RSU thresholds**.

We show no verification date on this guide: the cnss.ma pages read do not detail these points.

## Checklist

<Checklist id="amo-retour" lang="en" title="AMO on return: my checklist">

- Length of continued foreign rights requested in writing
- Interim insurance (private or international) in place
- Status defined: employee, self-employed, retiree, no activity
- Registration done (employer or CNSS) and MaCNSS account activated
- Spouse and children declared as dependants
- End date of the qualifying period noted
- First AMO card or certificate received

</Checklist>
