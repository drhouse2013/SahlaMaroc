After years of cover abroad, returning to Morocco quickly raises the health question: who covers you, from when, and with which papers? In Morocco, compulsory basic health insurance (**AMO**) is run by the **CNSS** for employees and the self-employed, under the oversight of the **ANAM**. This guide complements the [permanent return guide](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide) and the [health and insurance page](/en/health-morocco-travellers-insurance-emergencies).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Employee in Morocco**: affiliation goes through your employer and the CNSS; it is not optional.
- **Self-employed or auto-entrepreneur**: you must cover yourself; contribution amounts published by third-party sites **disagree**, so confirm with the CNSS.
- **No activity or insufficient income**: the support scheme (the former RAMED, now AMO Tadamon) runs through the **Unified Social Registry (RSU)** with an eligibility score.
- **Retiree**: see [retiring in Morocco](/en/retire-in-morocco-pension-tax-guide); CLEISS cites a pension threshold of 500 MAD per month, to be confirmed.
</Callout>

## 1. The main situations

| Your situation | Likely cover | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| You are hired | AMO through the employer (CNSS) | Give your papers to the employer |
| You work for yourself | AMO for non-employees, or a contribution tied to auto-entrepreneur status | Register with the CNSS; see [starting a business](/en/start-business-auto-entrepreneur-morocco-mre) |
| You receive a pension | Depends on the fund and the convention | Ask your fund for the relevant form |
| You have no income | Support scheme subject to the RSU | RSU registration, then AMO Tadamon application |

## 2. Unified Social Registry and AMO Tadamon

Press reports say that in March 2024 the health minister announced about **7.7 million effective beneficiaries** of the support scheme, after 10.4 million former RAMED beneficiaries were fed into the database and 1.2 million people above the index threshold were excluded. These figures date from March 2024 and do not describe the situation today.

## 3. What we could not confirm

- The **waiting period** or first opening of rights after a return: not found in an official text.
- The **exact contribution amount** for the self-employed in 2026: third-party sites state 144 MAD a month, others a scale of 224 to 1,200 MAD a month. Do not pay on that basis.
- The **list of documents**: varies with your status; the CNSS or ANAM sets it out.
- Cover for **care received before rights open**: clarify before any planned treatment. Keep private or international insurance until your rights are confirmed in writing.

## Suggested order of steps

1. Cancel or suspend your insurance abroad **after** your new rights are confirmed.
2. Get your civil-status documents legalised ([apostille](/en/apostille-legalisation-documents-morocco)).
3. Contact the CNSS (branch or portal) with your CNIE number and your status.
4. Add your dependants (spouse, children: see [children and CNSS](/en/return-morocco-children-school-equivalence-cnss)).
