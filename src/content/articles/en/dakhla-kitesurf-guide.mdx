Between desert and ocean, the **Dakhla lagoon** is one of the best kitesurfing waters in the world: steady wind, flat turquoise water, and room to start out as well as to progress. Here's what to know to plan your stay.

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- **Ideal season:** **April to October**, with the strongest wind from **June to August** (up to about 35 knots).
- **Wind:** the lagoon has **over 300 windy days a year**.
- **Water:** a wetsuit (shorty or 3/2) is still recommended by specialist guides.
- **Access:** flights to **Dakhla**, often connecting in Casablanca.
- **Beginner course (8–12 h of lessons):** around **€400–550** at the schools checked in October 2026.
</Callout>

## The lagoon spots

| Spot | Who it's for | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **La Dune Blanche** and **la Piscine** | Beginners, flat | Flat, shallow water, steady wind |
| **PK25** | All levels | Works in every wind direction |
| **Speed spot** | Experienced | Pure speed, sustained wind |
| **La Sarga (ocean side)** | Experienced, waves | Swell and more committing conditions |

Conditions change with tide, season and wind: follow the school's advice.

## When to go by level

- **Beginner:** spring and early autumn, when the wind is gentler.
- **Experienced:** high summer for the most powerful winds.
- **Cooler air and water:** plan a wetsuit in winter; the wind season is weaker from November to March.

## How to get there

Simplest: a **flight to Dakhla airport**, most often with a **connection in Casablanca**. Depending on the season, direct Paris – Dakhla flights run on certain days. Check airline schedules before booking. Morocco is on GMT all year: [time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt).

## Lessons, gear and budget

- **Beginner course (8–12 h of lessons over 2–5 days):** around €400–550 at the schools checked in October 2026.
- **Private lesson:** from about €200 for 2 h at one lagoon school (October 2026).
- **All-inclusive stays** (kite camp accommodation, meals, lessons): compare packages and check insurance.

Choose a **school with a safety boat**, recent gear and qualified instructors. Dakhla is also a great base for the **white desert**, dunes and lagoon excursions.

## Before you go

- Plan **cash** in dirhams: see [cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
- Get a **local SIM**: [SIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).
- For other board sports: [surfing in Taghazout](/en/taghazout-agadir-surf-guide) and the city of [Essaouira](/en/essaouira-day-trip-from-marrakech), also famous for wind.
