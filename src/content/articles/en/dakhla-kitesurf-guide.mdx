Between desert and ocean, the **Dakhla lagoon** is one of Morocco's most famous gliding destinations: steady wind, water sheltered in places, dunes all around. The Moroccan National Tourist Office cites spots such as **PK25**, **Foum El Bouir** and **Pointe du Dragon**, used by kitesurfers, surfers and windsurfers. This guide explains how to choose your season, spot and school, and what to prepare for a stay so far from the main routes.

<Callout type="tip" title="In brief">
- **Season:** spring to autumn according to schools, with the strongest wind in summer; confirm with your school.
- **Access:** flight to Dakhla airport; the road from Agadir exceeds 1,000 km.
- **Beginners:** favour a school with a safety boat and certified instructors.
- **Prices, flights, wind:** variable, not fixed here.
</Callout>

## The lagoon spots

The tourist office cites several places of practice: **PK25** (swimming and gliding in the lagoon), **Foum El Bouir** and **Pointe du Dragon**, used by kitesurfers, surfers and windsurfers. Schools have their own access points. A spot's level, the tide and the wind change the practice: follow your school's instructions and never go out alone when the wind picks up.

| Criterion | What to ask the school |
|---|---|
| Level | Spot suited to beginner or advanced |
| Tide | Hours when the water is most favourable |
| Safety | Boat, radio, drift protocol |
| Equipment | Age, size and condition of kites and boards |

## When to go according to your level

- **Beginner:** periods of gentler wind, according to the school, allow calmer learning.
- **Advanced:** the windiest months suit those seeking sustained conditions.
- **Water:** temperature varies; a wetsuit is often advised, ask the school.

The tourist office describes pleasant sun all year in Dakhla. The wind season depends on schools and years: do not fix it without checking.

## How to get there

The simplest is a **flight to Dakhla airport**, often with a connection in Casablanca; check airlines for any direct links. The tourist office gives over 1,100 km from Agadir-Taghazout, which makes the road a journey in itself. Morocco is on GMT all year: [time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt). For airports, see [Morocco's airports](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre).

## Lessons, gear and organisation

- **Beginner course:** several sessions over a few days, progressing from kite handling to riding.
- **Private lessons:** useful to progress faster, usually pricier.
- **All-inclusive stays:** accommodation, meals and lessons bundled: compare packages and what is included.

Choose a **school with a safety boat**, recent equipment and certified instructors. Do not pay in full in advance without a written contract. Dakhla is also a good base for dunes and lagoon excursions. For the city page, see [Dakhla](/en/cities/dakhla).

## What to pack

- Wetsuit suited to the season, rash vest, helmet and impact vest depending on the school
- Strong sun protection, glasses with a strap, hat
- Cash in dirhams: see [money and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco)
- Local SIM: [SIM card](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco)
- Travel insurance documents covering kitesurfing

## Common mistakes

1. **Booking at the last minute** in wind season.
2. **Overestimating your level** and heading out alone in the lagoon.
3. **Choosing without a safety boat** or certified instructors.
4. **Neglecting the sun**, intense even when the wind cools.
5. **Not checking insurance** for the sport.
6. **Comparing prices without comparing inclusions** (equipment, transfers, meals).

## How to choose a school without promoting a name

We recommend no school. Ask: instructor qualifications, students per instructor, equipment (brands, year), safety boat, policy when there is too little wind, and a written quote. Read recent reviews.
