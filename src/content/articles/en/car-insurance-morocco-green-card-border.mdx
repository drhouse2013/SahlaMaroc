Driving a foreign-plated car in Morocco without valid insurance is illegal and very risky. The good news: it takes two minutes to sort out **if you know how to read your green card**.

<Callout type="tip" title="The golden rule">
Check the list of countries on your **green card**: if the code **MA** (Morocco) is **present and not crossed out**, you are covered. If it is **crossed out** or missing, you must buy **border insurance** on arrival.
</Callout>

## Your decision in 3 questions

1. **Is MA not crossed out on my green card?** Yes: nothing to buy, keep the card within reach.
2. **MA crossed out, but my insurer can activate it?** Ask for a green card valid in Morocco **before you leave** (often an amendment or an option). This is usually the best value for a long stay.
3. **Not possible or too expensive?** Buy **border insurance** when you enter Morocco.

## The European green card and the "MA" code

The green card proves that your car insurance covers you abroad. It lists **country codes**, and Morocco is **MA**. When a code is crossed out, cover does not apply in that country. Also check the **validity period**: it must cover your whole stay, including the return.

## Border insurance in Morocco

It is for owners of foreign-registered motor vehicles entering Morocco with an **invalid green card**. It is sold on the spot by approved providers such as **CAT (Compagnie d'Assurances de Transport)**, present at border posts, ports included, every day and **24 hours a day**.

- It fills the gap in your international cover.
- The period you choose can usually be **extended** if your stay gets longer.
- Keep the **certificate** in the car throughout your stay.

### Available durations

Border contracts are not sold day by day: they follow fixed **steps**.

| Step | Who it suits |
|---|---|
| 5 days | Short stay, quick round trip |
| 10 days | One-week to ten-day trip |
| 1 month | Typical summer holiday |
| 3 months | Longer stays |
| 6 months | Extended stays (note the 180-day limit for the car) |

There is no 8-day or 15-day option: pick **the next step up** from your planned stay, with a margin for the unexpected.

### How much does it cost?

**There is no single official price set by the state**: Morocco regulates the model of the certificate, not its price. Figures seen online are only market averages (for example around **800 to 1,200 MAD for 30 days**, depending on the vehicle). The price depends on duration, engine power and provider.

**The practical solution:** **CAT**, which handles border insurance at the posts (Tanger Med, Bab Sebta, Tangier city, Nador, etc.), lets you **run a quote and subscribe online** on its official website before you board the ferry. You then know the **exact price** before arriving and avoid surprises at the counter. Make sure you are on the official [CAT Assurance](https://www.cat.co.ma/en/assurance-aux-frontieres) site.

## Don't pay twice

- **Before you leave**, call your insurer: a valid **MA** green card avoids any purchase at the border.
- Don't buy border insurance out of habit if your card is already valid: show it at the counter.
- If you do buy border insurance, choose the **right** length: the longest step is not useful for three weeks.
- Keep every certificate until the car leaves Morocco.

## Reminders

- Insurance does not replace the **temporary admission declaration** for your car: see the [180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule).
- Do not lend the car to a Moroccan resident: see the same guide.
- Prepare your trip with the [summer return checklist](/en/summer-return-checklist-mre).
