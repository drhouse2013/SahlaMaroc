Tetouan and the M'diq coast complement each other: the first for culture and history, the second for the sea. For a summer stay or a transit from Tanger Med, the real question is "city or beach?".

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Culture, architecture, short visit:** the Tetouan **medina** (UNESCO-listed) or the **Ensanche**, the Spanish-era town to its west.
- **Family beach stay:** **Martil**, **Cabo Negro** or **M'diq**, on the coast.
- **Arriving via Tanger Med:** Tetouan is about 46 km away according to [Wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tétouan).
- **Summer:** the coast is very busy, book ahead.
</Callout>

## Tetouan: medina and Ensanche

Tetouan's medina is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Sleeping in the medina suits history lovers, but as in any old town access is on foot; ask your host for directions. The **Spanish Ensanche**, west of the medina, is a protectorate-style district with buildings of about five storeys and shops at street level: a practical choice for restaurants and taxis.

## The coast: Martil, Cabo Negro, M'diq

- **Martil** lies north-east of Tetouan on the Mediterranean. It is a nearby seaside area.
- **Cabo Negro** is north of Martil, known for its golf resort according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martil).
- **M'diq** sits between Tetouan and Fnideq. It has a marina and fishing harbour and also draws weekend visitors from Ceuta, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%27diq).

These towns belong to the M'diq-Fnideq prefecture. In summer, traffic, parking pressure and prices rise. If you have a car, check that the accommodation has parking.

## Which type of accommodation?

| Situation | Best option |
|---|---|
| One night in transit | Hotel in Tetouan (Ensanche) |
| Culture, 2 nights | Guesthouse or riad in the medina, after checking access |
| Family at the beach, 1 to 3 weeks | Furnished apartment in Martil, Cabo Negro or M'diq |
| Touring | Campsite or small coastal hotel, with parking |

For furnished rentals, see the [short-term rental guide](/en/short-term-rental-morocco-rules). To compare riad and hotel: [riad or hotel](/en/riad-or-hotel-morocco-choose).

## Precautions

- **High season (July-August):** heavy demand, book ahead.
- **Seasonal prices:** variable, check at booking.
- **Check the listing:** real location (walking distance to the beach or not), noise, water, air conditioning.
- **Deposit and cancellation:** ask for the terms in writing.
