Morocco reinstated military service with **Law 44-18** (2019). Its implementing decree, **Decree 2-19-46 of 19 February 2019**, sets out the census, call-up and exemptions, and includes an article specific to **Moroccans residing abroad**. This guide summarises that decree, read on Adala, the official portal of the Ministry of Justice. It does not replace an answer from your consulate.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- The decree says Moroccans abroad **may register on their own initiative** on the census website, with their **consular registration number** (article 12).
- It sets **no summons procedure specific to them**; for your case, ask your **consulate**, which the decree tasks with informing those concerned.
- If a **notice** reaches your family in Morocco, **do not ignore it**: there are 20 days to fill in the form (articles 8 and 9).
</Callout>

## The census, step by step

| Step | What Decree 2-19-46 provides |
|---|---|
| Starting list | The Ministry of the Interior uses the CNIE database and public data (education, higher education, vocational training) (art. 3) |
| Period | Once a year, for **60 days**; dates set by the Minister of the Interior and announced **30 days** in advance (art. 4) |
| Information | An information desk in every prefecture and province (art. 5); a dedicated website (art. 6) |
| Notice | Written notice handed, against receipt, to the person or their family by the local authority (art. 8) |
| Form | To be completed online within **20 days** of the notice; the local authority provides an equipped office (art. 9) |
| Exemption | Request within **20 days** of the form, with supporting documents, to the local authority where you live (art. 10) |
| Selection | A provincial committee rules on exemptions; others receive a call-up order and attend with it and their CNIE (arts. 13 to 15) |

The census website is known as tajnid.ma according to official statements reported by the press; we could not access it on 19/10/2026.

## What the decree provides for Moroccans abroad

**Article 12** allows Moroccans residing abroad who wish to do military service, and who meet the conditions of the law, to fill in the online form **on their own initiative and in person** during the census period, giving their **consular registration number**. Embassy and consulate staff provide the necessary information on site.

**Article 16** gives a committee at the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** the task of drawing up the list of volunteers living abroad.

Not registered with your consulate yet? See our guide to [passport, CNIE and consulate](/en/renew-moroccan-passport-id-abroad).

<Callout type="info" title="What we have not confirmed">
Age conditions are set in article 4 of **Law 44-18**, which we have not read: we give no figures here. The length of service, penalties and the position of someone abroad whose CNIE address is in Morocco are not covered by the decree. Ask your consulate or your prefecture's information desk.
</Callout>

## Exemptions provided

- **Provincial committee decision**: people whose request is approved are exempted, temporarily or permanently, as are those not admitted by the selection committee (art. 17).
- **Family breadwinner**: a person supporting relatives who would lack sufficient resources; certificate issued by the governor after the committee's decision (art. 18).
- **Studies**: provisional exemption for one school or university year, renewable, on presentation of a certificate (art. 20).
- **Not invited or not called up**: on provisional exemption and in a **regular situation**; the military authority issues a certificate on request (art. 21).

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Leaving a notice unanswered** because you live abroad: the 20-day period runs from receipt.
- **Trusting a rule read on a forum**: only Law 44-18, the decree and your consulate are authoritative.
- **Forgetting your consular registration** if you want to volunteer: the number is required.

- Check my consular registration and note the number
- Ask my family in Morocco to tell me about any notice received
- Watch for the census dates (announced 30 days ahead)
- If seeking exemption: gather supporting documents (studies, breadwinner)
- Keep receipts and certificates

</Checklist>
