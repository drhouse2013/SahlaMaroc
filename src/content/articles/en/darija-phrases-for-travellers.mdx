Moroccans speak **Darija**, a Moroccan Arabic with Amazigh, French and Spanish influences. French is widely understood in cities, and English is growing among young people. Still, a few words of Darija open doors and get you better prices in the souk.

<Callout type="tip" title="The 5 words that matter most">
**Salam** (hello) · **Choukran** (thank you) · **La, choukran** (no, thanks) · **Bchhal?** (how much?) · **Wakha** (OK)
</Callout>

## Greetings and politeness

| Darija | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| السلام عليكم | *salam alikoum* | Hello (reply: *wa alikoum salam*) |
| صباح الخير | *sbah lkhir* | Good morning |
| شكرا | *choukran* | Thank you |
| عافاك | *afak* | Please |
| سمح ليا | *smeh liya* | Excuse me / sorry |
| بسلامة | *bslama* | Goodbye |
| لاباس؟ | *labas?* | How are you? (reply: *labas, lhamdoulilah*) |

## In the souk

| Darija | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| بشحال؟ | *bchhal?* | How much? |
| غالي بزاف | *ghali bzaf* | Too expensive |
| نقص شوية | *n'qes chwiya* | Lower it a bit |
| آخر ثمن؟ | *akher taman?* | Your last price? |
| لا، شكرا | *la, choukran* | No, thanks |

## Getting around

| Darija | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| فين…؟ | *fin…?* | Where is…? |
| ديني ل… | *dini l…* | Take me to… |
| شغل الكونتور عافاك | *chaghel lcontor afak* | Turn on the meter, please |
| وقف هنا | *wqef hna* | Stop here |
| نيشان | *nichan* | Straight ahead |

## Food and cafés

| Darija | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| أتاي | *atay* | Mint tea |
| قهوة نص نص | *qahwa nss nss* | Half coffee, half milk |
| بلا سكر | *bla sokkar* | Without sugar |
| بنين! | *bnin!* | Delicious! |
| الحساب عافاك | *lhsab afak* | The bill, please |

## Numbers for prices

1 *wahed* · 2 *jouj* · 3 *tlata* · 4 *rb'a* · 5 *khamsa* · 10 *'achra* · 20 *'achrin* · 50 *khamsin* · 100 *mya*

<Callout type="info" title="Good to know">
In markets, prices are sometimes given in **rials**, an old unit still used orally: 20 rials = 1 dirham. If a price sounds huge, ask "*b dirham?*" (in dirhams?).
</Callout>
