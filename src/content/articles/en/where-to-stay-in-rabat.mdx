Rabat, the capital, is green, clean and easy to live in: tram, beach, a quiet medina and major monuments. It's a great base if you like human-sized cities. Here are the neighbourhoods to compare.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **First visit and heritage:** **Hassan** (Hassan Tower, mausoleum, palace) or the **medina / Udayas**.
- **Practical and lively:** **Agdal**, a modern district with shops and restaurants.
- **Calm and residential:** **Souissi**, away from the centre.
</Callout>

## The neighbourhoods

| Area | Atmosphere | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Medina and Kasbah of the Udayas** | Blue-and-white lanes, riads and guest houses | The best riads are in the central part of the medina; rooms around 700–1,200 MAD |
| **Hassan** | Historic quarter: Hassan Tower, Mohammed V Mausoleum, royal palace | Large hotels, close to monuments |
| **Agdal** | Modern, commercial, lively; business district | Ideal for convenience and restaurants |
| **Souissi** | Residential, villas, embassies | A haven of peace; you need a taxi to get around |

Prices shown are **ballpark figures reported in 2026** and vary by season; check when booking.

## Tips

1. **The tram** links Rabat to Salé, the station and the medina: see [tram and BRT](/en/tram-brt-casablanca-rabat-guide).
2. **Rabat-Ville station:** the **Al Boraq** high-speed train puts you about 1 h 20 from Tangier: see [trains in Morocco](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
3. **Rabat-Salé airport:** [getting to the centre](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre).
4. **Riad in the medina:** ask how to get there from the station or taxi.
5. **2030 World Cup:** Rabat is among the proposed host cities (FIFA's final list not yet published); plan ahead: [host cities](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-host-cities).

## Rabat or Casablanca?

Rabat is calmer and greener; Casablanca is bigger, livelier and cheaper. Many travellers stay in one and visit the other by train: read [where to stay in Casablanca](/en/where-to-stay-in-casablanca).
