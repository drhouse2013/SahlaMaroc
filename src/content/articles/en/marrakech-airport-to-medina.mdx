Marrakech Menara airport (RAK) is only about **6 km from Jemaa el-Fna**, so the ride is short. The only real challenge is paying a fair price after a long flight.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Cheapest:** airport bus **line 19**, **30 MAD** per person, about every 20 minutes from roughly 6 am to 9:30 pm.
- **Easiest:** a taxi from the official rank, roughly **70–80 MAD by day** and **100–120 MAD at night** according to published rate grids. Agree on the price before you get in.
- **Zero stress:** a pre-booked transfer with a driver holding your name. Ideal if your riad is deep in the medina.
</Callout>

## Option 1: the airport bus (line 19)

The airport bus stops outside arrivals and serves Jemaa el-Fna, Koutoubia and the main Gueliz hotels. It runs about every 20 minutes from roughly 6:00 to 21:30 (check the last departure if you land late), and the trip takes about 20 to 25 minutes without traffic and up to 50 in rush hour. It is the best choice if you travel light and arrive during the day.

**Tip:** have small change ready, and check the return schedule if you need the bus for your flight home.

## Option 2: taxi from the official rank

Walk past the people offering rides inside the terminal and go to the **official taxi rank** outside. Prices are higher than in town, especially at night, and meters are rarely used from the airport.

- Ask the price first and confirm it is for the whole car, not per person.
- Give the name of your riad **and** the nearest landmark (Bab Doukkala, Riad Zitoun, Kasbah…).
- Cars cannot enter most of the medina. The driver will drop you at the nearest gate.

## Option 3: a pre-booked transfer

For a late arrival, a family or a riad in a maze of alleys, a transfer is worth it. The driver waits with a sign, takes you to the closest drivable point, and many riads send someone to walk you the last few hundred metres.

## Getting to your riad in the medina

This is where newcomers get lost. Boys and young men may offer to "help" carry your bags and then ask for money. That's fine if you want the help, but agree on a price first (10–20 MAD is common). Otherwise, ask your riad to send someone to meet you at the drop-off point and share your arrival time on WhatsApp.

<Callout type="warning" title="Have dirhams before you leave the airport">
The dirham is a closed currency, so you can't buy it at home. Withdraw a small amount at a bank ATM in arrivals for the taxi and tips. Exchange counters are fine for small amounts but check the rate first. More in our [guide to cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
</Callout>

## Steps on arrival

1. **Clear customs and collect bags.**
2. **Withdraw dirhams** at the hall ATM.
3. **Decide the mode:** bus 19, official taxi or booked transfer.
4. **Message your riad** with your arrival time and ask for the drop-off point.
5. **Fix the taxi price** before boarding, for the whole car.
6. **Follow the driver or the riad's guide** for the last metres in the medina.

## Common mistakes

- Following a stranger offering a taxi inside the terminal.
- Not giving the landmark near your riad.
- Accepting an improvised "guide" with no agreed price: see [scams and safety](/en/morocco-scams-safety-travellers).
- Counting on bus 19 after its last departure.
- Forgetting small change.

## FAQ

**How long from the airport to Jemaa el-Fna?** About 20 to 25 minutes without traffic by bus per the sources cited; more at peak times.

**Do taxis use the meter?** Rarely from the airport: fix the price.

**Where is official information?** The [ONDA](https://www.onda.ma) site.

**What about continuing by train?** See [trains in Morocco](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq); there is no tram in Marrakech.

## Three arrival profiles

**Solo traveller, daytime, backpack.** Bus 19 is cheapest; get off at the stop near Jemaa el-Fna or the Koutoubia, then walk or take a petit taxi to your medina gate.

**Couple with a riad deep in the medina.** An official taxi or transfer drops you at the nearest drivable gate; the riad sends someone to guide you. Send your arrival time on WhatsApp.

**MRE family with children and bags.** Skip the bus: a grand taxi or transfer for the whole family is simpler. Have dirhams in small notes and fix the price before loading the suitcases. If you continue to Essaouira or Ouarzazate, see [buses and coaches](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco) and [car rental](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide).

## Before your return flight

Plan to leave the medina well ahead and book the taxi the day before. Avoid spending all your dirhams: the dirham is not easy to reconvert ([cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco)).

## Extra questions

**Does bus 19 take luggage?** Yes, subject to space; departures thin out in the evening.

**Can I book the transfer through my riad?** Yes, many riads offer a pick-up or transfer: ask the price in advance.

**What if the driver refuses to drop me at the door?** Cars cannot enter most lanes: ask for the nearest drop-off point and call your host.

## Good to know

Marrakech airport sits close to the city, so a short ride should not cost much: if a price seems high, step back and use the official rank. Have your riad's address and phone number saved offline, and take a photo of the entrance door so you can recognise it.
