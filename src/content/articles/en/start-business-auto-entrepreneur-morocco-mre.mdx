Returning to Morocco to work for yourself is a common plan, but the choice between **auto-entrepreneur** and a **company** (SARL, single-member SARL, etc.) affects taxation, social protection and liability. This guide gives the general framework and flags what we could not verify in an official text. See also [AMO after the return](/en/amo-health-insurance-return-morocco-mre) and [MRE taxes](/en/mre-taxes-morocco-what-to-declare).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Auto-entrepreneur**: simplified status (law 114-13), registered online on the **ae.gov.ma** portal, which secondary sources say is run by Barid Al-Maghrib with the tax authority and the CNSS.
- **Company**: formalities with the **Regional Investment Centre (CRI)** and the **OMPIC**; the legal form depends on your project.
- **Contributions**: CNSS amounts for auto-entrepreneurs published by third-party sites **disagree**; do not pay on that basis.
- **Overlap**: already being an employee affiliated to the CNSS may raise questions for the status; check before registering.
</Callout>

## 1. Auto-entrepreneur: what sources say

- Registration is **fully online** on ae.gov.ma, with the CNIE, proof of address under three months old and a sworn statement (list cited by a third-party source).
- You act in your own name, without a company; tax is computed on **turnover** (rate depends on the activity: confirm on the tax authority site).
- Annual turnover ceilings depend on the type of activity (trade, craft, services): **check the official site** before choosing this status.

## 2. Company: the general steps

1. Choose the legal form and capital.
2. Obtain a **negative certificate** (company name) from the OMPIC.
3. Go through the regional **CRI** for registration and the trade register.
4. Register the business with the tax office (tax ID, ICE) and the CNSS.

Timelines, costs and documents vary: ask the CRI for the list. A Moroccan accountant or lawyer helps with the articles of association.

## 3. Social protection and health

The AMO of a manager or auto-entrepreneur depends on the status chosen. See [health insurance after the return](/en/amo-health-insurance-return-morocco-mre). If you return with rights abroad, [retirement and conventions](/en/retire-in-morocco-pension-tax-guide) may matter.

## 4. Money, banking and tax

- A [bank account in Morocco](/en/open-bank-account-morocco-mre) is needed to receive income and pay contributions.
- Capital contributed from abroad: see [sending money](/en/send-money-to-morocco) and keep transfer receipts.
- Your tax residence determines taxation: see [MRE taxes](/en/mre-taxes-morocco-what-to-declare).

## What was not confirmed

- 2026 ceilings and rates for auto-entrepreneurs.
- Exact CNSS contributions (144 MAD a month on one site, 224 to 1,200 MAD a month on another).
- Any advantages reserved for investing MREs.
