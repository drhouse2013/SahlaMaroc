Moving back to Morocco to work for yourself is a common MRE project, but the choice between **auto-entrepreneur** and a **company** (SARL, SARL AU…) affects tax, social protection and liability. The trap is registering fast on the strength of an online guide whose ceilings and contributions differ from site to site. This guide gives the framework, the steps, the mistakes to avoid, and flags every figure we could not confirm on an official text. See also [AMO after returning](/en/amo-health-insurance-return-morocco-mre) and [MRE taxes](/en/mre-taxes-morocco-what-to-declare).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Auto-entrepreneur**: simplified status under law 114-13, for individuals only, registered online on **ae.gov.ma** (run, according to secondary sources, by Barid Al-Maghrib with the DGI and CNSS).
- **Company**: negative certificate from **OMPIC**, then registration through the **Regional Investment Centre (CRI)** and the commercial court.
- **Ceilings and rates**: secondary sources cite 500,000 MAD (trade, crafts) and 200,000 MAD (services), to be confirmed with the DGI.
- **CNSS contributions**: amounts published on third-party sites **differ**; do not pay on that basis.
</Callout>

## 1. Auto-entrepreneur: what the sources say

- **Online** registration on ae.gov.ma with the national ID, a proof of address under three months old and an ID photo (list cited by third-party sources). Our tools could not read the portal.
- Activity in your own name, with no company or partner; tax on **cash turnover**, at a low rate depending on the activity (0.5% trade, 1% services according to secondary sources).
- Annual ceiling by activity: 500,000 MAD trade, industry, crafts; 200,000 MAD services, with a per-client limit cited by some guides (80,000 MAD). One site gives other figures: **check with the DGI**.
- No company means no limited liability: your personal assets answer for the activity's debts.

## 2. Company: the general steps

1. **Choose the form**: SARL (several partners) or SARL AU (single partner), depending on the project. Set the object, registered office and capital.
2. **Negative certificate** (reserving the name) from OMPIC. Amounts and validity vary by source (35 to 230 MAD); check the tariff on ompic.ma.
3. **Articles**, tax registration and subscription declaration, with a notary, lawyer or professional as needed.
4. **Registration** in the commercial register through the **CRI**, then tax ID, ICE and **CNSS** registration as an employer if applicable.
5. **Bank**: a business account to receive the capital; keep proof of transfers from abroad.

Secondary sources give timeframes from a few days (CRI portal) to several weeks (full process with a professional); none is official. Fees vary widely: ask for a written quote.

### How to choose

- **Testing an idea, working alone, low volume**: auto-entrepreneur avoids articles and full accounting, but you stay under the ceiling and without limited liability.
- **Partners, investors, employees, large contracts or high risk**: a company, with its fixed costs, protects your assets better and reassures clients.
- **Switching from one to the other**: possible along the way, but plan the closure of the status, exit filings and the transfer of clients; get accounting advice before you hit the ceiling.
- **Clients abroad**: the treatment of foreign-currency payments and VAT depends on the nature of the services; do not guess, have it validated by the DGI or an accountant.

## 3. Money from abroad and MRE status

- A [bank account in Morocco](/en/open-bank-account-morocco-mre) is needed to receive income and pay contributions.
- To bring capital from abroad, prefer traced bank transfers; keep the transfer advices, useful with the bank and the Foreign Exchange Office: see [sending money](/en/send-money-to-morocco) and [transferring savings](/en/transfer-savings-morocco-mre).
- Your tax residence determines taxation: see [MRE taxes](/en/mre-taxes-morocco-what-to-declare). A tax treaty may avoid double taxation, depending on your country.

## 4. Social protection and health

AMO for an auto-entrepreneur or manager depends on the status chosen. A non-salaried worker faces, according to CLEISS, a 3-month qualifying period for AMO: see [health insurance after returning](/en/amo-health-insurance-return-morocco-mre). If you come back with foreign entitlements, [retirement and treaties](/en/retire-in-morocco-pension-tax-guide) may matter.

## 5. Common mistakes

- **Registering before checking the ceiling**: exceeding the turnover limit takes you out of the regime.
- **Mixing personal and business use** of the bank account.
- **Forgetting filing obligations** even at low activity.
- **Creating a company too fast** when a simple status is enough to test the market.
- **Paying CNSS contributions** on the word of a single third-party site.
- **Neglecting insurance and contracts** with your first clients.

## Checklist

- Status chosen: auto-entrepreneur or company, with a professional's advice
- Turnover ceiling and rate checked on the DGI site
- Moroccan bank account opened, flows from abroad documented
- Auto-entrepreneur: national ID, proof of address, photo; registration on ae.gov.ma
- Company: OMPIC negative certificate, articles, CRI registration
- CNSS and AMO affiliation confirmed in writing
- Tax obligations and filing dates noted
