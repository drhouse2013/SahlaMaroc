Sleeping under the stars in the dunes of **Erg Chebbi**, near Merzouga, is the trip most visitors remember for life. From Marrakech it's about **560 km**, so the classic format is **3 days and 2 nights**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **3 days** is the minimum and the most popular option. Choose **4 days** if you hate long drives.
- **Shared tours** are the cheapest. Private tours give you control over stops and timing.
- **Best season:** October to April. Desert nights are cold in winter, so bring a warm layer.
</Callout>

## Day 1: Atlas mountains and Aït Benhaddou

You leave Marrakech early and cross the High Atlas over the **Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260 m)**, a spectacular road of hairpin bends. Lunch is usually near **Aït Benhaddou**, a fortified earthen village listed by UNESCO and a famous film set. You continue via Ouarzazate to the **Dades Valley** for the night.

## Day 2: Todra Gorge and the camel ride

In the morning, you walk through the **Todra Gorge**, where rock walls rise hundreds of metres above a narrow river. After a long drive through palm groves and the town of Rissani, you reach **Merzouga** in the late afternoon. You swap the car for a camel and ride into the dunes at sunset to a **desert camp**: dinner, drums and an incredible sky.

## Day 3: sunrise and the long road back

Climb a dune for sunrise, ride back to the village, have breakfast and start the drive back to Marrakech, which takes **9 to 10 hours** with stops. You arrive in the evening.

## How to choose a good tour

- **Read recent reviews** and look for comments about the driver, the vehicle and the camp.
- **Check what "camp" means.** Standard camps have shared toilets; "luxury" camps have private tents with bathrooms.
- **Check what's included:** dinners and breakfasts are usually included, lunches usually aren't.
- **Free cancellation** is valuable in winter, when snow can close the Tichka pass.

## What to pack

A warm jacket (even in spring), a scarf for wind and sand, sunscreen, sunglasses, a power bank and some cash for lunches and tips. See our [tipping guide](/en/tipping-in-morocco) for drivers and camel guides.

<Callout type="info" title="Short on time?">
The **Agafay desert**, about 40 km from Marrakech, offers a rocky desert, camel rides and dinner under the stars in a single evening. It isn't the Sahara, but it's a good alternative for a short trip.
</Callout>
