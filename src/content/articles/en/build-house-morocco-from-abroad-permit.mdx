Building back home is the dream of many MREs. It is also a project where it is easy to lose money from a distance: land poorly checked, permit forgotten, contractor paid upfront, site unsupervised. Here is the method, **in order**. For figures we can prove we cite the **2026 MRE tax guide**; for deadlines and documents we stay cautious because they depend on the municipality.

<Callout type="tip" title="The 6 golden rules">
- **Land first**: land title, recent ownership certificate, buildable zone.
- **An architect** signs the plans and supervises the site.
- **Permit before the first digger**, through **Rokhas**.
- **Staged payments** tied to progress, always by traceable transfer.
- **Occupancy permit** at the end: it conditions occupation, connections and resale.
- **Tax declarations** not to be forgotten above 300 sq m built.
</Callout>

## The 7-step journey

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 1" title="Secure the land">

- Ask the land registry for a **recent ownership certificate** (ANCFCC online service): owner, mortgages, encumbrances.
- Prefer land with a **land title**. See [buying property from abroad](/en/buying-property-morocco-from-abroad). Reminder of costs: 5% registration duty on bare land, versus 4% for housing.
- Check that the plot is **buildable** and know the rules (height, footprint, setbacks).
- Land in **joint ownership**: require the written agreement of all co-owners.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 2" title="Request the urban planning information note">

This document, issued by the **urban agency**, states what the development plan allows on your plot. Your architect will tell you whether it is required and how long it stays valid in your municipality.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 3" title="Choose an architect and an engineering firm">

- The **architect** designs the plans, signs the file and takes responsibility. Check he is **registered with the Order of Architects**.
- The **structural engineering firm** calculates the structure.
- Require **verifiable references**, a **written contract**, a schedule and the detail of the site-supervision mission.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 4" title="File the permit application">

The national **Rokhas** platform describes the building-permit and occupancy-permit procedures. The file is prepared with the architect and generally includes plans, the structural study, proof of ownership and the urban planning note. **The exact list of documents depends on the municipality**: do not bet on a list found online.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 5" title="Processing: wait without starting">

Deadlines are not fixed: we could not confirm them from an official source, and they depend on the municipality and how complete the file is. **Start no earthworks before authorisation.** Use the time to compare contractors.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 6" title="The site">

- Display the authorisation on site.
- Choose the contractor on **verified references**, with a contract stating price, deadlines and late penalties.
- Pay **in stages** (foundations, structure, finishing works, fit-out), never a large advance.
- Have the architect **supervise the site** and plan regular visits, or a trusted relative with dated photos and video.
- Keep **all invoices**: they also serve for the construction-cost declaration and, later, for the capital gain.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 7" title="Occupancy permit">

At the end of works you request the **occupancy permit** (procedure described on Rokhas). Without it you cannot lawfully occupy the building, and resale becomes difficult.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## What the tax authority says when you build for yourself (2026 MRE tax guide)

- **Up to 300 sq m built** (main home): exemption from the social solidarity contribution on self-supplied construction.
- **Above 300 sq m**: the contribution is due on the **entire** built area (rate of 60 MAD for the 301-400 band, 100 MAD for 401-500 and 150 MAD above 500 according to the guide's scale, whose unit of calculation should be confirmed on tax.gov.ma). The **declaration is due within 90 days** of the occupancy permit being issued, with the occupancy permit and the building authorisation.
- **Annual construction-cost declaration** (above 300 sq m): before the end of February each year, from the start of works until the occupancy permit, with a statement of invoices. In the first year attach an **architect's certificate** estimating the cost; the last one shows the overall cost.
- **Housing tax**: 5-year exemption for a new build used as the main home; the **municipal services tax** remains due. The **completion declaration** is due by 31 January of the following year.

Scales and thresholds can change with finance laws: check [tax.gov.ma](https://www.tax.gov.ma) before declaring.

## Budget: keep a margin

On top of the building cost come architect and engineering fees, taxes and file fees, utility connections, fencing and landscaping. Keep a **10 to 15% margin** (good-practice rule of thumb, not a regulation) and simulate the total with the [budget calculator](/en/morocco-budget-calculator). If you borrow, the Office des changes allows a dirham loan to an MRE of up to 80% of the price to **buy or build**: see the [MRE mortgage guide](/en/mortgage-morocco-mre-guide).

## Common mistakes

- **Building without a permit** or **starting before authorisation**: possible penalties and no occupancy permit.
- **A contractor paid upfront** who disappears: pay on progress, against verified stages.
- **Land poorly checked**: joint ownership, mortgage, non-buildable zone.
- **Plans changed on site** without authorisation.
- **No supervision**: defects found at handover.
- **Forgetting tax declarations** (construction cost, contribution above 300 sq m).
- **Handing full powers** to a relative with no framework: see [power of attorney](/en/power-of-attorney-morocco-property-mre).

<Checklist id="construire-maison-maroc" lang="en" title="Building in Morocco: my checklist">

- Recent ownership certificate and buildable land verified
- Architect registered with the Order, written contract with supervision mission
- Engineering firm and contractor chosen on references
- Building permit obtained before any earthworks
- Staged payments, all by traceable transfer
- Invoices and dated photos kept
- Occupancy permit requested and obtained
- Tax declarations checked (construction cost, contribution above 300 sq m)

</Checklist>

## Power of attorney and remote follow-up

If you cannot be on site for signatures, give **precise, time-limited** powers in the required form: read [power of attorney for property](/en/power-of-attorney-morocco-property-mre).
