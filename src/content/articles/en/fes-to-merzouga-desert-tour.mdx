Leaving Fes for a night in the **Erg Chebbi** dunes near Merzouga is a very different tour from the one out of Marrakech. It crosses the **Middle Atlas**, passes Ifrane and the cedar forests, descends to Midelt and the Ziz valley, and reaches the first dunes. This guide is for those **leaving from the north**: if you are in Marrakech, see [Marrakech → Merzouga](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour). You will find the stages, inclusions, how to choose and the mistakes to avoid.

<Callout type="tip" title="In brief">
- **Recommended length:** 3 days / 2 nights, 4 days for less driving.
- **Landscapes:** Middle Atlas, cedar forests, Ziz valley, palm groves then dunes.
- **Season:** autumn, winter and early spring; summer is very hot, winter nights cold.
- **Prices and timetables:** vary by operator, confirm in writing.
</Callout>

## The typical itinerary

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 1" title="Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz valley">

Leave Fes and climb into the **Middle Atlas**: Ifrane, a mountain town with pitched roofs, and the cedar forest near Azrou, home to Barbary macaques. The route continues to Midelt then descends to the **Ziz valley** and its palm groves. Night in Erfoud or at the desert gates depending on the package. This first stage is long: ask for the detail.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 2" title="To Merzouga: dunes and a night in camp">

Arrive in **Merzouga** late in the afternoon. Ride a **camel** or take a 4×4 to the camp at sunset, dinner, music, a night under the stars.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 3" title="Sunrise and the long drive back">

Sunrise over the dunes, breakfast, then the long drive back to Fes. The duration depends on stops, confirm with the operator.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

The Erg Chebbi dunes form a sand field extending over several tens of kilometres, at the far west of the Sahara. For places and accommodation on site, see the [Merzouga city page](/en/cities/merzouga) and [where to stay in Merzouga](/en/where-to-stay-in-merzouga).

## What is included (usually)

- Transport in a 4×4 or air-conditioned minibus, with driver or guide
- **Night in camp** in Erg Chebbi and hotel or riad nights on the way
- Half board (breakfast and dinner), sometimes lunch
- Camel ride, sometimes sandboarding

**Check:** included meals, camp type (shared or private tents), tips, tourist taxes, cancellation. No price is shown here because offers differ greatly.

## Best time and level

Autumn and spring offer the best conditions. In winter, Ifrane and the Middle Atlas can be snowy, which may complicate the road; desert nights are cold. In summer, southern heat makes the long driving days gruelling. The tour needs no particular fitness but is **tiring**: hours on the road, the camel, the dune climb. Plan 4 days if you fear long drives.

## How to choose

1. **Recent reviews** about the driver, vehicle and camp.
2. **Very low prices:** beware of overcrowded camps or shortened stages.
3. **Free cancellation**, useful in winter when snow can affect the Middle Atlas.
4. **Small group** or private: more flexibility for stops and photos.
5. **Written programme** with nights, meals and the start point in Fes.

We recommend no operator names.

## What to pack

Warm clothes (even in spring), scarf for the sand, glasses, sunscreen, power bank, a small bag for the camp night and cash for lunches and tips: see [tipping](/en/tipping-in-morocco). For accommodation in Fes before departure: [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes) and the [Fes city page](/en/cities/fes). Morocco is on GMT all year, so desert sunrise is earlier on the clock: see [time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt).

## Common mistakes

1. **Booking a flight or train** for the evening of the return: arrival can be late.
2. **Choosing on price alone.**
3. **Not asking for the written programme**: stages and nights vary by operator.
4. **Neglecting the cold** at night in camp.
5. **Confusing this tour** with the one from Marrakech: landscapes and stages differ.
