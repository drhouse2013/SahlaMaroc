Leaving Fes for a night among the dunes of **Erg Chebbi**, near Merzouga, is one of the country's finest routes. It crosses the **Middle Atlas**, the cedar forests of Ifrane, the Ziz valley and palm groves before reaching the Sahara. Here is the typical itinerary, distances and points to check.

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- **Recommended length:** 3 days / 2 nights (4 days if you hate long drives).
- **Distance:** about **470 km** (≈ 7 h of driving) between Merzouga and Fes, with **stops**.
- **Best season:** **October to April**. Summer is very hot, winter nights are cold.
- **Alternative from Marrakech:** [Marrakech → Merzouga](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour).
</Callout>

## The typical itinerary

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 1" title="Fes → Ifrane → Azrou → Midelt → Ziz valley → Erfoud">

You cross the **Middle Atlas**: Ifrane (Morocco's "little Switzerland"), the cedar forest near Azrou, then Midelt, before dropping towards the **Ziz valley** and its palm groves. Overnight in Erfoud or at the gates of the desert depending on the package. Some packages head to the Todra gorges instead: this first leg is around 400 km.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 2" title="Erfoud → Merzouga: dunes and camp night">

Arrive at **Merzouga** in late afternoon. Ride a **camel** or a 4×4 to the camp at sunset, with dinner, music and a night under the stars.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Day 3" title="Sunrise and the way back">

Sunrise over the dunes, breakfast, then the long drive back to Fes (about 7 h with stops).

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## What's usually included

- **4×4 or air-conditioned minibus** transport, French- or English-speaking driver or guide;
- **Night in a camp** in Erg Chebbi and hotel or riad nights on the way;
- **Half board** (breakfast and dinner), sometimes lunch;
- **Camel ride**, sometimes sandboarding.

**To check:** included meals, camp type (shared or private tents with bathroom), tips, tourist taxes, cancellation.

## How to choose

1. **Recent reviews** about the driver, vehicle and camp.
2. **Very low prices:** beware of overcrowded camps or shortened stages.
3. **Free cancellation:** valuable in winter when snow can complicate the Middle Atlas.
4. **Small group** or private: more flexibility for stops and photos.

## What to pack

Warm clothes (even in spring), a scarf for sand, sunglasses, sunscreen, a power bank and cash for lunches and tips: see [tipping](/en/tipping-in-morocco). Stay in Fes before departure: [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes). Morocco is on GMT all year, so desert sunrise is earlier on the clock than before: see [time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt).
