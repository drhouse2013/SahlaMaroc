Essaouira is a small, human-sized coastal city listed by UNESCO. The choice is simple: **medina** (riads, atmosphere, everything on foot) or **seafront** (beach, pool, parking). The wind, which earned it the nickname "city of the trade winds", can also guide your choice.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Atmosphere, lanes, port and ramparts on foot:** a **riad in the medina**.
- **Beach, pool, parking, a more modern hotel:** the **Dunes district** or the **coast road** to the south.
- **Small budget:** hostel or guest room in the medina.
</Callout>

## Medina or seafront?

| | Medina | Seafront (Dunes district, coast road) |
|---|---|---|
| **Atmosphere** | White-and-blue lanes, souks, port, ramparts | Long beach, hotels with pool and spa |
| **Access** | On foot, no car | Car or taxi, parking |
| **Indicative prices** | Riads: ≈ 250–800 MAD a night (budget to mid-range) | Hotels: ≈ 400–1,600 MAD a night |
| **Wind** | Lanes give some shelter | More exposed, good for kitesurfing |
| **Ideal for** | Couples, wanderers, short stays | Families, sporty types, long stays |

**Indicative prices reported in 2026**, varying by season.

## Tips

1. **A night on site beats a day trip:** the town is at its best in the evening and early morning. See [Essaouira day trip from Marrakech](/en/essaouira-day-trip-from-marrakech).
2. **Pack a windbreaker,** even in summer.
3. **Access:** bus from Marrakech or Agadir: [CTM and Supratours buses](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco).
4. **Remote work:** Essaouira suits focused stays: [best cities for nomads](/en/best-cities-digital-nomads-morocco).
5. **Kitesurfing:** for a true wind country, also see [Dakhla](/en/dakhla-kitesurf-guide).
