Oujda is the main city of the Oriental region, Morocco's north-eastern gateway. Families often stop here, or use it as a base to reach Saidia and its beach.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Stopover, flight or train:** a hotel in **central Oujda**.
- **Culture and narrow lanes:** the **medina**.
- **Beach in summer:** **Saidia**, roughly 50 to 60 km from Oujda depending on the source.
- **July-August:** heavy family demand, book early.
</Callout>

## Oujda: the areas

### Medina

Oujda's medina covers about 25 hectares, ringed by 19th-century walls with four main gates (including Bab Sidi Abdelouahab and Bab El Khemis), according to [Wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oujda). It remains traditional. Interesting by day, it suits those who like narrow lanes; accommodation is more limited there.

### Modern centre

For a stopover, errands or a night before a flight, staying in town outside the medina is the most practical: shops, cafés and taxis nearby. For each listing, check the real distance to the ONCF station or to wherever you have business.

### Sidi Yahya

An oasis about 6 km from the centre that holds the mausoleum of Sidi Yahya Benyounes and a Friday souk, according to Wikipedia. It is an outing, not an accommodation area.

## Saidia: the beach near Oujda

Saidia is a seaside resort in Berkane province. According to [Wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saïdia), it is described as a holiday destination for thousands of people from eastern Morocco, with one of the country's longest beaches, the Moulouya river to the west and the Kiss wadi to the east. It also has a Plan Azur resort, **Méditerrania Saïdia**, opened in 2009, with a marina, hotels and a golf course (the [ONMT](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/oujda-saidia/sport) presents Saidia's marina and golf). Distances differ (52 to 60 km from Oujda depending on the page): work out your journey before booking. Summer demand is strong.

## Choosing by profile

- **Family in transit, or a Moroccan living abroad with paperwork:** a central hotel with parking, close to where you have business, rather than far from the centre.
- **Couple or culture seeker:** a hotel or guesthouse near the medina, to walk to the places on the [Oujda city page](/en/cities/oujda#spots): the medina, Bab Sidi Abdelwahab, Lalla Aicha Park or the Great Mosque.
- **Two weeks at the beach:** Saidia, with an apartment or residence; Oujda stays a base for errands or an excursion.
- **Long drive:** a hotel with secure parking, and an early start the next day.

Oujda is an inland city: summer is hot, winter cool. The seaside season concentrates on summer, when many diaspora families return; outside it the city is quiet.

## Which type of accommodation?

| Situation | Best option |
|---|---|
| One night, plane or train | Hotel in central Oujda |
| Family, beach, 1 to 3 weeks | Furnished apartment or residence in Saidia |
| Culture | Guesthouse or hotel near the medina |
| Long drive | Hotel with parking |

## Getting there

- **By air:** Angads airport (OUD), listed in the [ONDA airport network](https://www.onda.ma/Je-suis-Passager/Guide-du-voyageur/R%C3%A9seau-a%C3%A9roportuaire), about ten kilometres north of Oujda (10 to 12 km depending on the source). Our [airports guide](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre) explains getting into town.
- **By train:** Oujda station links the west of the country (Fes, Rabat, Casablanca). For summer 2026, ONCF announced, according to [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/06/322614/oncf-rolls-out-special-summer-2026-plan-to-meet-peak-travel-demand/), additional overnight services on Tangier-Oujda from 22 June to 13 September 2026. See the [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
- **Algerian border:** according to [France Diplomatie](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/conseils-aux-voyageurs/conseils-par-pays-destination/maroc/), the land border between Morocco and Algeria is closed (since 1994 according to Wikipedia); do not plan a crossing.
- **By car:** see [car rental](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide).

## Precautions

- **High season:** book early, especially in Saidia.
- **Seasonal prices:** variable, check them.
- **Listing:** real location, distance to the beach, equipment.
- **Deposit and cancellation:** in writing. See the [summer return checklist](/en/summer-return-checklist-mre).
