You have just landed at **Tanger Med** after hours at sea. Good news: the port connects directly to the motorway. Here is how to hit the road without stress, what the tolls cost and where to stop.

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- From the port, the **A5 Tanger Med – Rabat motorway** starts almost at the exit: follow signs for **Rabat / Kenitra**.
- A **Kenitra Nord → Tanger Med** trip costs about **89 MAD** in class 1 (cars); **Kenitra Nord → Tanger Est** about **78 MAD**.
- **ADM service areas** line the route (fuel, food, toilets).
- Keep **cash in small notes** for tolls and drinks.
</Callout>

## Route from the port

1. **After customs**, follow the signs to the port exit and the **Port Tanger Med toll station**.
2. Take the **A5 motorway** towards **Tangier**, then **Kenitra – Rabat**. This is the main axis between Europe and Morocco for tourism and freight, opened in stages between 1995 and 2016.
3. Continue towards **Casablanca** (then Marrakech) or branch off towards **Fes** and the east, depending on your destination.
4. For **Tetouan, Al Hoceima, Nador or Saidia**, the **Mediterranean Ring Road (RN16)** follows the coast and shortens the Tangier – Saidia trip considerably.

<Callout type="info" title="Rush hours">
Traffic is very heavy at the port exit and around Tangier on big arrival days (late June to early August, then late August). If you can, leave early in the morning or in the evening, and keep a margin for the rest of the journey.
</Callout>

## ADM tolls, class 1

Moroccan motorways are run by **ADM (Autoroutes du Maroc)** and charge tolls. **Class 1** means private cars (without trailer). Examples from the official ADM toll grid (grid read on the ADM website on 10 October 2026; it shows no effective date, and the 2024 document listed amounts 1–2 MAD lower):

| Trip (class 1) | Indicative price |
|---|---|
| Kenitra Nord → Sidi Allal Tazi | 14 MAD |
| Kenitra Nord → Moulay Bousselham | 29 MAD |
| Kenitra Nord → Larache | 41 MAD |
| Kenitra Nord → Tanger Ouest | 68 MAD |
| Kenitra Nord → Tanger Est | 78 MAD |
| Kenitra Nord → Port Tanger Med | 89 MAD |
| Casablanca → Rabat | about 25 MAD |

Prices change: check the **official ADM price list** before you go, or use a toll calculator. A car with a caravan or trailer falls into a higher class and pays more.

## Where to stop: service areas

ADM has equipped the Tanger Med – Rabat route with **service areas** and fuel. Main ones include **Oued Rmel** (near Tangier), **Assilah**, **Larache**, **Moulay Bousselham**, **Mnasra** and **Kenitra**. According to ADM, the **Mnasra** area serves both directions. ADM distinguishes **rest areas** (very rare: 2 on the network, for parking and resting) from **service areas** (58 on the network, about 50 km apart on average), which add fuel, car services and food from partner companies.

**Tip:** stop at a proper **service area** (a visible fuel station) rather than an isolated parking spot.

For families:

- Stop **every 2 hours**, especially after night driving in Spain.
- Fill up **before** the long stretches between areas.
- Keep water, snacks and wipes in the cabin.
