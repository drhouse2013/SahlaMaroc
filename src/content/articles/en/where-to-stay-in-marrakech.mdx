Marrakech is not one uniform city: it is a walled medina, a colonial-era new town and residential or hotel zones spreading across the plain. Your area decides your rhythm (walking or taxis), your sleep (noise, heat) and how you will arrive. This guide ranks areas by how you will use them rather than by hotel class.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **First stay, 3–5 nights:** a riad in the northern medina (Mouassine, Bab Doukkala) or near the Kasbah.
- **Long stay, remote work, family with a pushchair:** Gueliz.
- **Pool, garden and calm, with a car or transfers:** Hivernage or the Palmeraie.
</Callout>

## The northern medina: everything on foot, but a maze

This is the postcard Marrakech. Around **Mouassine** and **Bab Doukkala**, the lanes lead to the souks, rooftop cafés and several places listed on the [Marrakech city page](/en/cities/marrakech#spots): the Ben Youssef Madrasa, the Marrakech Museum or the House of Photography.

- **Pros:** monuments and souks on foot, riad atmosphere, breakfast on a terrace.
- **Limits:** no cars in the lanes, confusing layouts after dark, noise near Jemaa el-Fna until late, uneven Wi-Fi in old houses.
- **Tip:** ask your host where they will meet you and which route to avoid after nightfall.

## The Kasbah and Riad Zitoun: palaces and easier taxis

South of Jemaa el-Fna, the **Kasbah** area groups the Saadian Tombs, El Badi Palace and Bahia Palace (all on the city page). Taxis reach the Kasbah side more easily than the northern medina. The trade-off: lanes near Riad Zitoun are busy by day, and nights stay lively around the cafés.

## Gueliz: the "normal life" option

Built under the protectorate, Gueliz has wide avenues, cafés, supermarkets, the railway station and car-rental offices. The medina is a short petit-taxi ride away.

- **Best for:** remote workers, long stays, groups needing several rooms, early trains.
- **Good to know:** less riad charm, but easy landmarks. For Wi-Fi and coworking, see [fibre, internet and coworking](/en/fibre-internet-coworking-morocco).

## Hivernage: big hotels, between two worlds

Hivernage lies between Gueliz and the medina, with large hotels, restaurants and clubs. It is comfortable and within reach of the Koutoubia, but more international than local: a fit for an urban-resort stay with pool and service.

## Palmeraie: space and pools, but a car is essential

To the north, among the palms, villas and resorts offer gardens and large pools. Ideal for a family who wants to rest. Every outing into town needs a taxi or a transfer: plan both ways, especially in the evening.

## Which area for whom?

| Profile | Area to favour | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Couple, 3 nights | Northern medina or Kasbah | Atmosphere and monuments on foot |
| Family with young children | Gueliz or Hivernage | Pavements, pool, taxis |
| Remote work, 2–4 weeks | Gueliz | Cafés, supermarkets, connection |
| Culture and photography | Northern medina | Morning light on the lanes |
| Total rest | Palmeraie | Calm, but car or transfer needed |
| Very early train | Gueliz | Station nearby |

## By season

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable for walking the medina. Summer is very hot: a riad with a shaded patio and a pool, or an air-conditioned hotel, changes everything. In winter days are mild but nights cool, so ask whether rooms are heated. Over the year-end holidays and European school breaks, the best places fill early.

## Arriving and finding your way

- **From the airport:** the guide [from the airport to the medina](/en/marrakech-airport-to-medina) explains options to the gate nearest your riad.
- **Station:** it is in Gueliz; for times and journeys, see [trains in Morocco](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
- **Bus:** for Essaouira or Agadir, see [CTM and Supratours](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco).
- **Day trip:** if you add [Essaouira](/en/essaouira-day-trip-from-marrakech), sleeping on the Gueliz side makes for an earlier start.

## Safety and good habits

Marrakech is very touristy: expect approaches (fake guides, "shortcuts", offers at Jemaa el-Fna). Agree a taxi price before getting in or insist on the meter; confirm any porter's fee. Mopeds are fast in the souks: watch bags and phones.
