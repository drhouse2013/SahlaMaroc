Planning summer 2027? Here is what is known, what is not yet, and how to get organised now.

<Callout type="warning" title="2027 dates: not yet published">
At the time of writing (October 2026) we found **no official announcement** of the Marhaba 2027 dates. The Mohammed V Foundation for Solidarity normally publishes them each spring. The reference points below come from **2026** and are not a confirmation for 2027.
</Callout>

## Benchmark: Marhaba 2026

- The 2026 operation ran **from 10 June to 15 September** (closing announcement on maroc.ma).
- According to the Mohammed V Foundation, the operation is normally activated each year **from 5 June to 15 September**.
- More than 4.1 million MRE were welcomed in 2026 according to the [results published on maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/marhaba-2026-plus-de-41-millions-de-mre-accueillis), with peaks of around 80,000 arrivals a day and return traffic concentrated in late August (see the [Marhaba guide](/en/operation-marhaba-guide)).
- The 2026 launch was announced by the [Mohammed V Foundation](https://www.fm5.ma/en) in spring: watch the same calendar in 2027.

For 2027, plan for a window of **early June to mid-September**, to be confirmed once the official announcement is made.

## The ports

The main entry point is **Tanger Med**, from Algeciras. Other routes arrive at Tangier Ville, Nador and Al Hoceima, or leave from Sète, Genoa, Barcelona, Almería and Motril: lines, durations and operators are detailed in our [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide).

## Booking: when and how

- Book **as soon as sales open** (usually in spring): late July and August slots go first. We will not publish a 2027 opening date until the operators announce it.
- Compare several operators and ports; night or mid-week departures are often less crowded.
- Keep bookings **flexible** where possible and note the port check-in deadline.
- Arrive **2 to 3 hours** before departure in peak season.

## Papers and car

Check your national ID or passport, vehicle registration, licence and insurance valid in Morocco. If you travel in a foreign-registered vehicle, re-read the [180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule).

## Tips to avoid waiting

1. Avoid peak Saturdays and Sundays, outbound and inbound.
2. Shift your return by a few days if you can: queues are longest in late August.
3. Prepare everyone's documents before reaching customs.
4. Follow our [summer return checklist](/en/summer-return-checklist-mre), from 3 months before to departure day.

## Suggested timeline for summer 2027

1. **October to February: fix your dates.** Pick a departure and return window with a few days of slack. Start from the usual window (early June to mid-September) without treating it as certain.
2. **March to May: watch for announcements.** Marhaba dates (Mohammed V Foundation), start of ticket sales, the ADII circular for the season.
3. **As soon as sales open: book** the crossing, then a stopover hotel (Algeciras, Tarifa, Sète) if you drive.
4. **D-3 months: check everyone's papers**: ID or passport (passports valid more than 6 months), authorisations for minors ([children and pets](/en/travel-morocco-children-pets)).
5. **D-1 month: insurance and car**: green card or border insurance ([guide](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border)), roadworthiness check, tyres.

## Common mistakes

- Booking on the strength of a blog announcing "2027 dates" before any official announcement.
- Choosing a non-changeable ticket when your departure date is not fixed.
- Forgetting that the car counts toward the 180-day limit per calendar year: a stay straddling two years is calculated differently ([180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Are the 2027 dates known?** No, no official announcement has been found so far.

**Does Marhaba change ferry prices?** No: fares are set by the operators and rise in July and August.

**Where do I see official announcements?** On [fm5.ma](https://www.fm5.ma/en), [maroc.ma](https://www.maroc.ma) and [mre.gov.ma](https://www.mre.gov.ma).
