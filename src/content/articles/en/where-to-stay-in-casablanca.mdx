Casablanca is the economic capital: huge, lively, more practical than "postcard". Where you sleep changes the experience a lot, as distances are long and traffic dense. Here are the neighbourhoods to know, by traveller profile.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Business, short stays, convenience:** **Maarif** or **downtown**.
- **Trendy, restaurants, galleries:** **Gauthier** (popular with young professionals and expats).
- **Quiet apartment for a short stay:** **Racine**.
- **Beach and nightlife:** **Ain Diab** and the **corniche**.
- **Exclusive and residential:** **Anfa**.
</Callout>

## Neighbourhoods at a glance

| Area | Atmosphere | Ideal for |
|---|---|---|
| **Maarif** | Commercial and lively: restaurants, cafés, shops, malls | Practical stays, shopping, business |
| **Gauthier** | Trendy: art galleries, concept stores, good tables | Young professionals, expats, foodies |
| **Racine** | Recent residential, quiet streets, close to top restaurants | Short-term apartment rentals |
| **Anfa** | Hills, villas, luxury gated residences | High-end stays, affluent families |
| **Ain Diab / corniche** | Beach clubs, restaurants, seafront luxury hotels | Beach, nights out, relaxing |
| **Downtown** | Art Deco and Art Nouveau around Mohammed V square | Architecture, stations, getting around |
| **Old medina** | More traditional, some riads and boutique hotels | A touch of authenticity (careful at night) |

## Tips for choosing

1. **Think of traffic.** Sleep near where you'll spend most of your time. The **tram** helps: see [tram and BRT](/en/tram-brt-casablanca-rabat-guide).
2. **Airport arrival:** see [from the airport to the city centre](/en/casablanca-airport-to-city-centre).
3. **Apartment or hotel?** For more than three nights or with family, a furnished apartment in Maarif, Racine or Anfa is often more practical.
4. **Boutique hotel or riad** in the medina for atmosphere, but check access and safety.
5. **2030 World Cup:** Casablanca will host matches; book early: see [World Cup 2030](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-host-cities).
