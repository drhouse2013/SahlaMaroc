To reach **Essaouira, Agadir, Chefchaouen** or towns the train doesn't serve, the **coach** is the simplest and cheapest option. Two companies dominate: **CTM** and **Supratours** (ONCF's partner bus network).

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- **CTM:** comfortable air-conditioned coaches, online purchase possible on some routes, and several comfort classes.
- **Supratours:** complements the rail network; online booking depends on the route, otherwise **at the counter** of the bus station.
- **Luggage:** a small fee (around **5 to 10 MAD** per hold bag according to some sources) is often added, with a tag.
- **Book ahead** in high season and for long trips.
</Callout>

## A few routes and indicative fares

| Route | Duration | Indicative fare |
|---|---|---|
| Marrakech → Essaouira | ≈ 3 h with a break | ≈ 100–130 MAD |
| Marrakech → Agadir | ≈ 3 h to 3 h 30 with a break | ≈ 115–120 MAD (CTM) |
| Essaouira → Casablanca | ≈ 6 to 7 h with stops | Varies: see ctm.ma |

Prices and timetables **change by season**. Check the carrier's website or app, or ask at the counter.

## How to book

1. **CTM:** on the carrier's website (ctm.ma) if you book ahead, or at an office at any time.
2. **Supratours:** tickets are mostly bought **at the bus station** (for example avenue Hassan II in Marrakech); ONCF offers some tickets online. See [trains and ONCF](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
3. **Arrive 30 minutes early:** tag and drop your bag, check the bay.

## Comfort and safety

- **Regular breaks** on long journeys, with shops and toilets.
- **Air conditioning:** bring a sweater, even in summer.
- **Valuables** with you, never in the hold.
- **Charger:** some classes offer sockets and Wi-Fi.
- **Night buses** exist, but arrive in daylight in an unfamiliar city if you can.

## Steps to take the coach

1. **Choose the route:** destination, day, time, and whether it departs at night.
2. **Check time and price** on the operator's site ([ctm.ma](https://www.ctm.ma) for CTM; [oncf.ma](https://www.oncf.ma) for the ONCF-linked network) or at the bus-station counter.
3. **Buy the ticket** online or at the counter; keep the receipt.
4. **Arrive 30 minutes early,** find the bay and departure number.
5. **Have your hold luggage tagged** and keep the stub.
6. **Keep papers, money and phone** with you, not in the hold.
7. **On arrival,** collect your bag and plan onward travel (taxi, tram): see [taxis and apps](/en/taxi-apps-morocco).

## Common mistakes

- Trusting fares from an old blog: prices vary by season.
- Arriving at the last minute in a busy bus station.
- Leaving phone or wallet in hold luggage.
- Taking a night coach for a first arrival in an unfamiliar city.
- Forgetting small change for luggage or rest stops.

## FAQ

**CTM or Supratours?** Both link the main cities; compare times and comfort. Supratours complements the train for towns it does not serve.

**Do children pay?** Child fares depend on the operator: not confirmed here, ask at the counter.

**Can I cancel or change?** Conditions are specific to each company and were not read here: ask before buying.

**When should I buy?** Earlier in high season and before holidays; on the day is often fine off-season for frequent lines.

## Three practical cases

**Marrakech to Essaouira or Agadir, by day.** The typical coach case: departure from the bus station, luggage in the hold, a break on the way. Arrive early, as late-morning departures fill up in season.

**An MRE with suitcases full of gifts.** Check the number and weight of bags accepted with the operator before buying: the rules are not detailed here. For several people with a lot of luggage, a grand taxi or a rental car ([guide](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide)) may cost the same and save time.

**Travelling alone at night.** Prefer a trip arriving in daytime, or tell your lodging your arrival time and book a taxi from the bus station. See [scams and safety](/en/morocco-scams-safety-travellers).

## Comfort tips

Bring water, snacks, a sweater for the air conditioning, a charger and an offline map. Stops are brief: do not wander off and note the vehicle's plate or number. If the coach is late, warn your lodging. Keep the luggage tag until you collect your suitcase.

## Extra questions

**Can I carry a bike or sports gear?** Not confirmed: ask the operator before buying.

**Is there a toilet on board?** Not always; scheduled stops serve that purpose.

**How do I retrieve my bag?** Keep the tag stub and show it on arrival.

**Are there night services?** Some lines run overnight, but arriving by day in an unfamiliar city is easier. Confirm schedules on the operator's site.

## Good to know

During school holidays, Eid and summer, coaches and bus stations are very busy: book early and allow a margin. Departures and arrivals use Moroccan time, which is GMT all year since September 2026: see [time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt). For a trip to the desert or mountains, an organised transfer can be simpler than a coach. Always check the departure bay: bays can change at short notice.

## Checklist

1. Time and price confirmed.
2. Ticket bought, receipt kept.
3. Arrive 30 minutes early.
4. Luggage tagged, valuables on you.
5. Onward trip planned.
