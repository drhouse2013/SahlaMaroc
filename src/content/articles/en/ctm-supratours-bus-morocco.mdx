To reach **Essaouira, Agadir, Chefchaouen** or towns the train doesn't serve, the **coach** is the simplest and cheapest option. Two companies dominate: **CTM** and **Supratours** (ONCF's partner bus network).

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- **CTM:** comfortable air-conditioned coaches, online purchase possible on some routes, and several comfort classes.
- **Supratours:** complements the rail network; online booking depends on the route, otherwise **at the counter** of the bus station.
- **Luggage:** a small fee (around **5 MAD** per hold bag according to some sources) is often added, with a tag.
- **Book ahead** in high season and for long trips.
</Callout>

## A few routes and indicative fares

| Route | Duration | Indicative fare |
|---|---|---|
| Marrakech → Essaouira | ≈ 3 h with a break | ≈ 100–130 MAD |
| Marrakech → Agadir | ≈ 3 h to 3 h 30 with a break | ≈ 95–105 MAD |
| Essaouira → Casablanca | ≈ 6 to 7 h with stops | ≈ 190–210 MAD |

Prices and timetables **change by season**. Check the carrier's website or app, or ask at the counter.

## How to book

1. **CTM:** on the carrier's website (ctm.ma) if you book ahead, or at an office at any time.
2. **Supratours:** tickets are mostly bought **at the bus station** (for example avenue Hassan II in Marrakech); ONCF offers some tickets online. See [trains and ONCF](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
3. **Arrive 30 minutes early:** tag and drop your bag, check the bay.

## Comfort and safety

- **Regular breaks** on long journeys, with shops and toilets.
- **Air conditioning:** bring a sweater, even in summer.
- **Valuables** with you, never in the hold.
- **Charger:** some classes offer sockets and Wi-Fi.
- **Night buses** exist, but arrive in daylight in an unfamiliar city if you can.
