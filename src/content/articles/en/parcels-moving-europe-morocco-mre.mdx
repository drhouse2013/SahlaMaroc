Bringing your belongings to Morocco, or sending a parcel there, follows different customs rules depending on whether it is a **permanent-return move**, a **postal parcel** or **luggage**. This guide complements the [travellers' customs guide](/en/morocco-customs-what-to-bring) and [permanent return](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Permanent return**: an allowance for personal effects and furniture exists; the press reports that the customs (ADII) circular for Marhaba 2026 raises it to **40,000 MAD** (from 30,000 MAD), to be confirmed in the text.
- It applies to **one move per family**, in principle at the same time as the return; two shipments within **six months** with a single inventory are possible, per the press.
- You need a **certificate of change of residence** (municipality of your last home or consulate) and a **detailed, dated, signed inventory**.
- **Postal parcels**: above a certain threshold, duties and VAT apply; threshold and rates were not officially confirmed.
</Callout>

## 1. Permanent-return move

According to press articles citing the ADII circular:

- the value of the goods (used furniture, clothing, appliances in one copy per category) is capped at **40,000 MAD** and **cannot be assigned to a single item**;
- the inventory must list **all** items at the first crossing;
- shipments must go through the **same customs office**;
- documents: certificate of change of residence and inventory.

A car follows another regime: see [importing your car](/en/import-car-permanent-return-morocco).

## 2. Postal parcels and shipments

- **Barid Al-Maghrib** handles delivery in Morocco for several carriers; the press cites a 30 kg maximum for some parcels.
- A third-party guide says that above **1,250 MAD** of value, duties of **2.5% to 40%** plus **20% VAT** may apply depending on the goods: check with the ADII.
- Describe contents and value precisely; avoid prohibited goods or those needing authorisation.

## 3. The other way: Morocco to Europe

Rules depend on the destination country (European customs and VAT). Ask that country's customs authority and your carrier. We have not verified these rules.

## 4. Practical tips

- Compare two or three removal quotes and ask what is included (insurance, clearance, delivery).
- Photograph your goods and keep invoices for valuables.
- [Sending money](/en/send-money-to-morocco) and [insurance](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border) cover other needs.

## What was not confirmed

- The exact text of the 2026 circular on the allowance.
- The treatment of private postal parcels (threshold, duties).
- The exact six-month deadline and its conditions.

## The permanent-return move, step by step

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 1" title="Confirm your status and return date">

The allowance is for people changing residence to settle in Morocco. Before shipping, read the [permanent return guide](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide) to set your dates.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 2" title="Obtain the certificate of change of residence">

Request it from the municipality of your last home or the consulate. It proves your departure from abroad; check its validity with customs.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 3" title="Draw up the inventory">

List **every** item, room by room, with quantity and condition (new or used). The inventory must be dated and signed. Photograph valuables and keep invoices.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 4" title="Choose a mover and compare quotes">

Ask for 2 or 3 written quotes, specifying what is included: packing, insurance, transport, clearance, delivery. Beware very low prices and cash payments without receipts.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 5" title="Clear customs and collect your goods">

Present inventory and certificate at the designated customs office. Keep receipts and the clearance record. Later shipments must go through the same office, per the press.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Sensitive items: check before packing

Large quantities of electronics, medicines, food, alcohol, weapons, drones or artworks may face restrictions, licences or duties. For the precise list, see the [customs guide for travellers](/en/morocco-customs-what-to-bring) and ADII. Never conceal an item: a false declaration leads to seizure and penalties.

## Postal parcels: good practice

Describe contents and value honestly, track the parcel, insure valuable shipments and prefer known carriers. Avoid informal "carriers": they make you responsible if the contents cause trouble.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving without a dated and signed inventory, or splitting shipments without planning it.
- Believing the allowance applies to each shipment: it covers one move per family.
- Mixing the car and furniture in the same procedure.
- Paying the mover in full in advance.
- Undervaluing or omitting valuable items.

<Checklist id="colis-demenagement-maroc" lang="en" title="Europe-Morocco move: my checklist">

- Permanent-return date set
- Certificate of change of residence obtained
- Detailed, dated and signed inventory
- 2 or 3 quotes compared, insurance specified
- Sensitive items checked (electronics, medicines, food)
- Destination customs office identified
- Receipts and clearance record kept

</Checklist>

## Sending from Morocco to Europe

Rules depend on the destination country (European customs and VAT). Ask the destination customs administration and the carrier before sending anything. We have not verified these rules; keep proof of the value and nature of the goods, and avoid items subject to licences such as medicines or food products in large quantities.
