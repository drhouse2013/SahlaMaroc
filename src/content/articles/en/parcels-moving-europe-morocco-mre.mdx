Bringing your belongings to Morocco, or sending a parcel there, follows different customs rules depending on whether it is a **permanent-return move**, a **postal parcel** or **luggage**. This guide complements the [travellers' customs guide](/en/morocco-customs-what-to-bring) and [permanent return](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Permanent return**: an allowance for personal effects and furniture exists; the press reports that the customs (ADII) circular for Marhaba 2026 raises it to **40,000 MAD** (from 30,000 MAD), to be confirmed in the text.
- It applies to **one move per family**, in principle at the same time as the return; two shipments within **six months** with a single inventory are possible, per the press.
- You need a **certificate of change of residence** (municipality of your last home or consulate) and a **detailed, dated, signed inventory**.
- **Postal parcels**: above a certain threshold, duties and VAT apply; threshold and rates were not officially confirmed.
</Callout>

## 1. Permanent-return move

According to press articles citing the ADII circular:

- the value of the goods (used furniture, clothing, appliances in one copy per category) is capped at **40,000 MAD** and **cannot be assigned to a single item**;
- the inventory must list **all** items at the first crossing;
- shipments must go through the **same customs office**;
- documents: certificate of change of residence and inventory.

A car follows another regime: see [importing your car](/en/import-car-permanent-return-morocco).

## 2. Postal parcels and shipments

- **Barid Al-Maghrib** handles delivery in Morocco for several carriers; the press cites a 30 kg maximum for some parcels.
- A third-party guide says that above **1,250 MAD** of value, duties of **2.5% to 40%** plus **20% VAT** may apply depending on the goods: check with the ADII.
- Describe contents and value precisely; avoid prohibited goods or those needing authorisation.

## 3. The other way: Morocco to Europe

Rules depend on the destination country (European customs and VAT). Ask that country's customs authority and your carrier. We have not verified these rules.

## 4. Practical tips

- Compare two or three removal quotes and ask what is included (insurance, clearance, delivery).
- Photograph your goods and keep invoices for valuables.
- [Sending money](/en/send-money-to-morocco) and [insurance](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border) cover other needs.

## What was not confirmed

- The exact text of the 2026 circular on the allowance.
- The treatment of private postal parcels (threshold, duties).
- The exact six-month deadline and its conditions.
