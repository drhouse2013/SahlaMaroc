This guide complements our [Taghazout and Agadir surf guide](/en/taghazout-agadir-surf-guide), which covers spots, season and lessons. Here we only cover **where to sleep**: the village, neighbouring villages, Agadir or further afield, and the type of lodging.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Surf atmosphere, no car:** the **village of Taghazout**, where everything is on foot.
- **A base less centred on the village:** **Tamraght** (or Aourir), a few minutes by car or taxi.
- **Long beach, restaurants, services and flights:** **Agadir**, about 20 km south.
- **Long waves and an isolated village:** **Imsouane**, with a car.
</Callout>

## Where is Taghazout?

Taghazout is a fishing village on the Atlantic coast, about **20 km north of Agadir** via the N1 (20.5 km according to [Wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taghazout)). It lies halfway between Agadir and Cap Ghir. A new seaside resort, **Taghazout Bay**, has been developed alongside; the [SMIT](https://smit.gov.ma/taghazout-bay-en/) (Moroccan Society for Tourism Engineering) presents the project with a capacity of 12,376 beds over 615 ha; the actual progress and number of beds open are to be confirmed.

## Taghazout village

- **Best for:** surfers, travellers without a car, short stays.
- **Pros:** cafés, surf schools and gear rental close by; relaxed atmosphere; Anchor Point is about 3 km north of the village (the [ONMT](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/agadir-taghazout/beach) lists it among the favourite spots of experienced surfers).
- **Limits:** Wikivoyage notes busy line-ups over the Christmas break, and the village is developing fast. For noise, ask whether the room faces the road, a terrace or a café, and prefer an inner-facing room if you sleep lightly.

## Tamraght and Aourir

- **Best for:** travellers who want a base less centred on the village, longer stays.
- **Pros:** Tamraght is home to Devil's Rock, a beginners' spot (see the surf guide). According to Wikivoyage, Taghazout is growing into Tamraght and Aourir.
- **Limits:** you will often need a taxi or a car for dinner or to get to Taghazout.

## Imsouane

Imsouane is a coastal town between Agadir and Essaouira. Its "La Bay" spot is described as a long wave suitable for beginners and longboarders, while "La Cathédrale" is more exposed and powerful ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imsouane)). Public transport is limited; a car or taxi is the norm. It is therefore a choice for a dedicated stay, not something to combine easily with Agadir or Taghazout. Check the full journey from the airport before booking.

## Agadir: if you want big-city comfort

Further away but close to the airport, with supermarkets, restaurants and pharmacies. See [where to stay in Agadir](/en/where-to-stay-in-agadir). Handy for the start or end of a trip, or a mixed beach-and-surf stay.

## Which type of lodging?

| Type | Best for | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Hostel** | Tight budget, solo | Dorm bed, noise, lockers |
| **Surf camp** | Beginners, groups | Included: lessons, gear, meals, transfers |
| **Apartment or house** | Long stays, groups | Wi-Fi, local rules, see [short-term rentals](/en/short-term-rental-morocco-rules) |
| **Seafront hotel or riad** | Couples, comfort | Beach access, distance from village |

Surf camps often bundle several things: compare what is included (lodging, lessons, meals, transport). According to Wikivoyage, some apartments or houses are also offered on the central square for longer stays: book with a clear contract and proof of payment.

## Car or no car?

- **Without a car:** stay in Taghazout or Tamraght. Wikivoyage mentions local buses (lines 32 and 33) from Agadir, about 45 minutes, and taxis. Check schedules and lines locally.
- **With a car:** more flexibility (Imsouane, Paradise Valley, spots). See the [car rental guide](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide) and avoid night driving in rural areas (France Diplomatie).
- **Airport:** Agadir Al Massira (AGA) is 25 km from Agadir according to [ONDA](https://www.onda.ma/Je-suis-Passager/Guide-du-voyageur/R%C3%A9seau-a%C3%A9roportuaire); see the [airports guide](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre).

## Season

Surfing in Taghazout runs roughly from September to April according to Wikipedia; periods are detailed in our [surf guide](/en/taghazout-agadir-surf-guide). Book earlier for winter and the end-of-year holidays, when spots are very busy.
