Getting around during the 2030 World Cup means separating what already exists (Al Boraq, trams, current airports) from what is only announced (the high-speed line to Marrakech, RER, new motorways, airport expansions). This guide, **valid as of 9 October 2026**, gives each project's status and the dates shown by sources. It complements the [general host cities guide](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-host-cities).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
Today the Al Boraq high-speed train links Tangier, Rabat and Casablanca (confirmed). The Kenitra-Marrakech extension is under construction, with commissioning announced for late 2029. Airport expansions, the Casablanca RER and several motorways are announced, not all with firm dates. As far as we could find, no official match-day shuttle plan has been published.
</Callout>

## Legend

**(confirmed)**: in service or official launch established. **(announced)**: stated by ONCF, ONDA, the government or the transport minister; schedule may change. **(provisional)**: figure or date from press reports, to be reconfirmed. **(hypothesis)**: not announced.

## Airports

- **Traffic:** 36.3 million passengers in 2025, per ONDA figures relayed by the press (confirmed).
- **Programme:** ONDA launched a 2026-2030 plan of about MAD 38 billion aiming to double capacity, with a target of 80 million passengers in 2030 (announced). It includes a new hub terminal and a new runway in Casablanca.
- **Marrakech, Agadir, Tangier, Fes:** the African Development Bank announced on 30 September 2026 the signing of a EUR 270 million loan to ONDA, with 2030 capacity targets of 14 million passengers a year in Marrakech, 5 million in Agadir, 3.6 million in Tangier and 3 million in Fes (announced). Contracts for the Marrakech-Menara and Agadir-Al Massira extensions were awarded in August 2025.
- To get from the airport to the city centre, see our guide to [Morocco's airports and city transfers](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre).

## Trains

### Al Boraq (existing)

The high-speed line between Tangier, Rabat and Casablanca is in service (confirmed). Timetables, tickets and tips: [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).

### Kenitra-Marrakech high-speed line (under construction)

Works were officially launched on 24 April 2025 (confirmed). The line is over 400 km (390 to 430 km depending on the article) with speeds up to 320 km/h. Commissioning is announced for **late 2029**: September 2029 according to the transport minister before the House of Representatives (May 2026), November 2029 in the ONCF schedule published in 2025 (announced). Journey times quoted in the press vary (for example Casablanca-Marrakech at roughly 1 h to 1 h 15): **reconfirm with ONCF**.

ONCF is also reorganising operations during the works: a new timetable has applied since 14 September 2026 and the Casablanca-Facultes and Ennassim stations are to close progressively (announced, per Le Brief, 4 September 2026). Always check the day's timetable on the ONCF website.

### Casablanca RER and other cities (announced)

ONCF has presented RER projects in Casablanca-Settat, Rabat-Sale-Kenitra and Marrakech-Safi. For Casablanca, a line is planned between Benslimane (the stadium) and Mohammed V airport, with operations hoped for around 2030 (announced, Médias24, June 2025). In late September 2026, however, the press said frequencies and start dates were still to be confirmed (provisional). Do not build a match-day journey on the RER until a timetable is published.

## Roads and motorways

- **Access to Benslimane:** the state is funding a programme of about MAD 5 billion for roads around the stadium (64 km of new roads, interchanges, a continental motorway), per Médias24 (May 2026) (announced).
- **Rabat-Casablanca (continental) motorway:** about 59 km via Benslimane, estimated at MAD 6.5 billion; launch described as imminent in May 2025 (announced). An October 2026 article notes that some sections may not be ready for the tournament (provisional).
- **Driving:** rental and rules in the [car rental guide](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide).

## Trams, buses, taxis, shuttles

- Casablanca's and Rabat-Sale's tram and BRT already exist: [tram and BRT guide](/en/tram-brt-casablanca-rabat-guide).
- Taxis, ride-hailing and intercity coaches: [taxi apps](/en/taxi-apps-morocco) and [CTM and Supratours buses](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco).
- **Match shuttles:** we found no published official plan. Treat them as a hypothesis until FIFA or the authorities communicate.

## Practical tips

1. Book trains and flights late, once the match schedule is known; FIFA has not published it yet. See [trip planning](/en/plan-trip-world-cup-2030-morocco).
2. For stadiums, check the [status of each stadium](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-stadiums-status) before choosing where to sleep: [where to stay during the World Cup](/en/where-to-stay-world-cup-2030-morocco).
3. Prices and timetables change: check at booking.
