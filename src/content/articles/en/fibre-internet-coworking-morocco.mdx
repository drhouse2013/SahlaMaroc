Working remotely from Morocco is perfectly feasible, provided you **secure your connection**. Fibre is spreading fast in big cities, 5G has launched and coworking spaces are multiplying. Here's how to set up something reliable.

<Callout type="tip" title="The setup that avoids headaches">
- **Home fibre** for video calls and big transfers.
- **4G/5G mobile plan** as backup: see [SIM and eSIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).
- **Coworking** on big call days, or a café with sockets.
- **Ask for a speed test** from your host before booking.
</Callout>

## Fibre in 2026

Fibre (FTTH) generally costs **about 249 to 1,000 MAD a month** depending on the operator, speed and options. The three operators:

| Operator | Indicative prices | Speeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Maroc Telecom (IAM)** | From about **400 MAD/month** (100 Mb/s); about 500 MAD for 200 Mb/s; about 1,000 MAD for 1 Gb/s | Up to 1 Gb/s |
| **Orange Maroc** | From about **249 MAD/month** | Up to 500 Mb/s |
| **inwi** | From about **299 MAD/month** | Up to 1 Gb/s |

Prices and speeds change (Maroc Telecom doubled its fibre subscribers' speeds in 2025 without changing prices). **Test your address's eligibility** before signing: coverage is denser in Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech and Tangier.

## 4G, 5G and a backup connection

5G has been launched by all three operators since November 2025 in several cities. A **mobile plan** with several dozen GB or a **4G modem** is a good safety net: see plan prices in our [SIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).

## Coworking spaces and cafés

Big cities have many coworking spaces, with day, week or month passes. Choose on:

- **Connection stability** (test on a trial day if possible);
- **Backup power** (power cuts can happen);
- **Atmosphere** and proximity to your accommodation;
- **Opening hours**, useful for calls with Europe: Morocco is on GMT all year, so 1 hour behind Paris in winter and 2 hours in summer: see [time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt).

## Which city to work from?

Casablanca for reliability, Marrakech for community, Rabat for calm, Taghazout for surf, Essaouira for focus: see [best cities for digital nomads](/en/best-cities-digital-nomads-morocco) and [cost of living](/en/cost-of-living-morocco).

## Remote worker checklist

<Checklist id="remote-work" lang="en" title="Remote work in Morocco: my checklist">

- Speed test obtained from the host
- Fibre or backup 4G/5G box planned
- Local SIM or backup eSIM
- Power bank / surge-protected power strip
- Coworking or café identified for big days
- Noise-cancelling headset for video calls
- Visa and length of stay checked: [visa and 90 days](/en/morocco-visa-90-day-rule)

</Checklist>

<Callout type="info" title="Indicative">
Offers and prices change. Check your address coverage and terms on the operators' websites.
