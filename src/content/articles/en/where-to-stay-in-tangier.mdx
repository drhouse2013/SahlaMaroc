Tangier, facing Spain, is Morocco's gateway: port, beaches on two seas, a steep medina and the high-speed train to Rabat and Casablanca. It's a city of very different neighbourhoods. Here's how to choose.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Charm and heritage:** **medina and Kasbah** (riads and guest houses).
- **Calm:** **Marshan**, just west of the Kasbah.
- **Beach and pool (families):** **Malabata**.
- **Modern, nightlife, restaurants:** **new town** or **Corniche**.
</Callout>

## The neighbourhoods

| Area | Atmosphere | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Medina** | Lanes, close to the Kasbah, sea, beaches and port | Mostly guest houses in riads and small hotels |
| **Kasbah** | Heights, strait views, riads of varying standard, apartments | The heart of the charm, walkable |
| **Marshan** | Calm, residential | A few places to stay, close to the Kasbah |
| **Malabata** | Modern seaside zone with luxury hotels | Ideal for beach and pool |
| **Corniche** | Facing the Mediterranean and the marina, modern establishments | Practical, nice views |
| **New town** | Wide streets, modern buildings, cosmopolitan feel | Contemporary hotels, restaurants, nights out |

## Tips

1. **Arriving by port or airport:** see [Morocco's airports](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre) and [ferry to Morocco](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide).
2. **Tangier-Ville station:** the **Al Boraq** train reaches Rabat in about 1 h 20 and Casablanca in about 2 h 10: [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
3. **In the medina:** streets are steep; ask your accommodation to meet you.
4. **Day trip:** [Chefchaouen from Tangier](/en/chefchaouen-day-trip-from-fes-tangier) is about 2 h 15 to 2 h 30 by bus.
5. **2030 World Cup:** Tangier is among the proposed host cities (FIFA's final list not yet published): [host cities](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-host-cities).

## A night before or after the ferry

If you arrive at night or leave early by ferry, consider a night in Tangier rather than at the port. For the return to Europe, see [Marhaba](/en/operation-marhaba-guide) and [motorway from Tanger Med](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route).
