Marrakech is an ideal base: in a day you can be in the Atlas mountains, at the foot of waterfalls or in a stone desert. But these four outings differ in time, cost and target audience. This guide compares them so you can choose by time, energy, season and what you want to see, without booking blind.

<Callout type="tip" title="The right pick in one sentence">
- **Short on time (half a day or an evening):** **Agafay**, a stone desert close to Marrakech.
- **Nature and coolness:** **Ourika**, green valley and river.
- **Hiking and Berber villages:** **Imlil**, gateway to Toubkal.
- **The best "waterfall" day:** **Ouzoud**, the farthest.
</Callout>

## Comparison

| Trip | Journey from Marrakech | Pace | Best for | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Agafay desert** | The shortest | Half day to evening | Sunset, dinner, camel, quad | Easy |
| **Ourika valley** | Medium | Day | River, Setti Fatma falls, riverside restaurants | Easy to moderate (rocks) |
| **Imlil and High Atlas** | Medium | Day or several days | Walking, villages, Toubkal start | Moderate |
| **Ouzoud falls** | The longest (about 150 km) | Full day | Big falls, walk, monkeys | Easy to moderate |

Prices depend on season, group or private, and what is included (lunch, entry, guide, activity). We do not fix them: ask for a written quote. Group tours are usually cheaper; private gives flexibility.

## Agafay desert

A **stony** desert within reach of the city, with views of the Atlas. It is not the Sahara, but it is perfect for sunset, dinner under the stars or an activity (camel, quad). Camps vary in standard: some offer dinner only, others tents and a pool. Ask what is included and the return time. For real sand dunes, see [Marrakech → Merzouga](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour).

## Ourika valley

A green valley with a **river**, villages and a path to the **Setti Fatma falls**. Very popular in summer for its coolness. The rocky sections are slippery: good shoes are essential. Avoid going after heavy rain, when the river can rise fast.

## Imlil and the High Atlas

Starting village for **Toubkal**, North Africa's highest peak. Even without climbing, walking between Berber villages is superb. For the summit trek, see [climbing Toubkal in 2 days](/en/toubkal-hike-2-days-imlil). For a simple day out, choose a local guide who knows the trails.

## Ouzoud falls

The Ouzoud falls, in Azilal province, are about **110 m** high in several tiers and drop into the El Abid river gorge. They are about 150 km from Marrakech: allow **a full day**. On site: paths around the falls, restaurants with views, boat rides depending on conditions and curious **macaques** (do not feed them, protect your belongings). The flow varies by season: check before you go.

## How to choose

- **You have half a day:** Agafay.
- **You want coolness in summer:** Ourika.
- **You enjoy walking:** Imlil.
- **You like spectacular scenery and accept the drive:** Ouzoud.
- **Travelling with young children:** Agafay or Ourika (short outings), adapt to age.

## Best time

In spring the falls are often fuller and the mountains greener. Summer is hot in Marrakech: the Atlas is cooler. In winter, clear days are possible but the mountains can be cold, even snowy at altitude. Check the forecast before leaving.

## What to pack

- **Water**, a hat and sunscreen
- **Walking shoes** (rocks at Ourika, paths at Ouzoud and Imlil)
- A warm layer: the Atlas is cooler than Marrakech
- **Cash** for meals, tips and small purchases: see [tipping](/en/tipping-in-morocco)
- Swimsuit and towel if you plan to swim

## Common mistakes

1. **Leaving late**: heat and crowds arrive quickly.
2. **Combining two trips in one day**: journeys and fatigue spoil both.
3. **Counting on bank cards** in small restaurants: bring cash.
4. **Unsuitable shoes** on wet rocks.
5. **Feeding the monkeys** or leaving belongings unattended.
6. **Booking without reading the inclusions**: lunch, entry fees, guide and return time should be in writing.
