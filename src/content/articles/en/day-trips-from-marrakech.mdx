Marrakech is an ideal base: in a single day you can be in the Atlas mountains, at the foot of waterfalls or in a stone desert. Here are the four most popular day trips, so you can choose by time, budget and mood.

<Callout type="tip" title="The right pick in one sentence">
- **Short on time (half a day):** **Agafay**, about 40 minutes away.
- **Nature and coolness in summer:** **Ourika**, about 1 hour.
- **Hiking and Berber villages:** **Imlil**, about 1 h 30.
- **The best "waterfall" day:** **Ouzoud**, 2 h 30 to 3 h by road.
</Callout>

## Comparison

**Indicative prices reported in 2026**, varying by season and operator. Group trips are cheapest; private gives flexibility.

## Agafay desert

A **stony** desert close to the city, with views of the Atlas. It isn't the Sahara, but it's perfect for a sunset, dinner under the stars or an activity (quad, camel). For a true dune desert, see [Marrakech → Merzouga](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour).

## Ourika valley

A green valley with a **river**, villages and the **Setti Fatma waterfalls**, reached on foot. Very popular in summer for its coolness. Wear good shoes for the rocky sections.

## Imlil and the High Atlas

The starting village for **Toubkal**, North Africa's highest peak. Even without climbing, walking between Berber villages is superb. For the ascent: see [Toubkal in 2 days](/en/toubkal-hike-2-days-imlil).

## Ouzoud waterfalls

The Ouzoud falls are the highest in the country. Plan **a full day**: about 2 h 30 to 3 h of driving each way. On site: paths around the falls, restaurants with a view and curious **macaques** (don't feed them, protect your belongings).
