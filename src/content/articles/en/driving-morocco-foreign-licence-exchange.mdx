Renting a car, driving your own or settling in: either way, the **driving licence** question comes up. The rules are simple for short stays, stricter for residents.

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- **Tourist or short stay:** your **valid foreign licence** is enough, if it is written in the **Latin alphabet**, for **one year at most** from the start of your stay.
- **Licence not in the Latin alphabet** (or countries outside agreements): plan for an **international driving permit** to accompany the national licence.
- **Settled resident:** at the end of that one-year period you must **exchange the licence** for a Moroccan one or retake the tests.
- **Car rental:** rental firms and insurers may require a **minimum licence age**. See [renting a car in Morocco](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide).
</Callout>

## Short stay: tourist or visiting MRE

- Drive with your **valid national licence**, with a **legible photo**, written in Latin characters.
- Keep the licence **with you at all times**, along with the registration document and vehicle insurance. If you come with your own car, see the [180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule) and [car insurance](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border).
- If your licence is not in the Latin alphabet, an **international permit** (official translation) is required as well.

## Settling in: exchanging your licence

Within 12 months of arrival, a resident who wants to keep driving must:

1. **File an exchange request** for their foreign licence against a Moroccan one (depending on agreements between countries), or
2. **Take the Moroccan driving tests** if exchange isn't possible.

A **60-day temporary permit** is generally issued once the file is judged complete. Usual documents: foreign licence and translation if needed, ID or residence permit, medical certificate, photos. The exact list depends on the regional equipment directorate: ask for it.

## To avoid

- **Driving with an expired** or illegible licence.
- **Exceeding the one-year period** without exchange.
- **Ignoring local rules:** speed limits, speed cameras, seat belts, phone use, frequent checks. See also [taxis and apps](/en/taxi-apps-morocco) for getting around cities without a car.
- **Lending your foreign car** to a Moroccan resident.

## Checklist

<Checklist id="driving-licence" lang="en" title="Driving licence: my checklist">

- Valid, legible licence in the Latin alphabet
- International permit if needed
- Registration document and insurance certificate valid in Morocco
- Arrival date noted (start of the one-year period)
- Exchange request filed before the end of 12 months (residents)
- 60-day temporary permit kept while waiting for the Moroccan licence

</Checklist>

<Callout type="info" title="Check the source">
Exchange agreements differ by country. Confirm with the regional directorate of the Ministry of Equipment and Transport and with your rental company. This guide is general information.
</Callout>
