When a foreign pension is paid in Morocco, the fund must regularly check that you are alive: this is the **life certificate** (proof of existence). Missing it can **suspend** the pension. The procedure **depends on your fund**; this guide gives the verified common points and sends you to your fund for the rest.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Follow the deadline written on your fund's letter or e-mail**: the official sources consulted give one month, two months or 30 days depending on the body.
- **Depending on your fund**, the certificate is completed by a local authority (for example the town hall), the consulate, a biometric app or an automatic data exchange.
- **Keep proof of sending** and tell the fund about any change of address.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="What is changing (updated 16 Oct 2026)">
The life certificate in this guide (proof of existence for a foreign pension fund) is a different document: your fund still requires it. The abolition concerns the Moroccan administrative life certificate.

Since law 55.19 (simplification of procedures) took effect, announced by the Interior Ministry in April 2021 alongside the Idarati portal, Moroccan administrations should no longer require certain certificates (single status, marriage, non-divorce, polygamy, kinship, widowhood, the Moroccan administrative life certificate...) and may accept a sworn declaration instead. This is not a 2026 novelty, and the full official list of the 22 documents is not public in the sources we checked.
</Callout>

## Why and how often

The fund checks the beneficiary is still alive before continuing to pay. For French funds, Service-Public says proof of existence may be requested **every year**, whatever your nationality, and that one certificate is enough for all French funds. Funds in other countries set their own rhythm.

## Ways to validate it (verified)

| Route | What the sources say | Depending on your fund |
|---|---|---|
| Local authority | The town hall, police station or another competent local service completes the form (Assurance retraite, CLEISS) | Confirm on the letter you received |
| Consulate | Certificate of existence endorsed by the consular service (Service-Public) | Confirm; see [consulate and passport](/en/renew-moroccan-passport-id-abroad) |
| Biometric app | "Mon certificat de vie": QR code by post or e-mail, 4 days to complete (Service-Public) | Only for the funds and retirees concerned |
| Online / post | Certificate downloaded from your personal account, completed and returned; by post if no internet access | Depending on your fund |

## Deadlines: do not rely on one figure

The official sources give different deadlines: **one month** after the notification e-mail (Service-Public), **two months** (Assurance retraite online service), **30 days** (Belgian Federal Pension Service). Apply the **shortest** one and the one on your letter. After the deadline, payment may be suspended.

## Moroccan pension paid to a resident of France

For Moroccan pensions, CLEISS states that the basic pension is justified in March to the Centre de données sociales (Casablanca) with the Moroccan social security number, and the supplementary pension at the start of each quarter, with proof of residence at the start of the year. Check with your fund, as these instructions can change.

## Living in Morocco with a foreign pension: step by step

1. **Identify your fund and its letter**: an email or letter gives the deadline and the validation method. Do not rely on a generic guide.
2. **Choose the allowed route**: biometric app if your fund offers it, otherwise a form to be completed by the authorised authority.
3. **In Morocco, the local authority** is most often the district office or administrative annex of your place of residence, or the municipal certificates service. Bring your national ID or residence card, passport and the pre-filled form, to be signed on the spot. The consulate of the fund's country generally completes the certificate only if that is impossible locally or if the fund instructs it (reply from the French consulate in Casablanca, 2021, reported by the press): confirm for your post.
4. **Photograph the signed document** before posting it, keep proof of dispatch (registered mail or online acknowledgement).
5. **Note the next due date**: some funds ask every year, others every two years or on fixed dates.

## Moroccan pension paid to an MRE abroad

A Moroccan retiree living in Europe must also prove they are alive to the CNSS and the supplementary pension funds. CLEISS relayed in 2015 an annual CNSS life-check campaign: a letter in March, a reminder in May, possible suspension at the end of June without a certificate, a form stamped by the town hall to be returned to the Social Data Centre in Casablanca. Exact dates change each year: rely on the current year's letter.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for the reminder**: suspension can come before you realise the first letter was lost.
- **Not reporting a change of address** (especially a permanent return to Morocco): mail goes to the old address.
- **Getting it validated by an authority the fund does not recognise**: a shopkeeper's or third party's stamp is worthless.
- **Confusing the fund's life certificate** with the Moroccan administrative life certificate.
- **Forgetting the Moroccan social security number** on the CNSS form.
- **Assuming a longer deadline** than the one in the letter.

## Checklist

<Checklist id="certificat-vie" lang="en" title="Life certificate: my checklist">

- Fund's letter or email read, deadline noted
- Allowed validation route identified (local authority, consulate, app)
- ID and passport ready, form filled without signing in advance
- Social security or pension number entered
- Copy of the signed document and proof of dispatch kept
- Address up to date with every fund
- Next due date added to my calendar

</Checklist>

## If you receive nothing or moved

1. Contact the fund **in writing** and report the change of address or country.
2. Ask for a duplicate rather than waiting for suspension.
3. Keep a copy of the returned document and proof of sending.
4. If a consulate must validate it, check hours and documents on its official site.
