Good news first: citizens of the **EU, UK, US, Canada, Australia** and many other countries can enter Morocco **without a visa for up to 90 days**. Here's what that means in practice, and what to do if you want to stay longer.

<Callout type="warning" title="Always check your own nationality">
Rules depend on your passport and can change. Check the website of the Moroccan consulate in your country before you book. This guide is general information, not legal advice.
</Callout>

## Entering Morocco

- **Passport:** it must be valid for your whole stay. Six months of validity is the safe standard airlines expect.
- **Entry stamp:** your 90 days start on the date stamped at the border. Keep a photo of the page.
- **Proof of onward travel and accommodation:** rarely checked, but have them on your phone.

Some nationalities need a visa or an **e-Visa**, applied for online before travel. Note that an e-Visa can't be converted into a residence permit later, so a long stay must be planned before you arrive.

## Is there a digital nomad visa?

**Not at the moment.** As of October 2026, Morocco has no dedicated digital nomad visa. Remote workers usually stay on the 90-day visa-free entry or apply for a residence permit if they settle for the long term.

## Staying more than 90 days

- **Residence permit (*carte de séjour*):** the formal route for long stays. You apply in Morocco at the police (Sûreté nationale) with a reason for residing, such as work, family, studies or sufficient resources, plus proof of address. Requirements vary by city and profile, so ask locally.
- **Leaving and re-entering:** some long-stay visitors leave the country before day 90 and come back. Re-entry is always at the border officer's discretion, so don't build a long-term plan on it.
- **Overstaying** can lead to a fine and problems when you leave. Avoid it.

## Practical tips

1. Count your days from the entry stamp, not from your flight booking.
2. Keep digital copies of your passport, insurance and rental contract.
3. For long stays, get health insurance that covers you abroad.
