Six Moroccan stadiums are proposed for the 2030 World Cup, but they are not all at the same stage, and FIFA has not yet published the final list of venues or the match allocation. This guide takes stock **as of 9 October 2026** and explains the status of each stadium. It complements the [general host cities guide](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-host-cities).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
Morocco proposes six stadiums: Benslimane (near Casablanca), Rabat, Tangier, Marrakech, Agadir and Fes. Only the Rabat complex is already inaugurated in its final form. The Grand Stade Hassan II is under construction. Marrakech, Agadir and Fes are entering a second phase of works. FIFA has not yet confirmed the final list or assigned matches.
</Callout>

## Legend used in this guide

- **(confirmed)**: established fact (inauguration, published official decision).
- **(announced)**: stated by Moroccan authorities, the FRMF or ONCF, but not validated by FIFA or likely to change.
- **(provisional)**: figure or schedule taken from press reports, sometimes old; to be reconfirmed.
- **(hypothesis)**: scenario discussed, nothing official.

## What FIFA has and has not decided

- Morocco, Spain and Portugal were named hosts of the 2030 edition, with three centenary matches in Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay (confirmed; FIFA article of 11 December 2024: [inside.fifa.com](https://inside.fifa.com/news/world-cup-2030-morocco-spain-portugal-argentina-paraguay-uruguay-reactions)). The opening match is due to be played in Montevideo, at the Centenario stadium.
- As far as we could find, the final list of host stadiums and the split of matches between countries are **not published** (press still wrote in September 2026 that FIFA had not finalised the list of cities).
- The venue of the final is undecided: see below.

These figures come from the Moroccan business press (Médias24, La Vie éco, Hespress, Le Brief, between December 2024 and September 2026). Sources sometimes give slightly different capacities; **confirm with the FRMF or FIFA** before buying tickets or committing to a trip.

### Grand Stade Hassan II, Benslimane

This is the flagship project. According to Médias24 (25 May 2026), construction is about 40% complete, with a capacity of 115,000 seats and delivery in December 2027 (announced). Earthworks took place in 2024-2025 and several lots (main structure, roof and facade, electrical) were awarded in 2025 and 2026. The state is also funding a road programme around the site.

The stadium is in Benslimane province, **outside Casablanca**. The distance quoted varies by source (about 38 km per Médias24, about 60 km in other reports): do not assume a quick trip without seeing the official access plan. See the [transport guide](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-transport).

### Rabat

The Prince Moulay Abdellah complex was inaugurated on 4 September 2025 (confirmed) with 68,700 seats. Press reports say it was designed to meet FIFA requirements up to the semi-finals (announced). It is currently the most advanced venue in the bid.

### Tangier, Marrakech, Agadir, Fes

These four stadiums were first renovated for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations, and are due to go through a second phase (more capacity, roofs, facilities). In June 2026 the ANEP, the agency running the sports projects, launched its first tenders for Agadir and Marrakech, with Fes to follow (announced, per Médias24). Completion dates are not all published; 2028 is cited for Agadir and Marrakech (provisional).

## The final: still undecided

On 10 September 2026, FRMF president Fouzi Lekjaa said the final would be played at the Grand Stade Hassan II. FIFA replied that a decision would be taken "in due course", and Spain also claims the final. **There is no official allocation** to date: any travel plan built around the final is a hypothesis.
