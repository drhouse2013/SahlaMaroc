Morocco's official time is now **GMT (UTC+0), all year round**. The change took effect on **Sunday 20 September 2026**, when clocks went back one hour at 2 am. If you read older guides that say "GMT+1", they are out of date.

<Callout type="tip" title="The short version">
- **Morocco = GMT all year.** No summer time, and **no more switch for Ramadan**.
- Versus **France, Spain, Germany, Italy:** Morocco is **1 hour behind in winter** and **2 hours behind in summer**.
- Versus the **UK:** same time in winter, **1 hour behind** in summer.
- Your phone updates by itself. Double-check any alarm, ferry or train time you noted before the change.
</Callout>

## What changed, exactly?

- Since 2018, Morocco had kept a permanent **GMT+1**, apart from a temporary return to GMT during Ramadan.
- **Decree no. 2.26.530**, published in the *Bulletin officiel* of 29 June 2026, ends that regime and repeals the 2018 decree that created it.
- The night of **19 to 20 September 2026**, clocks went back by 60 minutes. Since then, the country keeps one time all year unless a new decision is taken.

## Time differences with Morocco

| Country / region | Winter (late Oct – late Mar) | Summer (late Mar – late Oct) |
|---|---|---|
| France, Spain, Germany, Italy, Netherlands | Morocco 1 h **behind** | Morocco 2 h **behind** |
| United Kingdom, Ireland | Same time | Morocco 1 h **behind** |
| Portugal | Same time | Morocco 1 h **behind** |
| US East Coast | Morocco 5 h **ahead** | Morocco 4 h **ahead** |
| Gulf states (e.g. UAE) | Morocco 4 h **behind** | Morocco 4 h **behind** |

The European columns move because Europe changes its clocks twice a year while Morocco no longer does.

## What it means for your trip

- **Flights and ferries** always show **local time** at each end. Check this when you plan a connection or a pick-up, especially in summer when the gap with Spain and France is 2 hours.
- **Ramadan 2027** (expected around 8 February to 9 March, depending on the moon) will no longer shift clocks. Shop and restaurant hours still change during the month. See our [public holidays and Ramadan guide](/en/morocco-public-holidays-ramadan-2027).
- **Sunrise and sunset** are about one hour earlier on the clock than in recent years. Plan sunrise dune climbs and evening walks accordingly, and see the [Sahara tour guide](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour).
- **Calls with Europe:** schedule a meeting in "Paris time" carefully. A 10:00 call in Paris is **9:00 in Morocco in winter and 8:00 in summer**.

## For remote workers and MREs

Working hours overlap with Europe a bit less in summer. If you serve a European team, agree on a fixed reference time zone (for example CET/CEST) and use a calendar that shows both. More in our [guide for digital nomads](/en/best-cities-digital-nomads-morocco).

If you drive back with the summer return, remember that your arrival time at the port is in Moroccan time, which is behind Spanish time. See the [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide) and [Operation Marhaba](/en/operation-marhaba-guide).
