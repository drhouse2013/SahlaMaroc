Beyond 90 days, or as soon as you settle, you need a **residence title**. The procedure is feasible, but it requires a complete file and patience. This guide separates what the law says, which we read, from the practice of foreigners' offices, reported by secondary sources and to be confirmed locally.

<Callout type="tip" title="At a glance">
- **Two titles** exist in law 02-03: the **registration card** (1 to 10 years, renewable) and the **residence card** (art. 5, 10, 16).
- The file is submitted **in Morocco**, to the competent services of your place of residence (foreigners' office of the police prefecture or station).
- The card must be presentable **within 48 hours** (art. 8); the **receipt** of the application temporarily replaces it.
- No "digital nomad" visa to our knowledge: see the [visa and 90-day guide](/en/morocco-visa-90-day-rule).
</Callout>

## What the law says (law 02-03, text read in the Bulletin officiel)

| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| **5** | Two residence titles: registration card and residence card |
| **6** | A foreigner over 18 staying in Morocco must hold one of the two |
| **8** | Application for a renewable card; the card must be presented within 48 hours; the receipt temporarily replaces it |
| **9** | Exemption for stays of up to 90 days with a regular travel document |
| **10** | The registration card authorises a stay of 1 to 10 years maximum, renewable, depending on the reasons given |
| **11** | If the card is refused or withdrawn, the foreigner must leave within 15 days of notification |
| **13** | Mentions "visitor" (own resources, no activity subject to authorisation), "student", or the activity carried out |
| **15** | Issue may be subject to a stay visa of more than three months |
| **16** | Residence card: regular, uninterrupted residence of at least 4 years |
| **17** | Residence card issued notably to the foreign spouse of a Moroccan national, subject to regular stay and unless exempted |
| **43, 44** | Staying without a card: 5,000 to 30,000 MAD and one month to one year in prison; failing to renew in time: 3,000 to 10,000 MAD and one to six months |

The maximum duration of a card (1 to 10 years) and the choice between the two titles depend on the reason invoked and the administration's decision, which may refuse a card to a foreigner whose presence would threaten public order (art. 14).

## The MRE angle: spouse, children, parents

- **Foreign spouse of a Moroccan:** this is the case explicitly provided for in article 17. Enter legally (see the [e-Visa](/en/morocco-e-visa-guide) if your nationality requires it) and prepare the file on arrival rather than on day 89.
- **Foreign children:** the position of minors depends on their nationality and their parents' titles (art. 6); if your child is also Moroccan, the residence question does not arise in the same terms. Ask the foreigners' office or the consulate before filing.
- **Foreign parents dependent on a Moroccan:** article 17 also covers certain dependent ascendants; proof of dependency is the sensitive point.
- **Permanent return of a mixed family:** the foreign spouse's stay is prepared together with the [permanent return](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide), [AMO health cover](/en/amo-health-insurance-return-morocco-mre) and children's schooling.

## Before you start

- Enter Morocco legally with a valid passport and the **entry stamp**: your 90-day exemption (art. 9) starts on that date.
- Do not let your legal stay expire before filing: exceeding the authorised period exposes you to article 42 (2,000 to 20,000 MAD and one to six months' prison, or one of the two).
- Remote workers have no category of their own: the "visitor" card is reserved for those who prove they live on their own resources without activity subject to authorisation (art. 13). Remote work for a foreign employer is not defined by the text read: ask the foreigners' office.

## Documents generally requested (indicative list)

The law refers to a **regulatory text** for the procedures (art. 8); we could not read a current official list. Secondary sources (firms, welcome associations) cite a base file close to this one, to be confirmed:

- valid passport and entry stamp or visa;
- application form and ID photos;
- proof of address in Morocco (lease, accommodation certificate);
- proof of the reason for stay;
- medical certificate and criminal record extract;
- proof of resources;
- tax stamp for the fees (100 MAD per year granted according to several secondary sources, no official schedule found in October 2026).

### By reason

| Reason | Specific documents (indicative) |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | Work contract approved by ANAPEC, employer certificate |
| **Student** | Enrolment certificate from an institution |
| **Spouse of a Moroccan** | Marriage certificate, spouse's ID documents, proof of address |
| **Retiree / annuitant** | Proof of pension or regular income, regular transfers to a Moroccan account, valid health insurance |

## The 4-step route

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 1" title="Prepare and translate">

Gather originals and copies. Documents issued abroad often need to be **apostilled or legalised** and **translated** by a sworn translator. See [apostille and legalisation](/en/apostille-legalisation-documents-morocco) and the [Moroccan birth certificate](/en/moroccan-birth-certificate-full-copy-extract-watiqa-mre).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 2" title="Submit the file">

Ask the foreigners' office whether an **appointment or pre-application** is required in your city, then file. You receive a **receipt**: it temporarily replaces the card (art. 8).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 3" title="Wait for the decision">

Times cited by secondary sources range from about **five weeks to three months**, longer in Casablanca and Rabat according to the same sources. No official time was found. Keep the receipt with you.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 4" title="Collect and renew">

Note the **expiry date** and start renewal well before: article 44 penalises failure to apply in time.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Common mistakes

- **Incomplete file:** most delays come from missing or non-apostilled documents.
- **Insufficient proof of resources:** for retirement, show **regular** income and regular transfers to a Moroccan account. See [open a bank account in Morocco](/en/open-bank-account-morocco-mre) and [retire in Morocco](/en/retire-in-morocco-pension-tax-guide).
- **Non-compliant translations:** have them done by a sworn translator.
- **Staying without a card after the 90 days:** fine and prison possible (art. 43), even if application varies.
- **Confusing visa and card:** an e-Visa does not turn into a residence permit.

## Checklist

<Checklist id="residence-permit" lang="en" title="Residence permit: my checklist">

- Reason for stay chosen and matching documents listed
- File apostilled and translated if documents come from abroad
- Stamped entry date noted, filing planned before the end of the 90 days
- Appointment or pre-application confirmed with the foreigners' office
- Receipt kept on you after filing
- Card expiry date noted, renewal planned

</Checklist>
