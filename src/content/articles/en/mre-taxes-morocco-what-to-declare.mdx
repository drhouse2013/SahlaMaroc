When you live abroad and have income or property in Morocco, the question returns every spring: **who taxes me, on what, and where do I declare it?** This guide relies on the **2026 Tax Guide for Moroccans Residing Abroad** published by the tax authority (DGI) and the ministry in charge of MRE ([copy on mre.gov.ma](https://www.mre.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2026-07/Guide_MRE__FR_2026_4.pdf)), and says clearly what could not be confirmed.

<Callout type="tip" title="Landmarks to remember">
- **Tax residence:** under article 23 of the General Tax Code, as reported by specialist sources (re-read the official text on [tax.gov.ma](https://www.tax.gov.ma)), your tax home is in Morocco if your permanent home or the centre of your economic interests is there, or if you stay more than 183 days in any 365. **This test is not in the MRE tax guide read: confirm with the DGI.**
- **A non-resident** is taxed in Morocco on **Moroccan-source income** (rent, property gains, dividends, interest).
- **Tax treaties** prevent double taxation: ask your tax office for a **tax residency certificate**.
</Callout>

## What's your tax status?

| Situation | Taxed in Morocco on… |
|---|---|
| **Tax resident in Morocco** | Their **worldwide** income, subject to treaties |
| **Non-resident (MRE living abroad)** | **Moroccan-source** income |

Days alone are not enough: home, activity and economic interests count. Keep an **attendance log** (tickets, stamps): it is your best proof. If in doubt, have a tax adviser establish your status.

## Moroccan income to know about

**1. Rental income (property let in Morocco).** According to the 2026 guide: a flat **40 %** allowance before the progressive scale. If the tenant is a company or a professional on the actual or simplified net-profit regime, a non-final withholding applies: **10 %** (gross annual income below MAD 120,000) or **15 %** (MAD 120,000 and above); you may opt for the **final 20 % rate**, which exempts you from the annual return. Otherwise, file and pay **online before 1 March** of the following year via the DGI SIMPL service. A home lent **free of charge** to parents or children is outside income tax.

**2. Progressive income-tax scale (2026 guide).** MAD 0 to 40,000: 0 %; 40,001 to 60,000: 10 %; 60,001 to 80,000: 20 %; 80,001 to 100,000: 30 %; 100,001 to 180,000: 34 %; above 180,000: 37 % (with an amount to deduct per band).

**3. Property gain (land profit).** Taxed at **20 % of net profit, with a minimum payment of 3 % of the sale price**. The return is filed **online within 30 days** of the sale. Exemptions include a sale whose total value does not exceed MAD 140,000 in the year, and the sale of a **main home occupied for at least 5 years** (a home kept by an MRE as their Moroccan residence, or occupied free by spouse, parents or children, counts while not rented out). See [property taxes when selling and renting](/en/property-tax-selling-renting-morocco-mre) and the [capital-gains calculator](/en/property-capital-gains-tax-morocco-calculator).

**4. Foreign-source pension** (if you settle in Morocco): **70 %** allowance on the part up to MAD 168,000 a year, 40 % on the rest, and an **80 % reduction** of the tax if the pension is permanently transferred in non-convertible dirhams. Attach a payment certificate and a bank certificate of the dirham equivalent. See [retiring in Morocco](/en/retire-in-morocco-pension-tax-guide).

**5. Residence tax and municipal services tax.** A **75 %** reduction of rental value for the home kept as main residence by an MRE (or occupied free by spouse, children or parents). New builds used as main home: residence-tax exemption for 5 years after completion. Filings: completion of works, change of owner or use by **31 January** of the following year; vacancy declared in **January**.

**6. Dividends and interest:** withholding tax depending on type and treaty. Rates are not in the MRE guide read: check tax.gov.ma or your bank.

## Avoiding double taxation

Morocco has signed many tax treaties, notably with France, Spain, Belgium and Canada (official list on tax.gov.ma). For property income they generally allow taxation **in the country where the property is**. Your country of residence may then give a credit or an exemption, but **you must declare** the income.

1. Identify the **treaty** between Morocco and your country of residence.
2. Get a **tax residency certificate** from your country.
3. Declare your Moroccan income **in your country**, with proof of tax paid in Morocco.

## Practical steps

1. **Get a tax ID:** online via the DGI remote identification service (SIMPL), without travelling, or at the regional tax office.
2. **List your Moroccan income and property** for the year.
3. **Compute** (allowances, withholding already suffered) or get help.
4. **File and pay online** before the deadlines.
5. **Archive** leases, receipts, statements and tax notices for several years.
6. **Then declare in your country of residence.**

## Common mistakes

- Thinking an informal let to a relative avoids tax: rent received is taxable.
- Forgetting to declare **rent received in dirhams** when the tenant withheld nothing.
- Missing the **30-day** deadline after a sale.
- Estimating days of presence instead of documenting them.
- Forgetting the declaration abroad after paying in Morocco.
- Neglecting municipal services tax and the MRE residence-tax reduction, which must be claimed.

## FAQ

**Do I declare in Morocco my salary earned in Europe?** In principle no if you are non-resident for tax: Moroccan taxation targets Moroccan-source income. If you become tax resident, the regime changes.

**What if I miss 1 March?** The guide passages read do not detail penalties; ask the DGI (05 37 27 37 27).

**How do I get a tax ID from abroad?** Through the DGI remote identification service SIMPL (digital identity, no travel), per the 2026 guide.

**Is there an official guide?** Yes: the [2026 MRE Tax Guide](https://www.mre.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2026-07/Guide_MRE__FR_2026_4.pdf) (in French).

## Checklist for MRE owners and investors

<Checklist id="mre-taxes" lang="en" title="MRE taxes: my checklist">

- Tax status (resident or non-resident) confirmed
- Days spent in Morocco tracked every year
- Tax treaty between Morocco and my country identified
- Tax residency certificate obtained
- DGI tax ID obtained
- Moroccan income declared in Morocco before 1 March
- Moroccan income declared in my country of residence
- Proofs (leases, receipts, taxes paid) archived
- Municipal services tax and residence tax up to date

</Checklist>
