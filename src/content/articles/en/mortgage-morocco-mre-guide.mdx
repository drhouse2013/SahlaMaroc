Moroccan banks lend to **MREs** (Moroccans living abroad) with conditions close to those for residents. Figures below are **indicative ranges reported in 2026**. Always get a written offer from each bank.

<Callout type="tip" title="Typical ranges (to confirm with your bank)">
- **Rate:** roughly **4.2 % to 5.8 %** fixed in dirhams, depending on profile and bank.
- **Loan share:** usually up to **70 %** of the property value, sometimes **80 %**.
- **Down payment:** commonly **20–30 %**, plus buying costs of around **6–8 %**.
- **Duration:** up to **25 years**, sometimes 30 for young borrowers.
</Callout>

## Step by step

1. **Set your budget:** price, costs and works. Use the [buying costs calculator](/en/morocco-property-buying-costs-calculator).
2. **Simulate your payment** with the [mortgage calculator](/en/morocco-mortgage-calculator).
3. **Compare offers:** request quotes from at least three banks (for example Attijariwafa bank, Bank of Africa, Banque Populaire, CIH Bank), including those with MRE desks in Europe.
4. **Assemble the file:** ID, proof of income and employment abroad, recent bank statements, the preliminary sales contract.
5. **Sign with the notary:** the bank's mortgage is registered at the Land Registry.

## Documents usually requested

- Passport or national ID and proof of residence abroad.
- Recent payslips and an employer certificate, or accounts if self-employed.
- Bank statements showing your savings and the origin of the funds.
- The promise of sale or preliminary contract, and the property's title.

## Mortgage in Morocco or in Europe?

A loan in dirhams avoids exchange risk if you'll repay from Moroccan income. A loan in euros may be cheaper if you earn in euros, but it adds exchange risk and requires collateral. Use a traceable bank channel for every transfer and keep the receipts. See [sending money to Morocco](/en/send-money-to-morocco).

## Aid and extra savings

If you buy a new home within the price limits, check **Daam Sakane** (70,000–100,000 MAD): [our guide](/en/daam-sakane-housing-aid-mre). To avoid traps, read [buying property from abroad](/en/buying-property-morocco-from-abroad).
