Taghazout, a small fishing village about 20 km north of Agadir, has become Morocco's **surf** capital. Rocky points, sandy beaches, schools at every price and a laid-back mood: here's how to choose your spots, season and package.

<Callout type="tip" title="In short">
- **Beginners:** best period **March to May** and **October–November**, with manageable waves and pleasant water.
- **Biggest swell:** **December to February**, for experienced surfers.
- **Group lessons:** around **250–350 MAD** per person; private lessons around **400–600 MAD** for 2 h.
- **Surf camps:** from around **3,500 MAD** a week (depending on package).
</Callout>

## The spots

| Spot | Level | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Devil's Rock (Tamraght)** | Beginners | A safe, forgiving setting for first waves |
| **Hash Point** | Intermediate | Good spot to progress towards reef breaks |
| **Anchor Point** | Advanced | A long right peeling over the reef; needs at least 1.5 m swell; tight take-off zone, very active locals |
| **Killer Point** | Advanced | Powerful, fast right below the cliff, entry and exit through a rocky channel |

**Respect the locals and priority rules** in the water, especially at advanced spots.

## When to go

- **March to May and October–November:** 1–2 m waves, gentler winds, fewer crowds and lower camp prices: ideal to learn.
- **December to February:** North Atlantic storms, 2–4 m swells, 15–20 surfable days a month for experienced surfers.
- **Summer:** smaller waves, good for a first taste; warmer water.

## Lessons, gear and camps

- **Group lessons** (4–6 people) cost around 250–350 MAD; **private** around 400–600 MAD for 2 h. Packs of 5 or 10 lessons cut the per-session price.
- A **lesson with transport and gear** is around €30 depending on the school.
- **Surf camps** include accommodation, lessons and sometimes meals: compare what's included.
- Choose **qualified instructors**, equipment in good condition and a group matched to your level.

## Access and life on site

**Agadir Al Massira airport** is the closest: see [Morocco's airports](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre) to get to Agadir, then a taxi or transfer to Taghazout.
