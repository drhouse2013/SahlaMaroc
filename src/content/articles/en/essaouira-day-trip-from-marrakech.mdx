After the heat and buzz of Marrakech, **Essaouira** feels like a deep breath: a white and blue port town on the Atlantic, with a UNESCO-listed medina, fresh fish and constant trade winds.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- About **3 hours** by road from Marrakech.
- **By bus:** Supratours has several departures a day (around **140 MAD** one way at our last check).
- **By tour:** the easiest option, often with a stop at an argan cooperative.
- **Our tip:** stay **one night**. The town is at its best in the evening and early morning, when day-trippers have left.
</Callout>

## Getting there

**Supratours buses** leave from the station next to Marrakech railway station, with a short comfort break on the way. Book ahead in high season. **CTM** also runs departures, though fewer. Organised **day trips** pick you up at your hotel and give you about 5–6 hours in town.

## What to see

- **The port:** blue fishing boats, seagulls and fish auctions in the morning. Pick your fish and have it grilled at the stalls nearby.
- **The Skala ramparts:** old cannons and ocean views, especially at sunset.
- **The medina:** relaxed and easy to navigate, with woodwork (thuya), galleries and small cafés.
- **The beach:** long and windy, perfect for kitesurfing and windsurfing, less so for sunbathing.

- Bring a **windbreaker**, even in summer.
