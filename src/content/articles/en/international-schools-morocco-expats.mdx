Settling in Morocco with a family quickly raises the school question. The country offers a very developed network of **French establishments**, **American and British schools** in the big cities, and of course Moroccan schooling. Here's how to find your way.

<Callout type="tip" title="The essentials">
- Morocco has **23 French establishments under AEFE** and **7 under OSUI**.
- **Tuition fees** at international schools range from about **50,000 to 140,000 MAD a year** depending on the school and level (indicative).
- **Apply early:** places are limited and files take time.
</Callout>

## The main school families

| Type | For whom | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **French education** (AEFE, OSUI) | French and French-speaking families, curriculum continuity | In Casablanca: École française internationale (EFI), Lycée Lyautey; in Rabat: Lycée Descartes, Lycée Léon l'Africain; in Marrakech: Lycée Victor-Hugo |
| **American schools** | American curricula and sometimes IB | Casablanca American School, Rabat American School, Marrakech |
| **British schools** | British curriculum | British International School of Casablanca |
| **Belgian, Spanish, etc.** | Depending on nationality | Dedicated schools in several cities |
| **Moroccan private or public** | Long-term settled families | Arabic, French, Amazigh depending on curricula |

## Tuition fees (indicative)

Annual fees vary widely:

- American schools: about **70,000 to 140,000 MAD** a year;
- British schools: about **60,000 to 110,000 MAD**;
- General range for international schools: **50,000 to 140,000 MAD**.

Registration, canteen, transport and activity fees come on top. Always ask for the full fee schedule.

## Enrolling your child

1. **Choose the curriculum** that ensures continuity (French, IB, American, British).
2. **Contact the school very early,** ideally 6 to 12 months before the start of the year.
3. **Prepare the file:** school reports, birth certificate, vaccination record, passport, photos, possibly entrance tests.
4. **Visit** the school and meet the management.
5. **Plan housing** near the school: urban traffic can lengthen journeys. See [where to stay in Casablanca](/en/where-to-stay-in-casablanca) and [where to stay in Rabat](/en/where-to-stay-in-rabat).

## For returning MREs

If you return with children, also look at bilingual Moroccan schooling and local private schools. See our [permanent return guide](/en/permanent-return-morocco-mre-guide) to organise schooling, removal and paperwork together.

<Callout type="info" title="Indicative">
Fees, capacity and curricula change every year. Check the schools' websites and AEFE for the French network.
</Callout>
