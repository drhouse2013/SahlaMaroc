Buying a car locally avoids clearing an imported car through customs, but calls for precise checks, especially for a **used** one. This guide complements [importing your car](/en/import-car-permanent-return-morocco), [the 180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule) and [car insurance](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border).

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Used**: check the registration document, that there is no **opposition** (checkable online, per the press) and that the **annual vehicle tax (TSAV)** is paid.
- **Registration transfer**: NARSA has run an online platform since 17 January 2023; per the press, the file must be filed within 30 days of the legalisation of the signature, with a receipt valid for 60 days.
- **Vehicle tax**: due before 31 January; for a vehicle put into service during the year, the tax authority says 60 days after the filing receipt.
- **2026 tax rates**: sources disagree; check vignette.ma or the tax authority.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="What is changing (updated 16 Oct 2026)">
Since law 55.19 (simplification of procedures) took effect, announced by the Interior Ministry in April 2021 alongside the Idarati portal, Moroccan administrations should no longer require certain certificates (single status, marriage, non-divorce, polygamy, kinship, widowhood, the Moroccan administrative life certificate...) and may accept a sworn declaration instead. This is not a 2026 novelty, and the full official list of the 22 documents is not public in the sources we checked. For the "legalised signatures" mentioned in this guide, the abolition of signature legalisation may apply: confirm with NARSA or the notary. Depending on the administration or receiving body, the document may still be requested, especially for a file abroad or at a consulate: confirm before submitting.
</Callout>

## 1. Before buying

- Ask the seller for the **original registration document**, the insurance certificate and proof of this year's vehicle tax.
- Check online that there is no **opposition** to the transfer; if there is, a release is needed.
- Have the car inspected by an independent mechanic; check mileage and history.
- Pay by transfer or cheque, never in cash without a signed receipt.

## 2. The registration transfer

1. Seller and buyer declare the sale and purchase through the NARSA portal.
2. Signatures are legalised.
3. File the dossier at an authorised branch (Barid Bank and Barid Cash, per the press).
4. Receipt, then collection of the new registration document.

A third-party guide mentions 300 to 800 MAD and 3 to 10 working days: unofficial figures.

## 3. Vehicle tax and insurance

The tax (TSAV) depends on fiscal horsepower and fuel. Payment is free at ATMs and bank websites, and the certificate can be downloaded at vignette.ma. Insure the vehicle **before driving**: see [car insurance](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border).

## 4. New or used?

- **New**: manufacturer warranty, invoice, no history; the price includes the final buyer's taxes.
- **Used**: lower price but higher risk; prefer an identified seller.
- **Importing**: for a car you already own abroad, see [customs clearance](/en/import-car-permanent-return-morocco).

## What was not confirmed

- The 2026 vehicle tax scales (two sources give different ranges).
- Actual transfer fees and timelines.
- Financing conditions for an MRE (ask your bank, [bank account](/en/open-bank-account-morocco-mre)).
