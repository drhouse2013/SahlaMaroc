Mohammed V airport (CMN) is about **30 km south-east of downtown Casablanca**. The **train** and the **Aérobus** are the cheapest options. Taxis are simple but cost much more.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Train (ONCF):** to **Casa-Voyageurs** in about **30–35 minutes**, **50 MAD in second class** and **70 MAD in first** according to ONDA. Roughly one train an hour, from about 06:50 to 22:50 from the airport.
- **Aérobus (line AE):** direct to **Casa-Port station** via the city centre, every 30 minutes, 24/7, **50 MAD**.
- **Taxi:** fares are not published online and cost far more than the train, with a **night surcharge**. Agree the price before getting in.
- **Private transfer:** useful for families or a late arrival.
</Callout>

## Option 1: the airport train

A dedicated station inside the terminal links the airport to **Casa-Voyageurs**, the main city station, with roughly one train an hour (timetables on oncf.ma). From Casa-Voyageurs you can reach Rabat, Marrakech, Fes and Tangier. See our [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).

- Buy your ticket at the station counter or on the ONCF app.
- Check the last train of the evening. Late arrivals often need a taxi.
- Keep your ticket until you exit.

## Option 2: the bus

- **Aérobus (line AE):** links the airport to **Casa-Port station** via the city centre. Departures every **30 minutes, 24 hours a day**. The flat fare is **50 MAD**; check on the spot which payment methods are accepted.
- **CTM and Supratours coaches:** serve stations and central points of Casablanca, with few departures: check times and fares with the operators.

The bus is a good choice late at night, when the last train has left.

## Option 3: taxi

Use the **official taxi rank** outside arrivals. Taxis from the airport are usually quoted at a flat rate, and there is a night surcharge after about 8 pm. Confirm that the price is **per car, not per person**, and give the exact address of your hotel.

## Before you leave the airport

1. **Cash:** withdraw a small amount at a bank ATM in arrivals. See [cash and ATMs in Morocco](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
2. **Phone:** get connected with an eSIM or a local SIM. See the [SIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).
3. **Time:** Morocco is on GMT all year since September 2026. See [Morocco's time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt).

## Steps on arrival

1. **Clear police control and collect bags.**
2. **Withdraw dirhams** ([cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco)).
3. **For the train,** follow the "Train" signs in the terminal and buy your ticket (50 MAD second class per ONDA).
4. **For Rabat or Marrakech,** change at Casa-Voyageurs: [trains in Morocco](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
5. **For a taxi,** use the official rank and fix the price.
6. **Late at night,** check the last train before committing.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing the wrong station: Casa-Voyageurs is not Casa-Port.
- Arriving late with no plan B (last train gone).
- Accepting a taxi with no fixed price.
- Forgetting to keep your ticket.
- Staying on the train to the terminus without checking the arrival station.

## FAQ

**Does the train go straight to Rabat?** No: change at Casa-Voyageurs per the information read; confirm on [oncf.ma](https://www.oncf.ma).

**How long to the centre?** About 30 to 35 minutes by train to Casa-Voyageurs; a taxi depends on traffic.

**Official information?** [ONDA](https://www.onda.ma).

**With children and suitcases?** A taxi or booked transfer is simpler.

## Four typical arrivals

**Daytime arrival, light traveller.** The train is the cheapest: follow the signs, buy the ticket, get off at Casa-Voyageurs and continue by tram or taxi to your hotel (see [tram](/en/tram-brt-casablanca-rabat-guide)).

**Late arrival.** Check the last train time and the Aérobus departures. If in doubt, plan a taxi or booked transfer, and warn your lodging.

**MRE with family and many suitcases.** The train means carrying bags up stairs and along platforms: a grand taxi for the whole family at a price agreed in advance is often simpler. If your route continues north or south by road, see [the ferry and the motorway](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route).

**Connection to another city.** If you are going to Rabat, Marrakech or Tangier, the train via Casa-Voyageurs makes sense: see [the trains guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq). Keep a margin of at least an hour for the connection.

## How much margin?

Allow generously: police formalities and baggage reclaim can take time on arrival; these durations are not published here. For your return flight, arrive at the airport well in advance, especially in summer and during Operation Marhaba: [Operation Marhaba](/en/operation-marhaba-guide).

## Extra questions

**Where do I buy the train ticket?** At the airport station counter or on the ONCF app; keep the ticket until you exit.

**Can I pay the taxi by card?** In practice no: withdraw dirhams before boarding. See [cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).

**What if my flight lands very early in the morning?** Check the first morning train; otherwise a taxi or booked transfer is safest.

**Is there left luggage?** Not confirmed here: ask the ONDA.

## Good to know

Keep your passport and boarding documents handy for the arrival formalities. If you rely on a mobile app for transport, connect to a SIM or eSIM first: see the [SIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco). Always confirm the final price of a taxi before getting in.

## Checklist

1. Dirhams withdrawn.
2. Arrival station chosen.
3. Last train checked.
4. Taxi price fixed if needed.
5. Ticket kept.

## Other airports

At **Rabat-Salé, Fes-Saïss and Agadir-Al Massira**, public bus service is limited, or tied to flight times in Rabat-Salé. Most travellers use the licensed **grand taxis** with regulated fares from the terminal.
