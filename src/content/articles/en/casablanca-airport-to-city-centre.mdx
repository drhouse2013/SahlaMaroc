Mohammed V airport (CMN) is about **30 km south-east of downtown Casablanca**. The **train** and the **Aérobus** are the cheapest options. Taxis are simple but cost much more.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Train (ONCF):** to **Casa-Voyageurs** in about **30–35 minutes**, **50 MAD in second class** and **70 MAD in first** according to ONDA. Roughly one train an hour, from about 06:50 to 22:50 from the airport.
- **Aérobus (line AE):** direct to **Casa-Port station** via the city centre, every 30 minutes, 24/7, **50 MAD**.
- **Taxi:** fares are not published online and cost far more than the train, with a **night surcharge**. Agree the price before getting in.
- **Private transfer:** useful for families or a late arrival.
</Callout>

## Option 1: the airport train

A dedicated station inside the terminal links the airport to **Casa-Voyageurs**, the main city station, with roughly one train an hour (timetables on oncf.ma). From Casa-Voyageurs you can reach Rabat, Marrakech, Fes and Tangier. See our [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).

- Buy your ticket at the station counter or on the ONCF app.
- Check the last train of the evening. Late arrivals often need a taxi.
- Keep your ticket until you exit.

## Option 2: the bus

- **Aérobus (line AE):** links the airport to **Casa-Port station** via the city centre. Departures every **30 minutes, 24 hours a day**. The flat fare is **50 MAD**; check on the spot which payment methods are accepted.
- **CTM and Supratours coaches:** serve stations and central points of Casablanca, with few departures: check times and fares with the operators.

The bus is a good choice late at night, when the last train has left.

## Option 3: taxi

Use the **official taxi rank** outside arrivals. Taxis from the airport are usually quoted at a flat rate, and there is a night surcharge after about 8 pm. Confirm that the price is **per car, not per person**, and give the exact address of your hotel.

## Before you leave the airport

1. **Cash:** withdraw a small amount at a bank ATM in arrivals. See [cash and ATMs in Morocco](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
2. **Phone:** get connected with an eSIM or a local SIM. See the [SIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).
3. **Time:** Morocco is on GMT all year since September 2026. See [Morocco's time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt).

## Other airports

At **Rabat-Salé, Fes-Saïss and Agadir-Al Massira**, public bus service is limited, or tied to flight times in Rabat-Salé. Most travellers use the licensed **grand taxis** with regulated fares from the terminal.
