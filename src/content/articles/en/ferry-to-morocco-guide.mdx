The ferry is still the favourite way for MRE families to travel home with their car. Here are the routes, crossing times and the habits that get you the best price.

## How much does it cost?

In summer, expect roughly **€150–350** for a car and two passengers on Algeciras–Tangier, and much more for long crossings with a cabin from France or Italy. From Sète, the main French port with direct sailings to Morocco (about 34 to 48 hours to Tanger Med depending on the operator; La Méridionale also linked Marseille to Tanger Med in summer 2026), expect roughly €200–500 per person with a cabin, or €600–1,200 for a car and two people with a cabin. Prices climb sharply in July–August: these ranges are indicative only, check them when booking.

**Clocks:** Morocco is on GMT all year since 20 September 2026, so Spain and France are 1 h ahead in winter and 2 h ahead in summer. Check whether your ticket shows local or departure time.

After landing: [motorway from Tanger Med and tolls](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route).

## 6 tips to pay less and wait less

1. **Book in April–May**: the best fares go fast.
2. **Fixed-date tickets**: in August they're generally mandatory in Spanish and Moroccan ports.
3. **Avoid weekends** and the first and last days of the month.
4. **Arrive 3 hours early** in high season.
5. **Bring food** for long crossings.
6. **Compare the full cost** (crossing + fuel + stopovers) with flying and renting a car using the [summer budget calculator](/en/summer-in-morocco-budget).
