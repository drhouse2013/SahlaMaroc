The ferry is still the favourite way for MRE families to travel home with their car. Here are the routes, crossing times and the habits that get you the best price.

## Main routes

| From | To | Approx. crossing | Operators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Algeciras | Tanger Med | 1 h 30 – 2 h 30 | AML, Baleària, Trasmed, Intershipping |
| Tarifa | Tangier city | about 1 h | FRS |
| Motril | Tanger Med / Al Hoceima | 6 – 8 h | Baleària, Trasmed |
| Almería | Nador | 6 – 8 h | Baleària, Trasmed, GNV |
| Barcelona | Tanger Med | 24 – 32 h | GNV, Grimaldi |
| Sète | Tanger Med / Nador | 24 – 44 h | AML, GNV |
| Genoa | Tanger Med | 48 h + | GNV |

Times and operators based on published summer 2026 schedules; they change by season.

## How much does it cost?

In summer, expect roughly **€150–350** for a car and two passengers on Algeciras–Tangier, and much more for long crossings with a cabin from France or Italy. Prices climb sharply in July–August.

## 6 tips to pay less and wait less

1. **Book in April–May**: the best fares go fast.
2. **Fixed-date tickets**: in August they're generally mandatory in Spanish and Moroccan ports.
3. **Avoid weekends** and the first and last days of the month.
4. **Arrive 3 hours early** in high season.
5. **Bring food** for long crossings.
6. **Compare the full cost** (crossing + fuel + stopovers) with flying and renting a car using the [summer budget calculator](/en/summer-in-morocco-budget).
