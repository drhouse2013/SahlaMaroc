The ferry is still the favourite way for MRE families to travel home with their car. Here are the routes, crossing times and the habits that get you the best price.

## Main routes

| From | To | Approx. crossing | Operators |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Algeciras | Tanger Med | 1 h 30 – 2 h 30 | Baleària, Trasmed, AML, FRS, Naviera Armas |
| Tarifa | Tangier city | about 1 h | Baleària, AML |
| Motril | Tanger Med / Al Hoceima | 5 – 8 h | Baleària, Trasmed |
| Almería | Nador | 7 – 11 h | Naviera Armas, Trasmed, Baleària |
| Barcelona | Tanger Med | 29 – 34 h | GNV |
| Sète | Tanger Med / Nador | 34 – 48 h | GNV, Baleària |
| Genoa | Tanger Med | 48 h + | GNV |

Only the roughly 1 h 30 crossing for Tanger Med–Algeciras is confirmed on [Baleària's site](https://www.balearia.com/fr/routes-horaires/bateau-tangermed-algesiras). Other times and operators are indicative and change by season: check the operator's schedule before booking.

## How much does it cost?

In summer, expect roughly **€150–350** for a car and two passengers on Algeciras–Tangier, and much more for long crossings with a cabin from France or Italy. From Sète, the main French port with direct sailings to Morocco (about 34 to 48 hours to Tanger Med depending on the operator; La Méridionale also linked Marseille to Tanger Med in summer 2026), expect roughly €200–500 per person with a cabin, or €600–1,200 for a car and two people with a cabin. Prices climb sharply in July–August: these ranges are indicative only, check them when booking.

**Clocks:** Morocco is on GMT all year since 20 September 2026, so Spain and France are 1 h ahead in winter and 2 h ahead in summer. Check whether your ticket shows local or departure time.

After landing: [motorway from Tanger Med and tolls](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route).

## 6 tips to pay less and wait less

1. **Book in April–May**: the best fares go fast.
2. **Fixed-date tickets**: in peak season, a ticket with fixed date and time is the most common rule: check the change conditions.
3. **Avoid weekends** and the first and last days of the month.
4. **Arrive early** (2 to 3 hours before in high season, confirm on your ticket).
5. **Bring food** for long crossings.
6. **Compare the full cost** (crossing + fuel + stopovers) with flying and renting a car using the [summer budget calculator](/en/summer-in-morocco-budget).

## Which route for which traveller?

- **Coming from Spain or southern France by car**: Algeciras–Tanger Med is the shortest crossing. Plan a night in Algeciras or Tarifa.
- **Leaving from Sète with luggage and children**: the long crossing with a cabin saves a long drive, but costs more and depends on the operator's sailing days.
- **Heading to the Oriental (Nador, Oujda)**: Almería–Nador or Sète–Nador avoid the drive from Tangier.
- **No car**: compare with flying ([flights Europe–Morocco](/en/cheap-flights-europe-to-morocco-guide)), often faster for the same budget.

## At the port: the steps

1. Go to the operator's check-in with ticket, passport or ID for all passengers and the car's registration certificate.
2. Pass Spanish police control (outer Schengen border) when leaving Algeciras.
3. Board and follow the operator's instructions (any forms to complete on board).
4. On arrival in Morocco: police control, then customs for the car (temporary admission, see the [180-day rule](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule)).
5. Check your insurance before hitting the road ([green card and border insurance](/en/car-insurance-morocco-green-card-border)).

## Common mistakes

- Booking an outbound ticket without planning the return: late August is the tightest time.
- Arriving at the last minute assuming the time shown is Moroccan time when it is the departure port's local time.
- Forgetting that a minor travelling with one parent may need a document ([children and pets](/en/travel-morocco-children-pets)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is the fastest crossing?** Tarifa–Tangier city is the shortest (about 1 hour, indicative), but Tanger Med is better connected to the motorway.

**Is the ferry cheaper than flying?** It depends on the number of passengers and the car: with four people and a vehicle the ferry is often better; travelling alone, flying often wins.

**Where are the official timetables?** On the operators' websites (for example [Baleària](https://www.balearia.com)), the only reliable sources for current times and fares. For summer reception and assistance: [Mohammed V Foundation](https://www.fm5.ma/en).

## What if the sailing is cancelled or delayed?

Change, refund and compensation conditions depend on the carrier and the fare bought: they are in the ticket's terms, which we have not compared. Keep your booking number, contact the carrier through its official channel and keep receipts for any costs incurred. Avoid unknown resellers: a ticket bought outside official channels may be refused at boarding.

## Cabin crossings: for whom?

On the long routes (Sète, Barcelona, Genoa), a cabin lets you sleep on board, but its price is added to the passage. Compare the total cost with driving to Algeciras: tolls, fuel, hotels, fatigue. For a family with young children, the night at sea may justify the extra cost. Cabin prices vary sharply by date: no figure is given here for lack of an official source.
