Fes is split between the huge medieval medina, **Fes el Bali**, and the calmer **Ville Nouvelle**. Your choice shapes your stay more than the hotel itself.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **First visit, 2–3 nights:** a riad in **Fes el Bali**, near **Bab Boujloud** for easy arrival.
- **Quieter and cheaper medina:** the **Andalusian quarter**, east of the river.
- **Families, long stays, easy taxis:** the **Ville Nouvelle**.
</Callout>

## Fes el Bali: the medina

The world's largest car-free urban area is a maze of alleys, madrasas, tanneries and craft workshops. A riad here is the best way to feel the city.

- **Around Bab Boujloud:** the most convenient entrance. Taxis can drop you at the gate, and many riads send someone to meet you.
- **Kairouyine quarter:** the heart of the souks and monuments. Central, but busier and noisier.
- **Andalusian quarter:** quieter, more residential and often better value, with a more local feel.

**Good to know:** you walk everywhere, over uneven ground. Luggage is carried by porters or handcarts, so agree on a price first. Get a clear route from your riad or ask them to pick you up, and see our tips in the [Marrakech airport guide](/en/marrakech-airport-to-medina) for how arrivals in a medina work.

## Ville Nouvelle: practical and calm

The modern city has wide streets, cafés, supermarkets and the train station. It's a good base for families, long stays and remote workers. Allow a short taxi ride to reach the medina.

## Which area for which trip?

| Profile | Best area |
|---|---|
| Culture seekers, couples | Fes el Bali (riad) |
| Budget travellers | Bab Boujloud or the Andalusian quarter |
| Families with strollers | Ville Nouvelle |
| Remote workers, long stays | Ville Nouvelle |
