Morocco's **direct housing aid** programme (Daam Sakane), running from 2024 to 2028, is open to Moroccans living in Morocco **and abroad**.

<Callout type="tip" title="The amounts">
- **100,000 MAD** for a home priced at or below **300,000 MAD incl. VAT**
- **70,000 MAD** for a home between **300,000 and 700,000 MAD incl. VAT**
</Callout>

## Main conditions

- Hold **Moroccan nationality**.
- Not own a home in Morocco and never have received state housing aid.
- Buy a **new home** (first sale) with at least **two rooms** and an occupancy permit issued **from 1 January 2023**.
- Commit to living there as your **main residence for 5 years**. MREs may let their spouse, parents or children use it **free of charge**. Any paid rental or sale within 5 years means repaying the **whole aid**.

## How to apply

Applications are **100 % online** on the official platform **daamsakane.ma**, with ID, a no-property certificate, proof of residence abroad and the sale documents.
