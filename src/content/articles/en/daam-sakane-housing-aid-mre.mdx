The **direct housing aid** programme (Daam Sakane) is open to Moroccans **living in Morocco and abroad**. For an MRE the stakes are twofold: getting the aid, and above all **understanding the 5-year commitment**. This guide summarises the **official FAQ on daamsakane.ma** (read on 17 October 2026) and flags known updates; confirm current eligibility and timing before applying.

<Callout type="tip" title="The amounts">
- **100,000 MAD** for a home whose purchase price is **300,000 MAD incl. VAT or less**
- **70,000 MAD** for a price **above 300,000 MAD and up to 700,000 MAD incl. VAT**
</Callout>

## Who can apply?

According to the official FAQ, the buyer must:

- hold **Moroccan nationality**;
- have **never received a State housing benefit**;
- people who own property **jointly** may now also apply, following the 2026 expansion announced by the government; ask the platform to confirm how the rule applies to your situation;
- buy a home priced at **700,000 MAD incl. VAT at most**.

The property must be a **first sale** (first hand), have **at least two rooms** and hold an **occupancy permit issued from 1 January 2023**. Both the sale agreement and the final contract must be signed **before a notary**. Marital status does not matter: a single person without children can qualify.

## The MRE-specific rule: the 5-year commitment

The final deed contains two commitments from the beneficiary:

1. **Use the home as the main residence for 5 years** from the date of the final purchase contract.
2. **Grant the State a first- or second-ranking mortgage** guaranteeing repayment of the aid if the commitment is breached.

For MREs, the FAQ says the home must be **kept as their residence in Morocco** during those 5 years, or **made available free of charge** to their spouse, parents or children (first degree, direct line). A home that is **rented** or used for **professional purposes** does not meet that definition. The FAQ does not spell out how repayment works: do not assume you can rent it out quietly to holidaymakers or resell before 5 years without consequences. Have your notary review the commitment clause.

<Callout type="warning" title="Before you decide">
If you plan to rent the property while you are away, or to resell it quickly, Daam Sakane is probably not for you. The trade-off is simple: 70,000 to 100,000 MAD of aid against 5 years of constraints. For a rental project, see the [long-term rental guide](/en/rent-home-long-term-morocco-mre) instead.
</Callout>

## The process, step by step

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 1" title="Pick an eligible home">

Ask the developer or seller for the **price incl. VAT**, the **number of rooms**, the **occupancy permit** date (from 1 January 2023) and confirmation that it is a **first sale**. Also run the [developer and land title checks](/en/buying-property-morocco-from-abroad).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 2" title="Apply on the platform">

You fill in an **online form** on the Daam Sakane platform (website or mobile app): according to the FAQ, **no paper file** has to be lodged with the administration. The platform reports the outcome in your account and, according to the FAQ, by SMS. Check the current processing time and supporting documents in the form when you apply.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 3" title="The notary files the agreement and occupancy permit">

The notary files the **sale agreement** and the **occupancy permit** by the deadline shown in your application. The government approved a draft amendment to the programme decree in 2026; confirm the current date with the platform and the notary rather than relying on an older guide.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 4" title="Payment of the aid">

The amount and payment method are shown in your application after the agreement is filed. Confirm with the notary when the aid is received and how long you have to sign the final deed.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Step 5" title="Final deed and mortgage">

The final deed is signed **before a notary**, with the 5-year commitment and the mortgage in favour of the State. Track your application in the platform account or the app.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

If you cannot be present to sign, the power of attorney must follow a strict form: first read the guide on [power of attorney for property](/en/power-of-attorney-morocco-property-mre).

## Documents and information to prepare

- Passport and/or national ID, and proof of residence abroad (depending on what the form asks).
- The **sale agreement** at the notary and the home's **occupancy permit**.
- The exact price incl. VAT and a description of the property (rooms, area, address, land title).
- A copy of your bank account to trace the funds: useful for a later resale and repatriation.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying a resale home**, which is not eligible.
- **Exceeding 700,000 MAD incl. VAT**, even slightly.
- **Relying on an old deadline**: check the date shown in your current application and confirm it with the notary.
- **Renting the home out** to "offset costs" during the 5 years.
- Relying on unconfirmed conditions (for example an obligation to fund part of the price in foreign currency by transfer, relayed by third-party sites): the official FAQ we read does not mention it; ask your notary and bank.

<Checklist id="daam-sakane" lang="en" title="Daam Sakane: my checklist">

- Moroccan nationality, never received State housing aid
- Home: first sale, at least 2 rooms, occupancy permit since 01/01/2023
- Price incl. VAT at or below 700,000 MAD
- 5-year occupancy plan (or free use by close family) accepted
- Application filled in on the platform, answer received
- Notary informed: current filing and signing deadlines confirmed in the application
- Commitment clause and mortgage reviewed before signing

</Checklist>
