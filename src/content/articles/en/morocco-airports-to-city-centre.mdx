Casablanca and Marrakech already have their own guides ([Casablanca](/en/casablanca-airport-to-city-centre), [Marrakech](/en/marrakech-airport-to-medina)). For the other airports the offer is simpler: a **regulated-fare taxi**, sometimes a **bus** or **shuttle**. Here are the landmarks, city by city.

<Callout type="tip" title="Landmarks to remember">
- **Airport taxis** often quote a **fixed fare**: confirm it is **for the car**, not per person, and covers luggage.
- A **night surcharge** applies in the evening.
- **Buses and shuttles** exist but are **less frequent** than in Casablanca or Marrakech, especially late at night.
- Keep **50 and 100 MAD notes**: see [cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
</Callout>

## At a glance

| Airport | Distance to centre | Taxi (indicative) | Bus / shuttle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rabat-Salé (RBA)** | ≈ 14 km | ≈ 150 MAD by day, ≈ 200 MAD evening | Shuttle (25 MAD, every 15 min per press); cheaper city lines |
| **Fes-Saïss (FEZ)** | ≈ 15 km | ≈ 150 MAD (new town), ≈ 170 MAD (medina) | City bus no. 16, about every 35 minutes |
| **Tangier Ibn Battouta (TNG)** | ≈ 12 km | ≈ 100–150 MAD depending on the source | Line AE to the station, ≈ 40 minutes, ≈ 40 MAD |
| **Agadir Al Massira (AGA)** | ≈ 23 km | ≈ 150–220 MAD depending on time and source | Alsa shuttle ≈ 50 MAD; line 22 infrequent |

These prices are **ballpark figures**. Only the Rabat-Salé taxi fare (150 MAD by day, 200 MAD in the evening) is confirmed by ONDA (read on 10 October 2026); the 25 MAD Rabat shuttle every 15 minutes comes from the Le360 news outlet (January 2026). For Fes, Tangier and Agadir, no amount could be confirmed with an official source: figures differ between websites. Check the fare posted at the airport taxi rank on the spot.

## Rabat-Salé

The airport is north-east of Rabat. The **taxi** remains simplest. A **shuttle** and city lines exist, with varying frequency. To continue by train, head to Rabat-Ville station: see [trains in Morocco](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).

## Fes-Saïss

**Bus 16** serves the centre about every 35 minutes. A taxi costs a little more to the **medina** than to the **new town**. If you're staying in the medina, ask your riad to meet you: see [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes).

## Tangier Ibn Battouta

**Taxi** fares are regulated; sources quote between 100 and 150 MAD depending on the time, to be confirmed on the spot. A **dedicated bus line** links the airport to Tangier station in about 40 minutes. Tangier is the starting point of the **Al Boraq** high-speed train: see [the train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq) and [Chefchaouen day trip](/en/chefchaouen-day-trip-from-fes-tangier).

## Agadir Al Massira

The airport is about **23 km** from the centre. The **taxi** costs around 150–220 MAD depending on the source, more at night. The **Alsa shuttle** costs 50 MAD one way (80 MAD return, according to Alsa), and **line 22** is infrequent and unavailable late. For **Taghazout**, plan a taxi or transfer: see [surfing in Taghazout](/en/taghazout-agadir-surf-guide).

## Before leaving the airport

1. **Withdraw** a little cash from a bank ATM.
2. **Get connected:** [SIM and eSIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).
3. **Agree the taxi price** before getting in, or choose a [pre-booked transfer](/en/taxi-apps-morocco).
4. Check the **local time:** Morocco is on GMT all year ([time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt)).

## Steps on arrival

1. **Clear border control** and collect your bags.
2. **Withdraw a little cash** at a bank ATM ([cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco)).
3. **Choose the mode:** official taxi, bus or shuttle, or a booked transfer.
4. **For a taxi,** ask the price **before getting in** and check it covers the whole car.
5. **Give the exact address;** if your lodging is in a medina, tell them your arrival time.
6. **Keep change** for the ride and luggage.

## Common mistakes

- Accepting a "driver" who approaches you in the hall instead of the official taxi rank.
- Paying per person instead of a price for the car.
- Not anticipating the night surcharge.
- Arriving late and counting on a bus that no longer runs.
- Forgetting that bus times change: check before you go.

## FAQ

**Is there a train from these airports?** Not for Rabat-Salé, Fes, Tangier or Agadir in the information read; Casablanca has one: [Casablanca airport](/en/casablanca-airport-to-city-centre). Confirm on the [ONDA](https://www.onda.ma) site.

**How long to the centre?** Generally 20 to 40 minutes depending on city and traffic; an unofficial order of magnitude.

**Do taxis take cards?** In practice no: carry cash.

**What about a very late arrival?** Book a transfer or ask your lodging to arrange one.

## Three arrival profiles

**Solo traveller in Tangier, heading to the station.** An official taxi or the airport bus line works; from the station, the Al Boraq high-speed train links Rabat and Casablanca: [trains guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).

**Family in Agadir, heading to Taghazout.** Plan a taxi or booked transfer, with a price fixed in advance for the whole car.

**Traveller in Fes, staying in the medina.** Ask your riad for a pick-up at the airport or the medina gate, and compare with the official taxi.

## Extra questions

**Can I book a taxi in advance?** Yes, through your lodging or a transfer service; agree the price in writing.

**Which airport is farthest from its centre?** Among those in this guide, Agadir Al Massira (about 23 km) per the distances given.

**Do buses run at night?** Rarely; check before landing late.

## Good to know

Smaller airports have fewer services late at night: confirm transport options before booking a late flight. If you carry valuables or arrive after dark, a pre-booked transfer reduces risk. Keep your phone charged for maps and contact with your host.

## Checklist

1. Dirhams in your pocket.
2. Exact address noted.
3. Taxi price fixed before boarding.
4. Arrival time sent to your lodging.
5. Plan B if the bus no longer runs.
