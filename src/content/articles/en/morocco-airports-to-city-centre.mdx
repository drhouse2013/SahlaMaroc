Casablanca and Marrakech already have their own guides ([Casablanca](/en/casablanca-airport-to-city-centre), [Marrakech](/en/marrakech-airport-to-medina)). For the other airports the offer is simpler: a **regulated-fare taxi**, sometimes a **bus** or **shuttle**. Here are the landmarks, city by city.

<Callout type="tip" title="Landmarks to remember">
- **Airport taxis** often quote a **fixed fare**: confirm it is **for the car**, not per person, and covers luggage.
- A **night surcharge** applies in the evening.
- **Buses and shuttles** exist but are **less frequent** than in Casablanca or Marrakech, especially late at night.
- Keep **50 and 100 MAD notes**: see [cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
</Callout>

## At a glance

| Airport | Distance to centre | Taxi (indicative) | Bus / shuttle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rabat-Salé (RBA)** | ≈ 14 km | ≈ 150 MAD by day, ≈ 200 MAD evening | Shuttle (≈ 25 MAD); cheaper city lines |
| **Fes-Saïss (FEZ)** | ≈ 15 km | ≈ 150 MAD (new town), ≈ 170 MAD (medina) | City bus no. 16, about every 35 minutes |
| **Tangier Ibn Battouta (TNG)** | ≈ 12 km | ≈ 100 MAD by day, ≈ 150 MAD at night | Line AE to the station, ≈ 40 minutes, ≈ 40 MAD |
| **Agadir Al Massira (AGA)** | ≈ 23 km | ≈ 200–220 MAD (more at night) | Alsa shuttle ≈ 50 MAD; line 22 infrequent |

These prices are **ballpark figures reported in 2026**. Check the fare posted at the airport taxi rank on the spot.

## Rabat-Salé

The airport is north-east of Rabat. The **taxi** remains simplest. A **shuttle** and city lines exist, with varying frequency. To continue by train, head to Rabat-Ville station: see [trains in Morocco](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).

## Fes-Saïss

**Bus 16** serves the centre about every 35 minutes. A taxi costs a little more to the **medina** than to the **new town**. If you're staying in the medina, ask your riad to meet you: see [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes).

## Tangier Ibn Battouta

**Taxi** fares are regulated (about 100 MAD by day, 150 MAD at night). A **dedicated bus line** links the airport to Tangier station in about 40 minutes. Tangier is the starting point of the **Al Boraq** high-speed train: see [the train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq) and [Chefchaouen day trip](/en/chefchaouen-day-trip-from-fes-tangier).

## Agadir Al Massira

The airport is about **23 km** from the centre. The **taxi** costs around 200–220 MAD, more at night. The **Alsa shuttle** costs about 50 MAD one way (return is better value), and **line 22** is infrequent and unavailable late. For **Taghazout**, plan a taxi or transfer: see [surfing in Taghazout](/en/taghazout-agadir-surf-guide).

## Before leaving the airport

1. **Withdraw** a little cash from a bank ATM.
2. **Get connected:** [SIM and eSIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).
3. **Agree the taxi price** before getting in, or choose a [pre-booked transfer](/en/taxi-apps-morocco).
4. Check the **local time:** Morocco is on GMT all year ([time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt)).

<Callout type="info" title="Indicative fares">
Taxi and bus fares change and differ by source. Use them as landmarks and confirm on the spot.
</Callout>
