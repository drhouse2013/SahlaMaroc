Agadir is Morocco's seaside resort: a long bay, a mild climate nearly all year and a city rebuilt after the 1960 earthquake (the "memorial wall" is on the city's list of places). Its strength is simplicity: wide avenues, a promenade, services. Its limit is a less "historic" feel than Marrakech or Fes. The right area depends on your time budget, the sun you are after and who you travel with.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Beach and promenade on foot:** seafront.
- **Families and resorts with entertainment:** Founty and the tourist zone.
- **Restaurants, evening walks:** marina and bay.
- **Surf and village feel:** Tamraght, Taghazout (north).
</Callout>

## The seafront: beach and life on foot

Agadir's promenade and beach stretch across the bay (see the [Agadir city page](/en/cities/agadir#spots)). Seafront hotels often offer quick access to the sand, with restaurants and cafés close by. It is the ideal area for a short stay without a car. The trade-off: city-centre traffic and noise, and crowds in high season.

## Founty and the tourist zone: the all-in stay

South of the centre, the tourist zone groups large hotels and resorts with pools and kids' clubs. It is practical for a family wanting an enclosed, secure setting. Note that you depend more on the hotel for meals and entertainment; trips into town need a taxi.

## Marina and bay: evening and promenade

Around the marina, restaurants and shops animate the evening. It suits a couple who want to walk at night, with less noise than the central seafront. Souk El Had and the Agadir Oufella kasbah are separate visits, by taxi.

## Tamraght, Aourir and Taghazout: the surf north

About thirty minutes north, **Tamraght** and **Taghazout** are surf villages with hostels, apartments and surf camps. **Taghazout Bay** is a newer resort area alongside. These areas are made for surf and yoga more than shopping; read [where to sleep in Taghazout](/en/where-to-stay-in-taghazout) and the guide [surfing in Taghazout and Agadir](/en/taghazout-agadir-surf-guide).

## Access from the airport

Al Massira airport is about 25 km away (23 to 28 km depending on sources): see the guide to [Morocco's airports](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre) to compare options. By bus, Marrakech – Agadir takes about 3 to 3.5 hours; see [CTM and Supratours](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco). For Essaouira, read [where to stay in Essaouira](/en/where-to-stay-in-essaouira).

## Season

Winter remains mild, often more pleasant than northern Europe. Summer can be hot by day with hazy mornings on the coast. European school holidays and the year-end holidays fill places quickly.

## Safety and good habits

Agadir is generally seen as an easy city for families, but stay careful: watch your belongings on the beach, avoid leaving valuables visible in a car, and follow flags and swimming advice. Use identified taxis or an app; see [scams and safety](/en/morocco-scams-safety-travellers). If you work remotely, compare with the [best cities for nomads](/en/best-cities-digital-nomads-morocco).
