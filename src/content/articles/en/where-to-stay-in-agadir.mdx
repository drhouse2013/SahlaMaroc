Agadir is Morocco's big seaside resort: a long beach, sun almost year-round and very varied accommodation. To the north, the Taghazout coast attracts surfers. Here's how to choose.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **Beach and seafront hotels:** the **seafront** (boulevard du 20 août).
- **Resorts and all-inclusive for families:** **Founty** and the tourist zone.
- **Boutique hotels and upscale restaurants:** the **marina** and the bay.
- **Surf and a laid-back mood:** **Taghazout** and **Tamraght**, to the north.
</Callout>

## The areas

| Area | Atmosphere | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Seafront** | Hotels with direct beach access, often with sunbeds included | Most central for the beach |
| **Founty** | South of the centre, calmer; some of the most luxurious hotels and resorts | All-inclusive complexes with pools and kids' clubs |
| **Marina / bay** | Boutique hotels, restaurants and shops | Evenings out and strolls |
| **Tamraght** | Small hillside Berber village, ten-odd kilometres away | Calm, access to surf spots |
| **Taghazout and Taghazout Bay** | Surf village and new resort, about 30 minutes north | Surf, yoga, relaxed mood |

## Tips

1. **Arrival:** Al Massira airport is about 23 km away: see [Morocco's airports](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre).
2. **Surfers:** stay in Taghazout or Tamraght: [surfing in Taghazout](/en/taghazout-agadir-surf-guide).
3. **Families:** favour Founty or the seafront for beach and entertainment.
4. **Remote work:** check the Wi-Fi. See [best cities for digital nomads](/en/best-cities-digital-nomads-morocco).
5. **Season:** winter stays mild; in summer, morning haze can cool things down.
