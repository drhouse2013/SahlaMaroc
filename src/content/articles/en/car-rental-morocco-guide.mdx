Renting a car gives you the freedom to explore the Atlas, the coast or the desert at your own pace. It is also one of the areas where it's easiest to get caught out: deposit, excess, car condition, fines. Here's a simple method.

<Callout type="tip" title="The 6 reflexes">
- **A credit card** in the driver's name: the deposit is pre-authorised on it.
- **Photograph** the car from every angle before leaving, and keep the departure report.
- **Ask for the real excess** and what's excluded (tyres, underbody, glass).
- **Check the spare wheel** and jack.
- **Respect speed limits:** speed cameras and police checks are frequent.
- **Return the car with a full tank** if the fuel policy requires it.
</Callout>

## How much does it cost?

- **Off-season**, a small car starts around **€10–30 a day** depending on sources and companies. Prices climb in summer and holidays.
- **Deposit:** around **3,000 MAD** for a small car according to one source, but up to **€1,000–2,000** according to others, and more for an SUV. Ask for the exact amount before booking.
- **Excess buy-back** offered at the counter: roughly **€8–25 a day** depending on sources.

These amounts are indicative and vary by season, city and rental company.

## Insurance: read the excess

Third-party liability is included. Damage and theft cover (CDW/LDW) often is too, **with a high excess**. Excess buy-back reduces that amount but **frequently excludes** tyres, underbody, glass or keys. Ask for exclusions **in writing** before paying. Some bank cards also cover rentals: check the conditions of yours.

## Picking up the car

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="At the counter" title="Papers and contract">

Show your **licence**, **passport** and **credit card**. Read the contract: mileage, fuel, excess, additional drivers. Your foreign licence is enough at first: see [driving with a foreign licence](/en/driving-morocco-foreign-licence-exchange).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="In the car park" title="Condition report">

Walk around the vehicle with the agent, **film and photograph** scratches, tyres, glass, rims, interior and fuel level. Have every defect noted on the report.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Before leaving" title="Quick checks">

Spare wheel and jack, warning triangle, air conditioning, wipers, lights. Download an offline map: see the [SIM and eSIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="On return" title="Handing back">

Return the car on time, with a full tank if the contract says so, and get a **signed return report**. Keep it until the deposit is released.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Driving in Morocco: landmarks

| Area | Usual limit |
|---|---|
| Built-up area | **60 km/h** |
| Main route inside a built-up area | **80 km/h** |
| Road outside built-up areas | **100 km/h** |
| Motorway | **120 km/h** |

- Fixed and mobile speed cameras, **frequent checks** at city entrances.
- Speeding fines are graded by how far over you are, from **a few hundred to several thousand MAD**, with possible **licence suspension** from 50 km/h over (Law 52-05); reported scales differ; not wearing a seat belt and phone use at the wheel are also penalised. These amounts can change.
- **Tolls** on motorways: see [motorway from Tanger Med](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route).
- Watch for **pedestrians, two-wheelers and animals** in rural areas, and avoid driving at night in the mountains if you can.

## Common pitfalls

- **A very low price** advertised online but a high deposit and excess on site.
- **"House" repairs** billed on return with no departure report.
- **Fuel:** check diesel or petrol before filling up.
- **Forbidden roads:** some tracks are excluded from the contract.

## Speed limits: to confirm

The limits in the table (60, 80, 100 and 120 km/h) are those relayed by guides and the press; we did not re-read the official text. Always follow the **signs** and check the Highway Code (law 52-05) and the road-safety agency's information at [narsa.ma](https://www.narsa.ma).

## Common mistakes

- Booking the lowest price without reading deposit and excess.
- Not photographing or filming the car at pick-up.
- Accepting excess buy-back without reading the exclusions.
- Leaving items visible in the car.
- Driving at night in mountains or rural areas without need.
- Returning the car without a signed report.

## FAQ

**Which licence?** A valid licence; see [driving with a foreign licence](/en/driving-morocco-foreign-licence-exchange). The renter may ask for more (passport, credit card).

**Minimum age?** It depends on the renter: not confirmed here.

**Can I take it on the ferry?** See the contract terms and the [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide).

**What if I have an accident?** Inform the renter and the police (numbers: [scams and safety](/en/morocco-scams-safety-travellers), to be confirmed) and do not sign a report you do not understand.

## Three situations

**A weekend in Marrakech and the Atlas.** Rent a small car at the airport, check the excess and return it with a full tank. Avoid driving at night on mountain roads. See [day trips from Marrakech](/en/day-trips-from-marrakech).

**An MRE flying in and renting locally.** This is simpler than crossing with your own car: see [the ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide) and the [180-day rule for your car](/en/car-in-morocco-180-day-rule).

**A road trip to the desert.** Check the contract allows the planned roads, plan suitable insurance, water and an offline map, and plan your stages: [Merzouga desert](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour).

## Extra questions

**Can I add a second driver?** Depending on the renter and possibly for a fee: state it in the contract.

**Is a child seat provided?** Ask when booking.

**Is fuel included?** The fuel policy is in the contract: usually return full, as noted above.

## Before you drive off

Set your navigation offline, note the renter's emergency number, and check the fuel type. Take a photo of the fuel gauge and odometer. Keep the rental contract and your licence in the car, and park in guarded spaces in cities.

## Checklist

1. Credit card, licence and passport.
2. Excess and exclusions read in writing.
3. Photos and video before leaving.
4. Fuel, spare wheel, jack checked.
5. Signed return report.
