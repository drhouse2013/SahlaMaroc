Renting a car gives you the freedom to explore the Atlas, the coast or the desert at your own pace. It is also one of the areas where it's easiest to get caught out: deposit, excess, car condition, fines. Here's a simple method.

<Callout type="tip" title="The 6 reflexes">
- **A credit card** in the driver's name: the deposit is pre-authorised on it.
- **Photograph** the car from every angle before leaving, and keep the departure report.
- **Ask for the real excess** and what's excluded (tyres, underbody, glass).
- **Check the spare wheel** and jack.
- **Respect speed limits:** speed cameras and police checks are frequent.
- **Return the car with a full tank** if the fuel policy requires it.
</Callout>

## How much does it cost?

- **Off-season**, a small car starts around **€20–30 a day**, with weekly rates around €150–200. Prices climb in summer and holidays.
- **Deposit:** generally **1,000 to 5,000 MAD** for a small or compact car, and **8,000 to 15,000 MAD** for an SUV or high-end vehicle.
- **Excess buy-back** offered at the counter: on average **€15–25 a day**.

These amounts are indicative and vary by season, city and rental company.

## Insurance: read the excess

Third-party liability is included. Damage and theft cover (CDW/LDW) often is too, **with a high excess**. Excess buy-back reduces that amount but **frequently excludes** tyres, underbody, glass or keys. Ask for exclusions **in writing** before paying. Some bank cards also cover rentals: check the conditions of yours.

## Picking up the car

<Roadmap>
<RoadmapStep when="At the counter" title="Papers and contract">

Show your **licence**, **passport** and **credit card**. Read the contract: mileage, fuel, excess, additional drivers. Your foreign licence is enough at first: see [driving with a foreign licence](/en/driving-morocco-foreign-licence-exchange).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="In the car park" title="Condition report">

Walk around the vehicle with the agent, **film and photograph** scratches, tyres, glass, rims, interior and fuel level. Have every defect noted on the report.

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="Before leaving" title="Quick checks">

Spare wheel and jack, warning triangle, air conditioning, wipers, lights. Download an offline map: see the [SIM and eSIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).

</RoadmapStep>
<RoadmapStep when="On return" title="Handing back">

Return the car on time, with a full tank if the contract says so, and get a **signed return report**. Keep it until the deposit is released.

</RoadmapStep>
</Roadmap>

## Driving in Morocco: landmarks

| Area | Usual limit |
|---|---|
| Built-up area | **60 km/h** |
| Main route inside a built-up area | **80 km/h** |
| Road outside built-up areas | **100 km/h** |
| Motorway | **120 km/h** |

- Fixed and mobile speed cameras, **frequent checks** at city entrances.
- Speeding fines, according to the scales reported, are around **700 MAD** (10 to 20 km/h over), **1,500 MAD** (20 to 40) and **2,000 MAD plus licence withdrawal** beyond; not wearing a seat belt and phone use at the wheel are also penalised. These amounts can change.
- **Tolls** on motorways: see [motorway from Tanger Med](/en/tanger-med-motorway-tolls-route).
- Watch for **pedestrians, two-wheelers and animals** in rural areas, and avoid driving at night in the mountains if you can.

## Common pitfalls

- **A very low price** advertised online but a high deposit and excess on site.
- **"House" repairs** billed on return with no departure report.
- **Fuel:** check diesel or petrol before filling up.
- **Forbidden roads:** some tracks are excluded from the contract.
