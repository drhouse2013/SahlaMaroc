Morocco is about three hours by plane from most Western European countries. Since September 2026 its legal time is **GMT all year**: 1 hour behind Paris in winter, 2 hours in summer ([time zone](/en/morocco-time-zone-gmt)). That is comfortable for video calls with Europe, and the country offers cities of very different moods. The "best" choice depends less on a ranking than on what you seek: network, calm, waves, budget, community, culture. Here is a method, then an honest portrait of each city.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick picks">
- **All-rounder:** Marrakech.
- **Urban routine and connection:** Casablanca.
- **Calm and greenery:** Rabat.
- **Gateway to Europe:** Tangier.
- **Surf and community:** Taghazout.
- **Focus:** Essaouira.
- **Culture and slow pace:** Fes.
</Callout>

## How to choose: five criteria

1. **Connection.** Fibre where eligible, 4G/5G backup everywhere: see [internet and coworking](/en/fibre-internet-coworking-morocco).
2. **Climate in the season you are there.** Inland heat in summer, cold and damp in some riads in winter.
3. **Neighbourhood rather than city.** The neighbourhood decides walking, noise and Wi-Fi.
4. **Social rhythm.** A nomad community, or a more local town?
5. **Stay status.** No nomad visa appears in the sources read; check the permitted length in the [visa guide](/en/morocco-visa-90-day-rule) and, for a longer stay, the [residence permit](/en/morocco-residence-permit-guide).

## Marrakech: the hub

The most versatile city for a first stay: flights, cafés, coworking spaces, restaurants. Live in **Gueliz** for routine, in the medina for a few nights. Limits: very strong heat from June to September, tourist approaches, medina noise. Details in [where to stay in Marrakech](/en/where-to-stay-in-marrakech) and on the [city page](/en/cities/marrakech).

## Casablanca: the urban routine

Economic capital, less "postcard", but very practical: offices, shopping centres, tram, major stations. It suits you if you want city life rather than a holiday mood. Distances are long: pick a neighbourhood near your habits. See [where to stay in Casablanca](/en/where-to-stay-in-casablanca).

## Rabat: calm and organised

A green capital, with tram, a peaceful medina and an urban beach. Quieter than Marrakech, with neighbourhood life in Agdal. Ideal for a long stay as a couple or family. See [where to stay in Rabat](/en/where-to-stay-in-rabat).

## Tangier: the gateway to Europe

Port, ferry to Spain, Al Boraq high-speed train to Rabat and Casablanca: the best choice if you travel back and forth to Europe often. The climate is cooler and windier than inland. See [where to stay in Tangier](/en/where-to-stay-in-tangier) and the [ferry guide](/en/ferry-to-morocco-guide).

## Taghazout and Agadir: surf and sun

Taghazout, a surf village about 20 km north of Agadir, draws colivings and surfers. Winter is mild, the pace relaxed; but connection and services are less dense than in a big city. Agadir is the comfortable fallback. See [where to stay in Taghazout](/en/where-to-stay-in-taghazout) and [where to stay in Agadir](/en/where-to-stay-in-agadir).

## Essaouira: windy and focused

A small, artistic, windy town, good for concentrating; social life is smaller, especially in winter. The wind can tire some, but enchants kitesurfers. See [where to stay in Essaouira](/en/where-to-stay-in-essaouira).

## Fes: culture and slow pace

A huge medina and a practical Ville Nouvelle. Better suited to a cultural month than to networking. For calm and connection, choose a home in the Ville Nouvelle. See [where to stay in Fes](/en/where-to-stay-in-fes).

## Also worth knowing

Chefchaouen suits a few weeks of hiking and focus, with a connection to check: [where to stay in Chefchaouen](/en/where-to-stay-in-chefchaouen). Meknes, calmer and smaller, suits a slow stay: [where to stay in Meknes](/en/where-to-stay-in-meknes).

## Practical checklist

1. **Internet:** speed test requested from the host, local backup SIM ([SIM and eSIM](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco)).
2. **Visa:** length checked for your nationality ([visa](/en/morocco-visa-90-day-rule)).
3. **Health insurance:** covering long stays ([health for travellers](/en/health-morocco-travellers-insurance-emergencies)).
4. **Budget:** estimated with the [cost-of-living-by-city calculator](/en/cost-of-living-by-city-morocco-calculator).
5. **Money:** see [cash and ATMs](/en/money-cash-atms-morocco).
