Morocco is about a 3-hour flight from most of Western Europe, keeps hours close to European business days (Morocco has been on **GMT all year** since 20 September 2026 — 1 hour behind Paris or Madrid in winter, 2 hours in summer), and has cities that suit very different working styles. Here's how they compare.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick picks">
- **Best all-rounder:** Marrakech
- **Most reliable internet & city life:** Casablanca
- **Calm and liveable:** Rabat
- **Surf and community:** Taghazout
- **Cheapest with character:** Fes
</Callout>

## Marrakech

The busiest nomad hub, with the most coworkings, events and flights. Live in **Gueliz** for fibre and cafés, and visit the medina for the magic. Downsides: it's very hot from June to September, touristy, and more expensive than other cities. See [where to stay in Marrakech](/en/where-to-stay-in-marrakech).

## Casablanca

Morocco's economic capital is less pretty but very practical: the best fibre coverage, international offices, malls, the ocean corniche and the main international airport. It's ideal if you want a city routine rather than a holiday feeling.

## Rabat

The capital is green, clean and walkable, with a tram, a beach and a pleasant old medina. It's quieter than Marrakech and 1 h 20 from Tangier on the high-speed train. A good fit for couples and families.

## Taghazout

This surf village 20 minutes north of Agadir has become a coliving hotspot: sunny winters, surf lessons, yoga and an easy-going community. Choose a coliving with a backup internet connection for calls.

## Essaouira

Windy, artsy and slow. It's great for focused work and kitesurfing, with lower costs than Marrakech. The social scene is smaller, especially in winter.

## Tangier

A port city with Mediterranean and Atlantic beaches, a ferry to Spain and the **Al Boraq** high-speed train (2 h 10 to Casablanca). The perfect base if you travel back and forth to Europe.

## Fes

The world's largest car-free medina, deep history and low prices. Better for a cultural month than for networking. Pick an apartment in the **Ville Nouvelle** for a quieter base.

## Chefchaouen

The blue town in the Rif mountains is beautiful and calm, ideal for a few weeks of hiking and focus. Internet can be less reliable, so bring a local SIM as a backup.
