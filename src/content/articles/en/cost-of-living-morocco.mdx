Morocco is affordable by European standards, but costs vary a lot by city and lifestyle. Use the calculator below for your own estimate, then read the details.

<BudgetCalculator />

## Typical monthly budget (one person)

| Lifestyle | Total per month | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | ~8,000–10,000 MAD | Simple studio, local food, public transport |
| Comfortable | ~13,000–18,000 MAD | 1-bed flat in a good area, mix of restaurants, taxis, coworking |
| Premium | ~25,000 MAD and up | Modern flat or villa, restaurants, car, travel |

These ranges come from the same field-based estimates as the calculator. Casablanca and Marrakech are the most expensive cities; Fes and Essaouira are the cheapest.

## Rent

Rent is your biggest expense. Furnished apartments in sought-after areas (Gueliz, Maarif, Agdal…) cost much more than unfurnished flats in residential neighbourhoods. For stays under 3 months, short-term platforms are simplest; for longer stays, local listings and agencies are cheaper. See [where to stay in Marrakech](/en/where-to-stay-in-marrakech).

## Food

Markets and local eateries are very cheap: fresh produce, bread and mint tea cost little. Imported products and international restaurants are priced close to European levels.

## Transport

Petit taxis and trams are cheap, and intercity trains are good value. See our guides to [taxis and apps](/en/taxi-apps-morocco) and [trains](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).

## Connectivity

A local prepaid SIM costs about **100–130 MAD per month** for 10–20 GB, plus 20–30 MAD for the SIM card. Home fibre is available in big cities. See our [SIM guide](/en/best-sim-card-esim-morocco).

## Money

To avoid losing 3–5 % on every payment and withdrawal, use a card with low foreign fees.
