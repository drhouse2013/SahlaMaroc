Ouarzazate is the big stopover between the High Atlas and the south: people sleep here to visit kasbahs and ksour, or simply to break the road to the desert. The real question is not "which hotel?" but **where to base yourself**: in town, near a kasbah, or out in the countryside.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
- **A stopover night or a practical stay:** in **town**, near the Taourirt Kasbah, with restaurants and taxis within reach.
- **Atmosphere and quiet:** a **guesthouse or kasbah-style hotel** away from the centre, with a car or a transfer.
- **Early-morning flight:** stay **in town** rather than far out in the palm grove.
- **Winter:** check the **heating**: nights are cold.
</Callout>

## What to know about the town

Ouarzazate sits at about **1,150 m** above sea level, where the Dadès and Imini wadis meet to form the Drâa ([Wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouarzazate)). The climate is desert with continental influence: midsummer daytime highs average around **37 °C**, and winter nights can fall **below 0 °C** between December and February. In practice, air-conditioning matters in summer and heating in winter.

Two landmarks shape most visits: the **Taourirt Kasbah**, next to the medina, and the **film studios** (Atlas Studios, created in 1983). The **ksar of Aït Ben Haddou**, in Ouarzazate province, has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since **1987** ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/444/)).

## Where to sleep

### In town: the simplest

The best choice for a stopover: hotels, restaurants and taxis are close together, and early departures (flight, bus, tour) are easy. The trade-off is that the centre is functional rather than an old medina.

### Around the Taourirt Kasbah: the most "heritage"

Sleeping near the kasbah lets you visit on foot and enjoy the neighbourhood. Check the exact address and parking before booking.

### Guesthouse or kasbah-style hotel on the outskirts: the quietest

The south has guesthouses and kasbah-style hotels with pools and gardens. They are pleasant after a long drive, but remote: **without a car, plan a transfer or a taxi** for dinner and excursions. Ask the price and timing of the transfer before you book.

### Near Aït Ben Haddou: the most atmospheric

Staying near the ksar gives quiet access late in the day, once the tour groups have left. Choice is more limited and the distance from town means planning meals and the return. Check the distance on a map and how you will get there before booking.

## Getting there: air, road, bus

- **By air:** Ouarzazate has an **international airport**. Routes (Casablanca and, in some periods, certain European cities) change by season: check [ONDA](https://www.onda.ma), whose [airport network](https://www.onda.ma/Je-suis-Passager/Guide-du-voyageur/R%C3%A9seau-a%C3%A9roportuaire) includes Ouarzazate (OZZ), or the airline. For airport transfers, see our [airports guide](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre).
- **By road from Marrakech:** over the **Tizi n'Tichka pass**, the [ONMT](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/ouarzazate-zagora-tinghir-hollywood-africa) gives 194 km and 3 h 45 by road from Marrakech; Wikipedia gives about **3 h 30 by taxi and 4 h by bus**; actual time depends on traffic and weather. The pass reaches 2,260 m and snow can complicate winter crossings (see [Marrakech to Merzouga](/en/marrakech-to-merzouga-desert-tour)).
- **By bus:** see the [CTM and Supratours guide](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco).
- **By car:** handy for the valley, but avoid driving at night in rural areas (advice from the French foreign ministry, [France Diplomatie](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/conseils-aux-voyageurs/conseils-par-pays-destination/maroc/)). See the [car rental guide](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide).

## Precautions

- **Season:** spring and autumn are generally the most comfortable; summer is hot and winter nights are cold.
- **Street offers:** touts near tourist sites exist everywhere; confirm price and programme before leaving. See [scams and safety](/en/morocco-scams-safety-travellers).
- **Kasbah hotel:** ask what is included (breakfast, dinner, transfer), because the nearest restaurant may be far.
