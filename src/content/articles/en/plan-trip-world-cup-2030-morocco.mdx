Planning a trip to the 2030 World Cup in Morocco is mostly an exercise in patience: the essentials (exact dates, match allocation, ticketing) are not yet published. This guide, **valid as of 9 October 2026**, says what is actually known, what is not, and how to prepare without being caught out.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick answer">
The match schedule in Morocco is **not published** and the final's venue is undecided. Tickets are sold only through FIFA's official channels; as far as we could find, no on-sale date has been announced. Meanwhile: valid passport, travel insurance, a flexible budget and cancellable bookings.
</Callout>

## Legend

**(confirmed)**: established fact. **(announced)**: stated, not finalised. **(provisional)**: to be reconfirmed. **(hypothesis)**: nothing official.

## Schedule: what is known and what is not

- **Confirmed:** Morocco, Spain and Portugal host the 2030 edition, with three centenary matches in Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay. The opening match is due to be played at the Centenario stadium in Montevideo (source: [FIFA, 11 December 2024](https://inside.fifa.com/news/world-cup-2030-morocco-spain-portugal-argentina-paraguay-uruguay-reactions)).
- **Not published, as far as we could find:** the exact tournament dates, the final list of stadiums, the split of matches between countries and cities, team schedules. If you read a detailed "2030 schedule" elsewhere, treat it as a hypothesis.
- **Under discussion:** the format. The tournament is planned with 48 teams; a move to 64 was still under study according to the press in August 2026 (provisional).
- **Final:** not assigned. The FRMF president said on 10 September 2026 it would be played in Benslimane; FIFA replied that a decision would be taken "in due course". See [the stadium status guide](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-stadiums-status).

## Ticketing: official channels only

- FIFA is the only reliable source for tickets: its official website fifa.com and the ticketing portal it designates. **No on-sale date has been announced** as far as we could find.
- On 1 May 2026, FIFA's secretary general said the ticketing policy would be reviewed after criticism of 2026 prices (announced, no detail).
- **Beware of resellers**: unofficial sites, social networks, messaging apps, "packages" sold before any official opening. Tickets may be fake, cancelled or non-transferable. Never pay a stranger by bank transfer and do not book flights on the strength of a ticket you did not receive through an official channel.
- We offer no affiliate link for match tickets.

## Entry documents

- Visa rules depend on your nationality. See the [90-day rule guide](/en/morocco-visa-90-day-rule) and the [e-visa guide](/en/morocco-e-visa-guide).
- No special fan entry scheme has been announced for 2030 as far as we could find. For the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations, a free e-visa and a digital "Fan ID" via the YALLA app were announced for supporters (November 2025): a precedent, not a promise for 2030 (hypothesis).
- If you combine Morocco, Spain and Portugal, check each country's rules on its official websites.

## Travelling between host cities

The six proposed cities are Casablanca (stadium in Benslimane), Rabat, Tangier, Marrakech, Fes and Agadir.

- **Tangier, Rabat, Casablanca:** linked by the Al Boraq high-speed train (confirmed). See the [train guide](/en/trains-in-morocco-al-boraq).
- **Marrakech:** the Kenitra-Marrakech high-speed line is announced for late 2029. Until then, use the conventional network, buses or flights. Details in the [transport guide](/en/world-cup-2030-morocco-transport).
- **Fes and Agadir:** check ONCF, [CTM and Supratours buses](/en/ctm-supratours-bus-morocco) and flights ([airports and transfers](/en/morocco-airports-to-city-centre)).
- We give **no unsourced distances or journey times** here: check them on carriers' websites for your travel date.
- **Car rental:** [rental guide](/en/car-rental-morocco-guide); roads around stadiums will probably be busy on match days (hypothesis).
